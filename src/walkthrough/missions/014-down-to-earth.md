It starts at Brian's marina in Key Lento and is mostly flying, with one fight in the middle.

## Wash the boat

Brian puts Jason to work first. Clean the boat at the marina with the pressure washer until the job is done.

## Fly to Bobcat Bay

Board the seaplane and fly to Bobcat Bay to collect the package.

## The shootout

Things turn violent at the pickup. How you handle it feeds Jason's criminal profile: leaving people alive and killing them push it in different directions. The mission itself doesn't mind which.

## Make the drops

On the way back there are three drop points for the package. Fly to each marker and let the drop happen.

## Hide it

Back at the house, hide the leftover package before the visitor arrives. Follow the marker to the hiding spot.

> **Spoiler:** The visitor is Lucia's parole officer.

## Meditate with Lucia

Once the mission ends, Lucia introduces meditation at the house. This is the first meditation in the game.

> **Note:** Meditation counts toward the [Positive Mental Attitude](/trophies/#positive-mental-attitude) trophy. See the trophy page for an open question about this first one.
