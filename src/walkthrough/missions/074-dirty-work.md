A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job for both [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/). It opens once you've finished both [Too Much Baggage](/walkthrough/too-much-baggage/) and [Cash Injections](/walkthrough/cash-injections/). Go to the **planning room** with either of them and Andrea gets things going. There's no fighting in this one.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

> **Spoiler:** Lucia has won over Cisco and Nate, but Raina still doesn't trust her. Andrea's idea is to have Jason book Raina as a dominatrix while Lucia gets photos, so Raina loses face with Kordell's crew.

## Drive to the Velvet Palm Motel

Get in a car and head for the **Velvet Palm Motel** in **Tequesta**.

## Pick Lucia's vantage point

Playing as Lucia, choose where to shoot from. Go with the **containers** Jason points out: they give you a clear line into the motel windows.

## Keep Jason in the game

The view moves to Jason inside the room. Raina tries to take control, and each time a prompt comes up, press **Square / X** to push back. Keep it up until Jason gets the **window** open.

## Take the photos

Back as Lucia, aim the camera at the window where Jason is and take your photos.

> **Note:** The next objective reads as if you can keep shooting or leave. You don't have to choose: Lucia packs up on her own after a few pictures.

## Regroup in the alley

Switch to Jason and drive round the motel to meet Lucia in the **alley**.

## Talk to Nate at the pool

The story jumps to the next day. As Lucia, at the gym, go **upstairs and outside to the pool**. Nate is lying in one of the chairs. Talk to him to finish the mission.
