Opens after [Dealer's Choice](/walkthrough/#chapter-3). It starts from a bag in the closet of the new apartment in Bayside, and it comes in three parts spread over the chapter. Part 1 moves the story on; Parts 2 and 3 are optional but pay well.

> **Watch out:** If you want the whole job, say yes to Halifax in Part 1 and trust him in Part 2. Saying no to either one closes off what comes after.

## Part 1: take the statue to Capri

Interact with the bag in the closet of the Bayside apartment. Then head for Capri on the far west side of the map. You can drive, or call a RideMe taxi to save the trip.

> **Tip:** An optional objective asks you to explore Capri together. It goes away as soon as you walk into a shop or leave town, and you don't need to do anything for it.

## Part 1: the antique store

Try the front door of the store and the game takes you upstairs. The owner, Evelyn, won't value the statue, but a man called Halifax offers to do it for you.

Hand it over and he leaves you his Crowex watch as a deposit. You can wear it: open a wardrobe and look under Accessories, then Watches.

> **Missable:** Turned him down? You can still phone Halifax later and give him the statue. That optional call vanishes when Chapter 3 ends, and Parts 2 and 3 go with it.

## Part 2: break into the store

After a while Halifax goes quiet, and the next part starts back at the store. Wait for Evelyn to leave. You can force the door, or take the quiet way in below.

## Part 2: in through the window

Go round the back of the building, climb the stairs and drop onto the lower roof. The window above the air conditioning unit is open. Inside, walk to the door and it opens for Jason.

## Part 2: the drawer and the motel

Search the desk drawer in the north corner of the room. It leads you to the motel next door. Ask one of the people staying there, who points you to an upstairs room on the west side, then knock on that door.

## Part 2: trust him or not

After the scene you decide whether to trust Halifax. Only trusting him leads to Part 3.

## Part 3: Evelyn's tip

Some time later Evelyn gets in touch. Go back to the store to talk to her, and she gives you an address in the northwest part of Capri.

## Part 3: the chase on the water

When you get there, Halifax runs for a boat. Jason takes a Seashark after him. Get alongside and hold the prompt to jump onto his boat. He wrecks it and runs, so swim to shore and keep after him.

## Part 3: golf cart and bike

He steals a golf cart. Grab the motorbike in the yard and stay on him.

## Part 3: Lucia ends it

Halifax hits Jason with pepper spray, and the game switches to Lucia as he runs into her car. Get out, use focus mode (L3 and R3 together) and shoot him in the leg. Walk up to him, then call Jason on Lucia's phone.

## Part 3: the storage container

Search the storage container for about $2,500 in cash and jewellery. A '32 Hustler is parked in there too: keep it or sell it. You also get to decide whether to shoot Halifax or let him go.

> **Note:** Neither guide we checked reports any later effect from shooting or sparing him.
