After the pool scene that follows [Another Life](/walkthrough/another-life/), Valentina calls: she's found Jason and Lucia a lawyer. You can play this before or after [Company Cars](/walkthrough/company-cars/).

## Go to Abe's office

Drive to the marked office in Downtown Vice City and talk to the receptionist inside.

## Go to Sandy

Walk through to the back of the office and talk to Sandy, Abe's assistant.

## Go to Abe

Head upstairs to Abe Gonzalez's office. The mission ends after the meeting.

> **Spoiler:** Abe will take on their legal trouble, but he wants a favour in return. With Jason out of the room, he tells Lucia her case would be far easier if she gave Jason up. She turns him down.

Next is [Party Hard](/walkthrough/party-hard/), a security job for Valentina.
