Ocean Beach starts straight after [The Cartel Heist](/walkthrough/the-cartel-heist/), or the next time you go back to the Bayside apartment. [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) clear out of [Boobie's](/characters/boobie-ike/) place for good and move somewhere a lot nicer.

## Drive to the Y Hotel

Once the apartment is packed up, follow the marker across to the Y Hotel on the beach side of the city.

## Hand over the car

Pull up at the entrance. The hotel concierge takes the car off your hands, so there's no parking to worry about. Walk inside.

## Meet Valentina

Valentina is waiting on the right-hand side of the lobby. Talk to her, then follow her to the lifts.

> **Spoiler:** The suite is Valentina's doing. It's part of how she washes money, and she introduces Santino, the concierge.

## Find room 701

Ride the lift to the 7th floor. Turn left out of the lift and walk to the very end of the hallway: room 701 is the last door.

## Look round the suite

Follow Lucia from room to room as she opens them up. When she gets to the bedroom, interact with the **wardrobe**. It holds new outfits, and it's where you change clothes from here on.

## Champagne on the balcony

Follow Lucia out onto the balcony. When she asks about champagne, go back inside, pick up the two glasses on the counter and take one out to her. The mission ends there.

> **Spoiler:** Valentina promises to set up a meeting with a Vice City lawyer, Abraham Gonzalez, who could give the two of them a clean start.

Next is [Another Life](/walkthrough/another-life/), which starts as soon as this one ends.
