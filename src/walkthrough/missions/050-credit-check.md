The Chapter 3 mission where [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) meet the rest of [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/)'s crew. It's a warm-up: a run of small Fleeca branches before the big bank job Raul has in mind. It follows [Crosstown Traffic](/walkthrough/crosstown-traffic/).

## Meet the crew

Go to Raul's place in Stockyard, marked on the map. Inside, Raul introduces the people you'll be working with from here on: **Andrea**, who handles the cons and the disguises, **Karim** on tech, and **Felipe**, the driver.

## Pick your masks

Andrea has masks laid out. Pick a pair for Jason and Lucia. It's a cosmetic choice and doesn't change the job.

## Gear from Karim

Karim hands over the **Dye Pack Jammer**, which stops the dye packs hidden in the cash from going off, along with some sticky bombs. Then head out to the car.

> **Note:** You keep the Dye Pack Jammer after the mission. Without it, marked bills from a bank are worth nothing to a fence.

## The first bank

Drive to the first Fleeca branch. Raul's rules are simple: no shooting, and be gone before the police turn up. Masks on, go in, and use the threaten prompt on the guard, the staff and the customers to keep everyone down. Then empty the cash drawers and leave.

> **Watch out:** Firing your gun goes against Raul's plan. Threats are enough to keep the room under control.

## The next banks

Raul sends you on to the next branch, and then another. It's the same routine each time: get in, keep everyone down with threats, grab the cash and go.

## Lose the cops

After the last bank the police are after you. Break line of sight and get away until the wanted level clears.

## Back to the apartment

With the heat gone, drive back to the apartment. When someone knocks, answer the door. The mission ends there.

> **Spoiler:** The cash doesn't stay in the apartment for long. The knock at the door is someone come to collect the bags.

Once it's over, the small bank jobs and the payday loan robberies show up on your map. See the [money guide](/guides/money/#bank-jobs) for how they work. Next in the story: [Steal the Zorrusso for Randy](/walkthrough/steal-the-zorrusso-for-randy/).
