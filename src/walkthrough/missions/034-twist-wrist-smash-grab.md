A side job. A few in-game hours after [Thrillbillies](/walkthrough/thrillbillies/), Billy sends you a message and the job shows up on the map in Port Gellhorn. Jason or Lucia can take it.

## Meet Billy

Go to the marker and sit on the bench. Billy turns up with a Highwayman Sanchez for you and explains the plan: they are hitting an armoured van.

## Ride with the crew

Get on the bike and follow Billy until a cutscene shows the van.

## Chase the van

The rest of the crew joins in. Stay with the van until it crashes into a tree on its own. Don't shoot it; just ride, and don't come off the bike.

## Blow the doors

Walk to the back of the van and press Triangle/Y to plant the charge, then use the prompt that appears to set it off.

## The guard

The security guard steps off to the side. Knock him out or leave him alone; either works. Grab the bag from behind the van and get back on your bike.

## Head for the hills

Stay close to Billy as he leads the way up a mountain to meet the rest of the crew. A cutscene ends the job.

> **Tip:** The Sanchez is yours to keep. Park it in a garage (there is one in Port Gellhorn) and register it with [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/) so you have it for stunt jumps later.
