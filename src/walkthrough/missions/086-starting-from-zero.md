Chapter 4 ends with very little shooting. It picks up straight after [Sunset](/walkthrough/sunset/), with [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) at a junkyard in **Southside**, the police out in force and nobody left they can call on except Andrea.

> **Spoiler:** Their accounts are frozen and the whole city is looking for them. On the way, the two of them talk about whether it's time to go their separate ways.

## Get to the Aqua Stadium

Leave the junkyard and take the car parked nearby to the **Aqua Stadium**. Drive like you have nowhere to be: police are searching the streets.

> **Tip:** If they spot you, Andrea doesn't hang around. She leaves, and leaves the goods behind for you to collect. A shop on the way lets you change clothes so you stand out less, but it costs money and you can do without it.

## Find Andrea

Run up into the stands. She's sitting towards the **south-west end** of the stadium.

## Swap cars and go to the storage unit

Police pull up at the front of the stadium, so your car is no good now. Head **south** to the boat storage area and steal the car parked in front of the building there. Drive carefully to **Big House Storage Inc** in **Crosstown** and walk into the building; the game takes it from there.

> **Spoiler:** The storage unit holds the hard drive and the statue, both of which you leave with.

## Answer the message

A text from **Mattia** points you at a hotel. As Lucia, go in and take the elevator. Upstairs a hostess stops you and walks you to a table; follow her.

Then switch to Jason, go inside and take the elevator up to the **terrace**.

> **Spoiler:** The man waiting upstairs is Giorgio Gallo, the shipping magnate the bag belonged to from the start, and Jason and Lucia are going to be his guests whether they like it or not. He hands you a gun and points you at Mattia. Shoot or let the time run out, it makes no difference: the gun isn't loaded and Mattia walks away fine.

## Follow your hosts

Follow Giorgio as he shows you around the penthouse, then follow Dardan to the bedroom. Jason and Lucia turn in, the chapter ends and **Top Of The World, Ma** unlocks. [Bird in the Hand](/walkthrough/bird-in-the-hand/) starts in the morning.
