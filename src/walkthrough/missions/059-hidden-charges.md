The last bit of preparation for **The Cartel Heist**. Karim's plan is to knock out the phone towers around the bank, so the city has something else to deal with and the bank can't call for help. Either [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) or [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) can start it from the planning room in Stockyard, once [Company Property](/walkthrough/company-property/) and [Cooties](/walkthrough/cooties/) are done.

> **Spoiler:** Before you head out, Jason and Lucia tell Raul about Tomas and the hard drive. Raul promises to help with it once the bank is done.

## Load up the van

Talk to Karim and Raul outside the planning room, then get into the van they've been loading.

## Park in the garage

Drive downtown to the marker. It's a garage off an alley: drive inside and stop.

## Count your charges

You have up to three charges, one for each tower. Every crate you lost off the back of Brian's truck in [Company Property](/walkthrough/company-property/#step-7) is one fewer charge here. Each charge you plant adds time to the heist, so plant every one you have.

> **Tip:** Plant with both characters. You can switch to whoever is closer to the next tower instead of walking back to the van, and the teamwork gives Jason and Lucia's relationship a boost.

## The East tower

Start with this one: it sits right above the van. Walk round to the front of the western building, the one with the helipad on the map, and take the lift inside up to the roof. Climb the stairs to the helipad, cross the walkway onto the next roof, then climb up to the tower and plant the charge.

## The South tower

Switch to the other character, who is already close to the South tower. Walk past the pool to the lift, ride it to the roof, climb the ladder and plant the charge.

## The West tower

If you have a third charge, pick who plants it. Switching back puts you at the van, which saves a little time. Grab the bag and head for the construction building on the north side, going in through its door.

> **Watch out:** The workers spot you as soon as you walk in and report you for trespassing. Don't hang around.

The lift is on the left inside. Up on the roof, go round the far side of the grey tower, climb the ladder and plant the charge on the box.

## Get away

Leave as soon as the last charge is in. If you don't have a parachute, take one from the box on the rooftop. Jump, glide clear, and stay out of the police search area. The mission ends once they stop looking for you.
