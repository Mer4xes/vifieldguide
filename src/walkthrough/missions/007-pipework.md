A short rent run for Brian. Nothing here affects anything later.

## Talk to Mandy

Brian sends you to Mandy's house in Key Lento. Talk to her and follow her inside. She won't hand over any money until her sink is fixed. You can hear her out or cut to the plunger question; it ends the same way.

## Find the plunger

The plunger is on the bathroom floor, across from the bedroom.

## Fix the sink

At the kitchen sink, press R3 to use the plunger. A short repair follows under the sink: use the wrench on the pipe with R2/RT.

> **Spoiler:** Mandy flirts with Jason. He turns her down, and she doesn't take it well.

## Leave

Wrap things up with Mandy and head out.

> **Tip:** As you leave, Mandy throws a cigar box into the street. Pick it up to get the **Macizo Cigar**. Grab it before you drive off.

Brian calls a few minutes later with the next job.
