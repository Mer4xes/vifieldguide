Once [Lawyer Up](/walkthrough/lawyer-up/) is done, Valentina gets in touch with a personal security job. Meet her at VCIA, the airport. You play as both [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) and the game switches between them for you.

## Drive Andrés out of the airport

You're driving Andrés. Follow his lead car out of VCIA.

## Pick up Andrés' order

He wants a detour: stop at the marked spot in Little Cuba to collect his drinks order.

## Drive to the Megamundo offices

Carry on to the Megamundo building in Downtown Vice City.

## Follow Andrés inside

Follow him in. After the scene in the lobby, follow Petra and the rest of the group into the lift.

## Check on Andrés

Upstairs, go to the marked conference room.

> **Spoiler:** Armed men storm the building and drag Andrés away.

One gunman is still in the room. Hit him to knock the gun out of his hands, then knock him out.

## Clear the floor and grab the radio

Fight your way along the hallway. When the floor is clear, pick up the radio from the marked body.

## Lucia: get to the stairwell

You switch to Lucia. Crouch and keep to the left side of the room to reach the stairwell.

> **Watch out:** A man jumps you in the stairwell. Mash Square / X to fight him off, then again to kick his gun away. On the floor, hold R2 / RT and keep mashing, and press Triangle / Y when the prompt shows to hit him with the shoe lying next to you.

## Jason: chase after Andrés

Back with Jason, keep fighting while you follow the floor round to the left. Gunmen keep coming out of the offices, so check each door. Take the stairs up once it's clear.

## Lucia: through the vent

As Lucia, shoot the man in the lift. Climb the stairs to the hole in the wall, interact to get into the vent and crawl along until you drop down onto the party floor.

## Clear the party floor

Take out every gunman on the floor.

> **Watch out:** Lucia only has a **silenced pistol with no spare ammo**. Make every shot count. Being seen, or firing anything loud, sends all of them looking for you.

> **Tip:** Use focus (L3 + R3 while not aiming) to see where the gunmen are before you move.

There are hostages on the lower floor, so be careful where you shoot.

## Free Valentina and find the Navarros

Go down to the lower floor and untie Valentina, then check the marked door. The Navarros are hiding behind it.

> **Spoiler:** César Navarro runs Megamundo, and Petra, his number two, is Andrés' wife.

## Jason: jump on the helicopter

Run up to the roof as Jason and press X / A to leap onto the helicopter as it lifts off. Mash Square / X to fight the kidnappers and press X / A to dodge when the prompt appears. Once they're thrown out, hold R2 / RT and mash X / A to pull Andrés back inside.

## Land on the helipad

You're flying now. Head back to Megamundo and set the helicopter down on the rooftop helipad to finish.
