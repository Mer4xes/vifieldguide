The job starts in Southwind with Jason at the controls of the seaplane again.

## Take off

Get the seaplane into the air from Southwind.

## Protect Pete's plane

Fly with pilot Pete's plane and keep it safe until it is through.

## The deal

Land and go to the meeting. It is a trap: bikers are waiting, and you have to shoot your way out.

> **Spoiler:** The bikers grab Donnie, and Jason has to go in after him.

## Catch up on a bike

Get on a bike and chase down the others to finish. The story carries on straight into **Building a Life** without a break.
