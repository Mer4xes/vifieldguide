This mission teaches how the police recognise you: what you wear and what you drive. Cal also installs a police scanner on your phone, which plays police radio during crimes and chases.

## Ride to Hamlet

Get in Cal's truck and drive with him to the police impound in Hamlet.

## Get inside unseen

Find a way over the fence and into the lot without being spotted. Stay out of the guards' line of sight.

> **Watch out:** Knocking a guard out gets noticed, and the police send a patrol. Sneaking past is cleaner.

## Find the Phoenix

Use the photo on your phone to pick out the right car: a red Imponte Phoenix hooked to a tow truck at the back of the lot.

## Unhook it

Get in the tow truck and press R3 to release the car, then climb into the Phoenix.

> **Tip:** If a guard drags you out of a car, mash Square/X to fight free.

## Break out and lose the police

Drive out through the gate. This is where the police are alerted. Lose them with tight corners and short cuts off the road, then open it up on a long straight.

> **Tip:** Hold Circle/B while driving to duck down in your seat, which makes Jason harder to recognise.

## Respray and deliver

Take the Phoenix to the Pay 'n' Spray in Hamlet, then deliver it to Brian's garage and pick up the cash.

## Change your look

The icons at the top right show what the police remember about you: clothes, hat, glasses and car. Hold L1/LB to open the item wheel and use R1/RB to take off the hat and glasses, then buy a new outfit at the gun store next door and put it on. When the icons turn from red to white, you are clear.
