A Chapter 4 job for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/): get into a guarded mansion on Starfish Island, get down to its basement and get out again. The way in is quiet work; the way out is a gunfight.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

## Drive to Starfish Island

Meet Dre'Quan when he gets in touch, get in the car and drive to Starfish Island. The break-in waits until it's dark.

## Get over the wall

Dre'Quan leads you to a wall around the property. Climb over it to get into the grounds.

## Take out the guard

There's a guard on his own inside the grounds. Sneak up and knock him out, then pick up the body and put it in the closet so nobody finds it.

## Find the key card

Head upstairs to the master bedroom. The key card is in there, and it's what gets the elevator down to the basement.

## Get past the tiger

A tiger is roaming the house and moves between rooms. If it's in the upstairs hallway, go out onto the balcony and drop down to the level below rather than walk past it.

Downstairs, take the **raw meat** from the fridge and throw it away from where you need to go. Once the tiger is busy with it, follow Dre'Quan to the elevator and use the key card.

## Find the hidden room

> **Spoiler:** The basement looks like a dead end, but the room you're after is behind a large mural. Dre'Quan settles who opens it with a game of rock-paper-scissors.

Look at the shelf of items near the mural: one of them fits the slot in the wall. Pick one up and try it. If nothing happens, put it back and try another until the wall opens.

> **Spoiler:** Inside is a security room. While Dre'Quan copies what you came for, you can look through the videos on the screens.

## Fight your way back up

Grab a gun in the room before you leave, because guards are coming down for you. Clear them out, take the elevator up and fight through the house.

## Escape by boat

Don't head for the front, that's where more guards arrive. Go for the dock at the back of the house, take the boat and keep going until the police lose you. The mission ends once you're clear.
