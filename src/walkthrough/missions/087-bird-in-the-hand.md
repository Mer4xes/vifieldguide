[Chapter 5](/walkthrough/#chapter-5) begins in a penthouse at **Tequesta Retreat** in Vice City, which is home for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) from now until the end. Take a minute to learn the layout. The mission starts by itself the morning after [Starting From Zero](/walkthrough/starting-from-zero/).

> **Spoiler:** Giorgio explains what the hard drive really bought him: leverage over the senator, and with it a cocaine pipeline. The coast guard has seized his latest load, and if he doesn't get it back, the Albanian mob will kill him. He has hired three ex-Navy specialists, Vinny, Kevin and Liz, to help.

## Get changed and meet Giorgio

Use the **wardrobe** in your room to change. Then go downstairs to the lower floor and through the door by the elevators into the **gym**, where Giorgio is waiting.

## Scout the coast guard base

Once everyone is in the helicopter, fly to the marked **coast guard base**. The game hands you Lucia to look the place over. Look at each marked spot in turn:

1. The guards by the **open garage door**.
2. The guards on the right **by the water**, standing around a pallet.
3. The guards on the left **sitting with a dog**.
4. When the helicopter moves to the other side, the **drawbridge** on the right.
5. The **boat in the corner of the dock**, with more packages on pallets.

## Fly to the bridge and meet Kevin

Back to Jason at the controls. Fly over the **bridge to the west**, on the edge of Vice City, then head south to the marker near **Key Lento**. You don't need to fly the whole way: once the crew stop talking, the game moves you on.

## Skydive to the boat

Jump out, skydive, and open your parachute to land on the **boat**. Drive it to the marker **south of the military base**.

## Through the sewer

It's dark by now. Follow Vinny in. Hold **D-pad left** to switch night vision on and off.

At the **grate**, interact with it, then hold **R2 / RT** and sweep the **left stick** across the bars to cut through. Follow Vinny along the sewer until Jason climbs out with him inside the base.

## Cross the base to the hangar

There are soldiers everywhere, but the shadows hide you from most of them. Tag enemies with **D-pad right**, and press **Triangle / Y** to whistle someone over for an easy knockout. The middle of the roads is the quietest route.

Vinny points out a **truck on the left**. Knock out the man crouched at the back, climb in, and it carries you closer to the hangar. When you get out, the hangar is straight ahead: wait for the trucks on the road to go past, then run for it.

## Get to the roof hatch

Climb the ladders next to the hangar to the roof and drop in through the **hatch**.

> **Tip:** Reaching the hatch **without being detected** earns the **Professional** bonus.

## Open the hangar doors

Once the crew are inside, the game switches you to Lucia. Go to the end of the walkway and use the **console** to open the hangar doors.

## Hold off the military

Troops pour in as soon as the doors open. Keep them busy while the helicopter backs out, and kill them quickly so they don't shoot it up. Get in as soon as you're prompted.

## Defend the helicopter

On the way back, enemy helicopters come at you from the sides; shoot the soldiers hanging out of them. When the prompt comes up, switch to Jason, who has a **rocket launcher**, for the fighter jets chasing you. Aim at one, wait for the box on it to turn **red**, then fire: the rocket follows it. Destroy **both jets** to finish the mission.
