A side job, not part of the main story. It appears on the map during [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/), when Wyman mentions the dirt races at the motel cafeteria, along with the other optional jobs that open there. Either Jason or Lucia can start it at the Thrillbilly Mud Club in Kelly County. It is the first of Billy's jobs; see [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#contacts).

## New clothes

At the entrance to the Mud Club, go into the souvenir shop and buy outfits for both Jason and Lucia.

## Meet the crew

Walk out of the shop in the new clothes. A cutscene introduces Billy and his dirt-bike crew: Ricky, Brittney and Wyman.

## Rent a bike

Rent a bike at Roostere's Rentals. The Sanchez costs more than the Manchez but it is faster, and it is the better pick.

## To the start line

Ride to the start of the race, marked on the map, and talk to the organiser.

## Race

You race against 13 others. Winning isn't required: just don't come in last. The higher you finish, the more you are paid.

> **Tip:** Keep away from cars on the course. Hitting one can throw you off the bike.

> **Note:** After the race Billy says he'll be in touch with more work. His next job is [Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab](/walkthrough/twist-wrist-smash-grab/).
