A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job from [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/), for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) or [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/). It's open once [Power Play](/walkthrough/power-play/) is done; Raul usually calls a little after [Close Surveillance](/walkthrough/close-surveillance/) and [Delivery Method](/walkthrough/delivery-method/). Head to the marker at **VCIA**. There's no shooting in this one.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

## Get onto the roof

The catering trucks are parked by a building with workers around it, and anyone who sees you will report you for trespassing. Go round the **right side** of the building, keeping out of sight, and climb up the lower section onto the **roof**.

## Drop down by the trucks

Walk across the roof to the row of catering trucks. Drop down on the **left of the middle truck**; nobody is working on that side.

## Steal the truck

Break into it with the **Slim Jim** straight away, before anyone wanders over.

## Drive it to Rockridge

Take the truck out of the airport and on to the drop-off marked in **Rockridge**.

## Pack the bags

Back in the planning room, Raul talks while you work. Pick up fake cash from the pile and put it in the bags, a few times over, until the mission ends.

> **Spoiler:** While they pack, Raul opens up to Jason about his son, Tomás, and says the boy might be better off without a father like him.
