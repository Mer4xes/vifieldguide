The second step of **The Cartel Heist**. After [Sinfrontera National Bank](/walkthrough/sinfrontera-national-bank/) it starts straight away: Nicole Park, the security expert Raul needs, is being kept at her house under guard, and [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) are going to take her. Either of them can start it.

## Drive past the house

The game moves you to Waning Sands in Leonard County and marks Nicole's house. Drive past it slowly, the way Jason tells you, so you can get a look at the guards.

## Park up

Pull over at the marked spot nearby and get out. Don't drive up to the front door.

## Sneak in

Follow Jason to the house on foot, staying low. Wait at the back until a guard comes out of the back door, then slip in after Jason while the guard is outside keeping watch.

## Find Nicole

Go through the hallway towards the bedrooms. Nicole is behind the closed door of the northeast bedroom.

> **Spoiler:** Nicole doesn't come quietly. After a short scene she's out cold and you have to carry her.

## Carry her out

Pick Nicole up. Go out the back and follow Jason down the side of the house to the front. Stay right on his heels: his route keeps you out of the guards' sight.

> **Tip:** If you lag behind Jason or take your own route, you're far more likely to be spotted.

## Into the trunk

At the car, open the trunk and put Nicole inside. Then get in and drive off quickly to escape the area.

## To the planning room

Drive to Raul's planning room in Stockyard. The mission ends when you get there.

> **Spoiler:** Raul knows something about Nicole that the cartel would kill her for. Staying with them is no longer safe for her, and she agrees to join his crew.
