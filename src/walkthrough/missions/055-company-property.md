Part of the build-up to **The Cartel Heist**. [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) needs explosives for the bank, and [Brian](/characters/brian-heder/) knows an old smuggler who can fly some in. [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) are there to unload, and either of them can start it.

## Meet Brian at Skeeter's

Brian is waiting at Skeeter's, a bar just south of Hamlet. Talk to him, then follow him out to his truck.

## Drive to the airfield

Take Brian's truck south to the airfield and wait for the pilot, Pete, to bring his plane in.

## Load the truck

Pete hands out the weapons crates as soon as he's down. Carry each one to the back of Brian's truck, then pick up the last crate from the ground and load that too.

> **Spoiler:** The handover doesn't stay quiet. Federal agents raid the airfield, so Lucia and Brian get away with the truck while Jason stays behind to get Pete out.

## Fight through the hangar

Pete opens a garage door on the right of a hangar. Go in: there's an assault rifle and health on the wall facing the door, so grab both. Follow Pete into the next room and hold off the agents coming in.

## Stay with Pete

Pete runs out the back. Follow him south along the road and fight as you go. He climbs onto a roof while cars pull up on the road, then cuts through the trees and across the runway to another hangar, with more cars coming from the far side.

## Take off

Inside the hangar you climb into the plane with Pete. Taxi out, turn left onto the runway, go full throttle and pull up before the next cars reach you.

## Protect the cargo

Now you're Lucia, in the back of Brian's truck with a light machine gun and unlimited ammo. Cars try to ram the truck: shoot them before they get close.

> **Watch out:** Every crate that falls off is explosives you won't have in **Hidden Charges** later in the heist. Reload only when nothing is right behind you, and deal with the nearest car first.

> **Tip:** Keep every crate on the truck and you get the **Professional** bonus at the end of this part.

## Head for the planning room

Once the chasers are gone, Lucia takes the wheel. Follow the route towards the planning room until the game switches you back to Jason.

## Crash-land the plane

The plane is too badly damaged for a runway. Bring it down in the fields instead: the colourful field on the right of the river is the easiest place. The mission ends when you're down.
