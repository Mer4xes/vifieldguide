A short one, played as Jason. You met Tyrone in [Dealer's Choice](/walkthrough/dealers-choice/), where he hands over the product for Dre'Quan's deal. Now Jason goes back on his own to ask for more work.

> **Note:** Some guides fold this into the start of Here, Kitty Kitty rather than listing it as a mission of its own.

## Go see Tyrone

Switch to Jason and head to Tyrone's marker in Vice City. Talk to him and ask whether he has another job.

## The Kitty Litter talk

Keep talking until the conversation turns to Kitty Litter.

> **Spoiler:** Kitty Litter is a new synthetic drug spreading through Vice City, and Tyrone wants a piece of that trade. That is the thread his later jobs follow.

When the objective changes to speaking with Tyrone again and finding someone called Charles, you have moved on to [Here, Kitty Kitty](/walkthrough/here-kitty-kitty/).

> **Note:** Tyrone's side jobs about the Kitty Litter labs are on [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#tyrone).
