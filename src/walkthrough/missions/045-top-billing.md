A short Chapter 3 story mission that is mostly about [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/), with a quick switch to [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) at the end. It starts at the Jack of Hearts club, where [Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/) has his act, the [Real Dimez](/characters/real-dimez/), on the bill. No fighting and no choices.

## Backstage at the Jack of Hearts

Head into the club and take the corridor that leads backstage. After the scene, Dre'Quan needs you to find the duo. Ask the staff inside where they went: the answer is out back, not in the club.

> **Spoiler:** By the time you go looking, the Dimez have already left the building.

## Out to the parking lot

Go out to the lot behind the club, where Roxy and Bae-Luxe are. The conversation ends with Roxy driving off.

## Follow Roxy

Take Bae-Luxe's car and follow Roxy. Keep her in view, but don't stress: at the railway crossing she gets away no matter what you do, and that's scripted.

> **Watch out:** Losing her at the tracks is not a fail. Don't restart the checkpoint; the next objective appears on its own.

## Food at Peewees

The new marker is Peewees. Park, go in and order at the counter. While you eat, the group works out where Roxy has gone: a house party.

## Find Roxy at the party

Drive to the party and go inside as Lucia. Work your way through the house to Roxy. She won't leave with you, so the plan changes.

## Cut the power

Switch to Jason. Out on the sidewalk in front of the house is a green electrical box. Open it and pull the switch inside. With the music dead, the party breaks up.

> **Tip:** If you can't find the switch, you're looking in the wrong place. It's in the box on the street, not anywhere in the house.

## Roxy comes out

Switch back to Lucia and walk Roxy out of the house. A short scene ends the mission.

> **Note:** Next on our list is [Boobie Time](/walkthrough/boobie-time/), Boobie's driving job for Jason.
