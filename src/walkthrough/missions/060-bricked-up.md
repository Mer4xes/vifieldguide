[Boobie](/characters/boobie-ike/) calls once [Hidden Charges](/walkthrough/hidden-charges/) and the other remaining Chapter 3 jobs are done. Go back to the apartment in Bayside, where [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) find him with a job for them: collect the bricks a gang called San4San owes him in Rockridge.

> **Spoiler:** Boobie is blunt about it. As long as they're living in his place, they work for him.

## Drive to Rockridge

Head for Venture Apartments in Rockridge.

## Park outside the red zone

The block has a red zone round it on the map. Leave your car outside it: anything parked inside gets impounded during the mission. Walk the rest of the way to the front door.

## Ask for Raymond

In the lobby, turn right and talk to the man standing by the lift. Upstairs, walk down the hallway and knock on the red door with the slot in the middle.

## Grab the bricks

> **Spoiler:** The handover never happens. Captain Méndez and his dirty cops raid the building.

You only have pistols at first, so pick up the longer guns the police drop. Before you leave the first room, take the **red bag** near the end of the table. Those are Boobie's bricks.

> **Missable:** The bag is the whole payout. Without it there's no $3,500 and no Professional bonus.

## Fight back to the lifts

Work your way back along the hallway towards the lifts. Police come out of the side doors and from the bigger room just before the lifts, so check each doorway as you pass. Take the stairs at the end of the hallway or the lift. If the lift stops between floors, tap X / A to force the doors open.

## Through the dark floor

The lights are out on the third floor and police wait behind corners and cover. A police gun with a light on it, picked up off the floor, makes this part much easier.

## Out through the window

Keep going down the stairs to the second floor. Raymond is at the end of the hallway and points you into the apartment on his left. Lucia climbs out of the window: interact with her to help her down.

## Pick up Jason

You're Lucia now. Run to the parking lot on the south side, take a car and drive back under the window to pick Jason up.

## Lose the cops

A police helicopter is on you from the start, so this takes longer than usual. Put distance between you and the building and watch for roadblocks.

## Meet Boobie at the Jack of Hearts

Drive to the Jack of Hearts, where Boobie is waiting in his car out front. Stay out of the police search area on the way. Swapping cars is fine.

> **Spoiler:** After this, Jason and Lucia agree it's time to get out of Boobie's apartment.
