## Out the gate

The game opens on Lucia leaving Leonida State Correctional. A short cutscene hands you Jason, leaning on a weathered sedan across the lot. When control passes to you, you are on foot. Walk Lucia to the car. This is the movement tutorial, nothing more. Get in.

## Ride into Vice City

Jason tells you to drive. Follow the GPS line to the Sun Glow Motel on the edge of the city. The opening conversation between the two plays out over the drive, and the route eases you into steering, braking and the minimap.

> **Tip:** Cruise, don't race. Clipping traffic now cuts the dialogue short and wastes your no-damage run before it starts.

## The meet

Park in the marked bay at the Gas-N-Go and walk up to Jason's contact by the pumps. It is meant to be a simple hand-off: collect what Jason is owed and leave. Then a second car rolls in.

> **Spoiler:** The people in the second car are the ones the contact owes money to, and they are not here to talk.

## It goes sideways

Your first firefight, and the shooting tutorial. Take cover behind the fuel pumps when the prompt appears, then pop out to deal with the three who pile out of the car. Once they are down, grab the duffel off the ground.

> **Tip:** Snap to cover, let aim assist pull onto the nearest target, and fire in bursts. You don't need headshots here. You need to not get hit.

> **Watch out:** Don't shoot the pumps. The explosion looks great and takes a chunk of your health with it.

## Get out

Sprint back to the car. The shots drew attention, so a single wanted star lights up as you pull away. Jason shouts directions. Just drive: the goal now is distance.

## Lose the heat

A search cone sweeps the minimap. Break the police line of sight by cutting down a side street or slipping under an overpass, and stay out of the shrinking search area until the star clears.

> **Tip:** Corners beat straightaways. Every turn out of sight shrinks the search area faster than flooring it down a main road.

## Back to the motel

Pull into the Sun Glow Motel to trigger the closing cutscene and finish the mission.

> **Spoiler (ending of this mission):** They count the duffel, and it is light. That gap between what was promised and what is in the bag is the thread the story pulls on.
