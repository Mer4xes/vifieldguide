Opens after [Girls' Trip](/walkthrough/girls-trip/). This is the car-theft tutorial, and what you learn here is how you make money from cars for the rest of the game.

## Drive in

Bring any car to Moses's garage in Port Gellhorn and drive inside to start.

## Follow Moses

Follow Moses on foot to a lot nearby.

## Scan the cars

Open the WAiNK app on your phone and scan the cars he points out. A scan tells you what a car is worth and what kind of tracker it has:

- **No tracker:** safe to sell or keep.
- **Standard tracker:** Moses won't take it for now.
- **Advanced tracker:** high-end cars. You need the key cloner, which comes in Chapter 3.

## Get the Slim Jim

Pick up the Slim Jim. It is your tool for getting into locked cars from now on.

## Break into the Montagne XL

Go to the door of the Montagne XL and use the Slim Jim. Move the right stick until the bar turns pink and the lock pops.

## Back to the garage

Follow Moses back. If the car you brought had a tracker, he sends you out to find a clean one first.

> **Watch out:** Moses refuses cars stolen too close to his garage. Take one from further out, a main street is fine.

## Register a car

Respray the car, then register it as Lucia's. A registered car is yours to keep and can be delivered to you through the new Scooter Bros app.

> **Tip:** After each sale the garage closes for a minute or two. You also only have room for one registered car until you get a garage, or you sell one to make space.
