Opens right after [What's in the Bag?](/walkthrough/whats-in-the-bag/). The marker shows up the first time you step out of the motel room. It is a quick one and there is nothing to miss.

## The barn

Walk to the barn a short way east of the Starlet Motel and climb the stairs at the back. Lucia gets there just in time to watch a few addicts drive off in the ambulance.

## Chase it on the quad

Jump on the quad bike parked in the barn and follow the ambulance. Stay close and try not to crash; you don't have to ram it or shoot anyone.

> **Tip:** The ambulance stops on its own when it crashes into a tree a little to the south. Just don't lose it before then.

## Burn it

Pick up the red jerry can lying behind the wrecked ambulance. Hold the prompt to pour fuel over it, then choose **Ignite**.

> **Note:** If you have a gun, you can also throw the can into the back and shoot it.

## Get clear

The fire draws the police. Leave the area straight away and the mission ends.
