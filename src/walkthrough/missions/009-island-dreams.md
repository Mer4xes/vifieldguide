Your first dive. Kenton runs the Island Dreams shop by the beach; talk to him there to start.

## Head out

Drive the boat with the three guests to the dive spot.

## Suit up and dive

Put on the yellow scuba gear at the back of the boat and go in. Hold R1/RB to dive and press X/A to surface. The oxygen bar is at the top left, and you have plenty.

## Lead them to the wreck

Guide the group through the trench on the seabed to the shipwreck.

## Find the treasure

Pick the metal detector from the weapon wheel and aim it with L2/LT. Its light changes from red to green as you get close, then a chest icon appears on the seabed. You need all three, and they are outside the wreck, in the marked area.

## Free the diver and get back

One of the guests gets stuck under a grate. Lift it to free them, then escort everyone back to the boat.

> **Watch out:** A shark shows up on the way back. Switch to the harpoon in the weapon wheel; two hits drive it off.

> **Tip:** Hold R1/RB near fish to study them. It is optional, but animals you study count toward 100%.

> **Spoiler:** Whether you save the guest from the shark only changes the dialogue at the end.
