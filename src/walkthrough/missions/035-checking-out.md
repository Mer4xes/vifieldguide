The mission opens once every other Chapter 2 story mission is done and you have $15,000. Your room at the motel gets a marker, and a call comes in from your partner about something Cal has sent.

> **Watch out:** Starting this mission ends Chapter 2. Do the checklist above first.

## Regroup at the motel

Go back to the room. Open the bathroom door to set things off.

## Defend yourself

As Jason, grab the shotgun off the bed and fight off the attackers.

## Onto the bike

Lucia takes a motorbike and follows Jason. Stay close to his marker.

> **Watch out:** There is a known bug here. If you die during the bike ride, Jason can stop showing as an objective and the police search never ends. Restart the mission if that happens.

## Jump to the car

When the prompt shows, hold the button to jump from the bike to the moving car.

## Escape

Get away from the police with Jason. The mission ends as you reach Vice City.
