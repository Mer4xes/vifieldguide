Life at the penthouse doesn't stay calm for long. After [Taking Liberties](/walkthrough/taking-liberties/), go to where **Kat and Mattia** are sitting. Kat's baby is on the way, and Mattia needs [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) to get him out of Giorgio's place in a hurry.

## Follow Mattia

When the two of them get up, follow them to the **elevator**. Mattia asks for your help, and you get to answer. See [the choice](#choices) above: refusing ends the mission on the spot, and your answer changes a scene in the end credits.

## Get the key fob

If you said yes, Lucia heads into the living room to keep **Dardan** busy. When she throws his phone into the **pool**, move fast: go to the **couch** where he was sitting and grab the **key fob**.

## Pick a car

Take it back to Mattia, who's waiting by the **car elevator**. Ride it down to Giorgio's **garage** and take whichever car you like.

## Race to the hospital

The hospital is in the **north-east** of the city and you have **just under 4 minutes**. The timer is forgiving as long as you drive carefully and don't crash.

Follow Mattia inside when you arrive. The game brings you back to the penthouse and the mission ends.
