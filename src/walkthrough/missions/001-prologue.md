You play Lucia only. The whole mission is a tutorial for talking, threatening and running, and nothing in it can be missed.

## Talk to your cellmates

You wake up in Lucia's cell at Hamlet Correctional. Hold L2/LT to focus on one of the other women and start a conversation. Talk to whoever you like; the scene moves on either way.

## Follow the guard

CO Wilson comes to take Lucia to the counselor. Walk behind him through the block until you reach the office.

> **Spoiler:** The counselor is there to talk about early release, and the conversation sends Lucia back to the day she was arrested.

## Rob the Payday Loan store

The flashback drops you inside a Payday Loan store. Aim at the teller, threaten him until he gives in, then press Triangle/Y to scoop up the cash.

> **Tip:** This is the game teaching you how a hold-up works. Pointing the gun keeps the teller frozen; you don't need to fire.

## Shake off the customer

On the way out a customer grabs Lucia. A button prompt appears on screen. Press it as shown to break loose.

## Get away

The police arrive fast and a search area appears on the minimap. Keep moving away from the officers and out of sight until the scene changes. Getting caught here does not mean failure.

## Back to the coffee shop

Head for the coffee shop marker. The ending of the flashback is fixed: however well you run, the story plays out the same way.

> **Spoiler:** Lucia is arrested at the coffee shop. The scene explains why she is in prison and why getting out early means so much to her.
