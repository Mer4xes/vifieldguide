A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job where [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) never leaves the van. Karim hands him a drone, and the whole mission is spent tailing the two bodybuilders from Ajax Gym, Nate and Cisco, across Ocean Beach and into the apartment they call on. It opens after [Power Play](/walkthrough/power-play/).

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

## Meet Karim on the garage roof

As Jason, drive to the parking garage marked in **Ocean Beach** and take it all the way up to the top floor. Karim is waiting next to a van, and the drone launches from there.

> **Tip:** The drone holds its height on its own, a bit like a racing drone. Use the triggers to take it up and down.

## Find the bodybuilders on The Calique

Fly over to **The Calique** and sweep its roof. The pair are on the **north side**, tucked under the smaller white roof. Bring the drone in low near the white boxes and the game picks them up.

## Get close enough to listen

A bar at the bottom of the screen shows how well you can hear them. Edge in until it fills, then hold that distance while they talk.

## Find their car in the alley

When Nate and Cisco step into the elevator, don't follow them in. Fly round to the **alley on the right-hand side of the elevator**; Cisco's car is parked there.

## Tail the car

Follow them as they drive off. Keep the bar green, but stay up in the air and don't drop to street level near the car. They stop once on the way so Nate can collect something from a building, then carry on.

## Fly in through the third balcony

When the two of them go into an apartment, Karim tells you which balcony belongs to it. Go to the **third balcony** and fly in through the door that's been left open.

## Plant the six cameras

The man who lives there is home and moves from room to room while you work. Watch the awareness meter and keep the drone in a different room from him. The spots:

- **Living room:** next to the TV, and on the shelf on the wall opposite.
- **Kitchen:** wait until he walks out, then the corner of the counter, the top of the fridge and the shelf near the front door.
- **Bedroom:** once he's opened the door, the shelf to the right of the bed.

> **Tip:** If he's heading your way, back off into a room he isn't in and wait for him to move on.

## Watch the interrogation

Nate and Cisco arrive and get rough with him. Flick between the camera feeds with **L1 / LB** and **R1 / RB** so you keep the action on screen.

> **Spoiler:** The man is Omar, who runs baggage handling at the airport. The bodybuilders are leaning on him for a way into VCIA.

## Get the drone out

Karim slips up and the drone starts broadcasting into the room. Get it out of the apartment before anyone works out where the noise is coming from.

## Race back to the van

Fly straight to the van marked green on the street. Getting there quickly earns the **Professional** bonus.

> **Spoiler:** Once the drone is back, Karim shows Jason what he's pulled off the hard drive: footage of Camilia Fuentes, the former pop star who is now a Leonida senator.

## Drop Lucia off

When the scene ends, get in the car with [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) and drive her to the alley by **Ajax Gym**. Leaving her there ends the mission.
