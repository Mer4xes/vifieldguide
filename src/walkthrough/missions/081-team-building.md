A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job for [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/). It opens after [Power Play](/walkthrough/power-play/), when Kordell asks her to come to **Ajax Gym**. It's a short one built around a single melee fight.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

## Meet Kordell at Ajax Gym

As Lucia, go to Ajax Gym in **Washington Beach** and talk to Kordell. Follow him outside to his car in the **alley** and get in.

## Drive to the warehouse

Drive to the warehouse Kordell gives you. When you get there, walk round the **left side** to the doorway and go in.

## Win the cage fight

Lucia is pushed into a cage. Block and dodge with **X / A**, then keep hitting until the first fighter goes down. A second one comes in straight after.

When a **third** fighter tries to get into the cage, Kordell joins the fight. Finish off whoever's left with him.

> **Spoiler:** The warehouse is an underground fight club where Kordell looks for muscle for the job. He doesn't trust Lucia yet, but he still steps in when she's in trouble.

## Back to Kordell's car

Once Arlo has gone, head outside and get back in Kordell's car.

## Drive to Riverbank

Take the car to the **Riverbank Waterside Bar** and follow Kordell inside. The scene there ends the mission.

> **Spoiler:** Kordell has brought Lucia to meet Raul, the man Cisco has been following. It gets tense, but Raul walks away and Lucia's cover holds.
