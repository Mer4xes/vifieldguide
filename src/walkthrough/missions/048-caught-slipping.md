A short Chapter 3 story mission for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/), with a foot chase for each of them. It opens once you've finished both [Boobie Time](/walkthrough/boobie-time/) and [First Impressions](/walkthrough/first-impressions/): go to the Bayside apartment and it starts as you try to go in.

## Jason's chase

Someone has been in the apartment, and the thief runs. As Jason, give chase on foot. The route cuts through the construction site next door, so stay close.

> **Tip:** Hold Focus (L3 + R3 on PlayStation) and you can line up a quick shot at the thief's legs to end the chase.

## Search the bag

Once the thief is down, walk over and search the bag.

## Lucia's chase

The game switches to Lucia, who has a second thief to deal with. Same idea: keep up, use Focus if you have a clear line, and bring them down.

## Check what they dropped

This one drops a bag. Go over and search it.

> **Spoiler:** The thing Jason and Lucia were most worried about is still safe. They decide it shouldn't stay in the apartment.

## Off to Big House Storage

Meet up in front of the apartment and drive south to Big House Storage, shown on the map.

## The storage unit

Follow Jason into the building and take the elevator up with him to the unit he has bought. The mission ends there.

> **Note:** No money and no choices in this one. See the [walkthrough list](/walkthrough/#chapter-3) for what follows in Chapter 3.
