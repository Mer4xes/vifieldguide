Opens after [Mami Knows Best](/walkthrough/mami-knows-best/), once Mia messages Lucia. Head to Mia's office as Lucia to start. Expect a tail, some driving and one proper gunfight at the end.

> **Note:** The mission guides we checked don't agree on where this sits in the chapter. Some put it right after Mami Knows Best, others near the end of Chapter 3. Start it once Mia's message comes in.

## The house viewing

Leave the office with Mia and go to the house she is selling. Go inside with her. When the objective changes, head back out.

> **Spoiler:** Members of the M7 gang grab Mia at the house.

## Tail the kidnappers

Get into Mia's car and follow the M7 vehicle. Keep a good distance: the job is to see where they go without being spotted, so don't ride their bumper. Follow them all the way to where they stop.

## Meet Terro

Inside you meet Terro, who runs M7.

> **Spoiler:** Lucia is caught as well. Mia talks her way out by offering to find Terro a better headquarters, so the "viewing" carries on with the gang along for the ride.

## Show the properties

Drive Terro's crew to each property on the list, with Mia doing the selling.

## Deal with Viper

A fight breaks out with Viper's people. Take cover, clear them out, and go to Mia as soon as the objective switches to saving her.

> **Spoiler (ending):** Helping Terro deal with Viper is what gets Mia and Lucia let go.

## Mia's commission

The mission pays **$2,000**, Mia's commission, but it doesn't arrive straight away.

> **Tip:** If the money isn't there when the mission ends, carry on playing. It comes later.
