The job after [Fresh Evidence](/walkthrough/fresh-evidence/). Andrés's son Rafa has been taken, and the people holding him want a handover at a quarry. [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) does the sneaking, [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) does the covering, and both of you end up in a chase across the city. Start it at **Megamundo HQ** in Downtown as either of them.

> **Tip:** After [Adventures in Babysitting](/walkthrough/adventures-in-babysitting/) the mission list shows **Rafa's Kidnapping**. That's a phone call, not a mission: it comes from Abe once Jason has nothing else open, Fresh Evidence included, and it puts Child's Play on the map.

> **Note:** The map marks Child's Play like a limited-time job, but it won't disappear. Once everything else for Jason and Lucia before [Power Play](/walkthrough/power-play/) is finished, the game simply makes you play it.

## Drive to the quarry

After the first scene you're already in the car. Follow the route to the **quarry south of the airport**. At the gate you get waved down; keep driving in to the meeting point, where Lucia gets out.

## Find Rafa with the binoculars

The game switches you to Lucia to look the place over. Tag enemies by looking at them and pressing **D-pad right**. Rafa is on the **upper level of the building straight ahead**, the one with a **row of red lights** running above its walkway.

## Sneak up to Rafa

Lucia has a **silenced pistol**, so you can drop guards quietly. Stay to the **right**, close to the buildings, and make for the middle of the metal structures. Take the stairwell, then the ladder.

At the top, head south and up the stairs on the right. Two guards come down those stairs and walk off to the left; let them go. Follow the walkway to Rafa's door, keeping an eye on the pair at the corner, and go in through the door on the left. There's **one more guard** inside before you reach the boy.

## Protect Andrés

Down below, the meeting falls apart. As Jason, take out the group in front of you first, then fall back towards the cars. More of them come out of the building on the right.

## Get Rafa down

Switch to Lucia when the prompt appears. She carries Rafa now, and her aim suffers for it. Head north along the walkway to the ladder. She puts him down so he can climb; while he does, clear the gunmen on the walkways across from you, then follow him down.

On the lower platform pick him up again and get to the ground. Someone grabs Lucia there: mash **Square / X** to break loose and shoot the man who has Rafa straight away.

Keep fighting your way **south-east**. Rafa jumps on a forklift and drives off; hop onto the platform on its right so you stay with him.

## Hand Rafa over

When the others pull up in the car, carry Rafa over to **Gustavo** at the door.

> **Spoiler:** Gustavo doesn't wait for anyone. He drives off with Rafa, and he has been working with the people who took him.

## Chase Gustavo

Jason takes the wheel. When the option comes up, switch to Lucia to shoot from the car while Jason drives.

Before long the car flips. Get out and protect Andrés from the gunmen who arrive, then grab a **bike** nearby and get after the convoy.

## The bike chase

Switch to Lucia on the back of the bike: she handles the **big truck** sitting between you and the car with Rafa far better than Jason can. Enemy bikes pull up on both sides. When Jason calls out the **helicopter** ahead, shoot the pilot or the men inside to bring it down fast.

## Pull Rafa out of the SUV

Andrés turns up in another car and forces Gustavo off the road. Run down to the SUV on the **train tracks** and open the door. Mash **R2 / RT** to cut the seatbelt, rotate the **left stick**, then mash **Circle / B** to pull Rafa out.

## Catch Gustavo

Once Rafa is with his father, chase Gustavo up to the highway. He tries to take another car, fails, and ends up on the ground in front of you. A few scenes later the mission ends with **$30,000** and the **Family Matters** trophy.

> **Spoiler:** Gustavo, head of security for the Navarro family, had been working with FAP all along, and he doesn't leave the highway alive. Rafa is back with Petra and Andrés, and Abe lets Jason and Lucia know their new identities are ready.
