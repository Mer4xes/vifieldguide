Lucia only, a job from [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) after the dinner in [Meet the Heders](/walkthrough/meet-the-heders/). One car, one drop-off.

## Scout the chop shop

Drive to the gang's chop shop in Salton. Before you walk in the front, look for the way up onto the roof: it gives you a view of the yard and a way in from above.

## Find the Schafter

The car Raul wants is a **Schafter V12**, marked once you're inside. Get in and drive it out of the yard.

## Lose the heat

If you picked up wanted stars on the way out, shake them before you head for the drop-off.

## Deliver to Balsero

Take the Schafter to the car dealership in Balsero and drive into the marked lot.

> **Spoiler:** The buyer is Randy Kozlov, who runs the dealership and fences high-end cars on the side. He says he'll have more work for you.

## Randy opens for business

From now on Randy takes stolen cars, so he becomes one of your [fences](/guides/fences/). His own first job, a Zorrusso, follows later in the chapter; see [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#randy).
