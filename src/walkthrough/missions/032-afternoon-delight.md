Vicki calls the two of them some time after [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/), once the other open story missions are done. It pays more than anything else in the chapter, so it covers most of the $15,000 you need.

## Getting in

Lucia goes in through the front. Jason has to find his own way: go round the back, wait for the bouncers to walk off, then climb the fence and slip in through the dancers' dressing room.

## Help or don't help

Backstage, Jason gets a choice.

- **Help:** Jason goes on stage and dances. Lucia heads for the office alone, gets caught, and Jason comes to get her. Afterwards you buy him new clothes before meeting Vicki. Longer, with more scenes.
- **Don't help:** Jason gets the robbery going by firing into the ceiling, and the two of them go for the safe together. Quicker, but the chase afterwards is harder.

> **Note:** Both routes end the same way and pay the same. Pick the one you want to see.

## The safe

Open the safe in the office. Story safes show you the code, and both guides we checked report it as **2008**.

## Shoot your way out

Security knows what you did. Fight your way out of the club.

## Lose the bikers

Bikers chase you once you're outside. Shake them off.

## Meet Vicki

Take the bag to Vicki at the docks. She pays **$10,000** into BuckMe the next day.
