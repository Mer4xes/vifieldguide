Straight after [Bird in the Hand](/walkthrough/bird-in-the-hand/), back at the penthouse. There's no driving or shooting in this one.

## Find Giorgio in the spa

Go to the marked room **near the gym**. Giorgio is relaxing in the **spa**.

> **Spoiler:** He offers Jason and Lucia a **full presidential pardon** if they keep working for him. Before the deal is done, a hitman from the Albanian mob walks in. He gives Giorgio one last chance: stop a rival cocaine shipment on its way from Latin America to Europe.

## Follow him to the office

When Giorgio leaves the spa, follow him to his **office**. A scene plays and the mission ends.

Afterwards you're free to move around the penthouse, where two people want a word: Vinny on the balcony by the pool ([Doggie Bag](/walkthrough/doggie-bag/)) and Mattia and Kat ([VC Baby](/walkthrough/vc-baby/)).
