The big one. Everything in the [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) run of Chapter 4 leads here: the heist on **Vice City International Airport**, planned by [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) and pulled with Kordell's crew. You switch between [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) and [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) throughout. Start it from the **planning room** once every setup mission and [Headliners](/walkthrough/headliners/) are done.

> **Note:** The mission needs every setup job finished, and that includes two that sit a little outside the main run: [Adventures in Babysitting](/walkthrough/adventures-in-babysitting/) and [Headliners](/walkthrough/headliners/).

## Into the airport with Nate

You start as Lucia. Follow Nate through the airport.

## Drive the catering truck to the terminal

The game hands you Jason, at the wheel of the catering truck with the crew on board. Drive it round the airport to the terminal marked on your map.

## Deal with Janice

Back as Lucia, stay with Nate while he shows the wine expert around. When he spills a drink on Janice, follow her into the **bathroom**. Get behind her and press **R2 / RT** to knock her out. Lucia drags her into a stall on her own.

## Follow the vault manager

After a word with Raul, who is in disguise, follow Nate as he pushes the crate along behind the vault manager. In the elevator the manager asks Lucia a question about wine; answer it.

## Rooftop: reach the network terminal

Over to Jason on the airport roof, and a **5-minute timer** starts. Head for the marker in the **middle of the roof**. Guards are posted on the higher platforms: pick them off quietly with the **silenced pistol** or sneak up and knock them out.

When you reach the upper level, go into the **caged area** and interact with the terminal.

> **Watch out:** This is the only timed part of the mission. Don't get drawn into a long fight on the way.

## Open the vault

As Lucia, follow the manager into the vault and interact with the **wine crate**. When Kordell gives the signal, hit the manager to knock him out.

Run back down the hallway and press the **two buttons** to open the vault gates. Lucia ties up the guards, then heads back to the vault. Once the crew have the bags, follow them to the elevator.

## Take care of Omar

As Jason, press **R2 / RT** to knock Omar out. Pick him up and drop him in the marked **container**.

## Load the plane

Lucia drives the baggage cart with Kordell's crew to the plane at the **south terminal**. Take bags off the cart one at a time and put them on the loader until the plane is full.

## Fight across the tarmac

Things go wrong. As Jason, fight through the police on the tarmac towards the plane. They jump out from behind containers and vehicles. The game switches you to Lucia for a while to hold them off from her side too.

## Catch the plane

Jason ends up in a truck chasing the plane down the runway. Pull up on the **right side, close to the landing gear**, so Lucia can climb aboard.

> **Spoiler:** Raul's people turn on Kordell's crew and try to take the whole score for themselves.

## Chase the truck and fight Raina

Keep after the truck, with police on its tail, until it hits the highway. Meanwhile Lucia has to beat **Raina** to get into the plane. It's a fist fight, but an easier one than the big man in Headliners.

Back with Jason, Cisco starts throwing things out of the back of the truck. Use **Focus (L3 + R3)** to line up a quick shot on him.

## Investigate the truck

When the truck crashes, walk up to it. Omar crawls out. You don't have to kill him: a **shot to the leg** or a **knockout** gets you the **Professional** bonus.

## Find Raul on the plane

Lucia works her way forward through the cabin. The men on board are a gang Raul has hired, and some of them are hiding between the seats. When Kordell grabs Lucia, press **Square / X** at the prompt to fight back, then shoot him when he's dragged through the door.

> **Spoiler:** None of the bodybuilders survive the day, and neither does Karim.

## Defend Andrea and get in the van

On the ground, Jason holds off **NOOSE** while Andrea loads the bags. Look on the ground for **Francisco's LMG-6** and pick it up before you go; it's yours to keep. When Andrea shouts that the bags are in, get in the van fast.

## Land the plane

The last part is Lucia and Raul in the cockpit. Hold **R2 / RT** and pull the **left stick down** to keep the plane steady until it's down. A few scenes play out and the mission ends with **$250,000** and the **Hi-Jacked** trophy.

> **Spoiler:** Lucia and Raul bring the jet down in Vice City Bay, and Andrea and Jason get away with the money. Raul tells everyone to lie low: he, Valentina and Abe will sell the hard drive to the highest bidder, either the senator from Karim's footage or Giorgio Gallo.
