Brian calls Jason in, and the two of them head out into the swamp to sort things out with the Kingfishers face to face. Expect a big fight and a long chase. Nothing in this mission can be missed.

## Ride out with Brian

Meet Brian and get into his truck. He drives you out to the Kingfishers' camp, and it is a long ride, so sit back.

## Walk through the camp

On foot, stick with Brian as he crosses the camp to talk to the Kingfishers waiting at the far end.

> **Spoiler:** The talk does not stay friendly for long. The meeting turns into a fight, and you have to get out with Brian.

## Fight your way out

The camp is full of Kingfishers. You don't have to kill every one, but thinning them out makes the escape a lot easier. Use the shacks and vehicles as cover and keep Brian's marker in sight.

> **Spoiler:** In the chaos, the Kingfishers grab Lori and drive off with her. The new objective is to reach her with Brian.

## Take the helicopter

There is a helicopter at the edge of the camp. Get in and follow the marker.

> **Tip:** Shoot from the helicopter to thin out the Kingfishers below before the chase moves on.

## Catch the boat

The chase carries on over water in a boat of your own. Pull right up alongside the boat you are after and jump across.

> **Spoiler:** The kidnappers move Lori onto that boat. Once you are aboard, take down the man holding her and the mission ends in a cutscene.
