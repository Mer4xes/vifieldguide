A Chapter 3 story mission for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) only. [Boobie](/characters/boobie-ike/) needs a driver, so it starts at his club, the Jack of Hearts. Mostly driving, with one chase on the water.

## Pick up Boobie

Meet Boobie at the Jack of Hearts, follow him out to his car and take the wheel.

## Run his errands

Drive to each marker and let Boobie take care of business. At each stop, wait for Boobie to finish his conversation before you pull away.

## Chase on the water

Later in the day someone grabs Boobie's bag and escapes along the river. Jump on the Seashark and go after them. Stay close; the chase follows the river until the bag ends up in the water.

## Grab the bag

The bag floats where it dropped, shown by a marker. Ride straight over it to pick it up.

> **Watch out:** Don't head back without it. If you miss it on the first pass, turn around and go over the marker again.

## Back to Boobie

Take the Seashark back to where Boobie is waiting, get off at the shore and climb back into his car.

## Meet Yasmani

Drive to the last marker, a shop, and go inside. Boobie has someone he wants Jason to meet: Yasmani. The talk ends the mission.

> **Note:** From now on Yasmani buys stolen jewelry and marked bills, so he joins your [fences](/guides/fences/). His own [jewelry jobs](/guides/side-contacts/#yasmani) open after this mission. Next on our list is [First Impressions](/walkthrough/first-impressions/).
