After [Party Hard](/walkthrough/party-hard/), [Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/) has a job for you: a promo shoot for the [Real Dimez](/characters/real-dimez/). Meet him at the marked building in Tequesta. You play as both [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/).

## Up to the roof

Go inside and follow Dre'Quan into the lift, then out onto the roof where the helicopter is waiting.

## Photograph the Dimez

Once you're airborne you're Lucia. Bring up the phone camera and press R2 / RT to snap a photo every time the Dimez pose.

> **Spoiler:** Dre'Quan heads down to the lobby to meet a club promoter called KD, and walks into an ambush. Cash City take him.

## Chase the car from the air

The game switches you to Jason, who's flying the helicopter. Follow the car that's taking Dre'Quan away.

## Chase the car on a bike

When the car drives into a building, switch to Lucia, who picks up the chase on a bike at street level.

> **Watch out:** You can't shoot at the car here. Just stay with it.

After the second jump more cars join in and start attacking. Now you can shoot back, so take them out to keep the pressure off.

## Rescue Dre'Quan

When the chase stops, walk up to the gang and fight your way through to Dre'Quan.

## Clear the alley under the highway

More of them arrive.

> **Watch out:** Cars pull in from the right, and garage doors on the left of the alley can open with more gunmen behind them. Keep an eye on both sides.

Once everyone is down, get to the car at the end of the alley to finish.

> **Spoiler:** To make up for it, KD promises Dre'Quan a meeting with Royce Jones, another club promoter.
