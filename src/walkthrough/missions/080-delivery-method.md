A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/), set up by Andrea. It opens after [Power Play](/walkthrough/power-play/): go to the planning room as Jason to get the brief. Jason goes in dressed as a food delivery rider, and the whole job is about not getting caught.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

> **Spoiler:** The drives are for the Transport Security Services network. Once they're in, Lucia's details are on the system, so her cover as a wine expert holds up on the day of Kordell's job.

## Ride to the security gate

After the scene, get on the delivery bike and ride **south** to the airport's security gate.

## Find the TSS office

Once the guards let you in, turn **right** and go into the **TSS office**.

## Chat to the receptionist

Talk to the woman at the front desk. When she steps away, move fast.

## Plant the first drive

Slip **behind her desk** and interact with her computer to plug in the drive. When the hack finishes, interact again to **take the drive back**.

## Pull the fire alarm

The alarm is **behind the reception desk**. Interact with it and the building starts to empty.

## Go upstairs

Wait for everyone to clear out, then go up the stairs to the **second floor** straight away.

> **Watch out:** You don't have long. If someone comes back while you're still up there, you're caught and reported for trespassing.

## Hack the database computer

The computer you want is in the office at the **back left** of the room. Plug the drive in, wait for the hack to finish, then pull it out.

## Ride out

Leave the building quickly. If you were fast, the staff are still standing at the muster point on the other side of the car park. Get back on the bike and ride out of the airport to end the mission. Doing the whole thing without being caught earns the **Professional** bonus.
