A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/). [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) has got him a trial shift as a baggage handler at **Vice City International Airport**, and Karim rides along through a pair of camera glasses, mapping the place for the heist. It opens after [Making Gainz](/walkthrough/making-gainz/). It's a driving and loading job with a bit of sneaking around, and no gunfights.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

## Get into the staff car park

Drive Jason to **VCIA**. Pull up at the gate to the staff car park, talk to the guard, then drive in and leave the car in the lot.

## Report to the office

Walk over to the office nearby to start the shift. Afterwards you ride into the airport in a truck driven by **Omar**, the baggage boss you'll be working under.

> **Tip:** The ride is in first person. Look around: the glasses put a white box on things worth a closer look, and each one turns green once Karim has logged it as a point of interest.

## Follow Omar

When the truck stops, get out and stay with Omar. He walks you over to where the baggage carts are kept.

## Hook up the baggage tug

Climb into the **baggage tug** and back it into the **two trailers** so both latch on behind you.

> **Tip:** Get the tug dead straight in front of each trailer before you reverse, and go slowly. If you come in at an angle, drive forward, straighten up and try again.

## Drive to the plane

Take the tug **southeast** to the southern side of the airport, where a plane is being unloaded.

## Load the bags

Now you pass bags to Omar and he stacks them on the trailers. Each throw has a power bar: hold **R2 / RT** to fill it and let go at roughly **half**. The bar keeps creeping up after you release, so let go a fraction early. Keep going until every bag is on.

## Slip away to the loading bay

Once the plane is done, Jason makes an excuse and leaves Omar. Head out of the bay, walk **south along the wall** and go in through the **last set of double doors on the left**.

## Find the security office

Inside, keep to the **left-hand wall** until you reach a hallway, then take the **blue office door on the right**.

## Plant Karim's device

The restroom is **immediately to your right** as you come in. Go in and use the prompt **behind the toilet** to leave Karim's device. It sits close to the airport's security system, which is what Karim needs.

## Back to Omar

Leave the restroom and the game takes you back to Omar on its own.

## Tell Omar you want the job

When Omar starts talking to you, hold **L2 / LT** to focus on him and tell him you want the job. That ends the shift and the mission.

> **Spoiler:** The shift is cover. Everything Jason looks at and plants today feeds the plan for **VCIA Takedown**, the airport heist.
