Opens after [Dealer's Choice](/walkthrough/dealers-choice/). Lucia meets Mia in Peacock Bay. No hard parts, but be ready to shoot fast at one moment in the back yard.

## Meet Mia

Drive to Peacock Bay and talk to Mia. Then get in her car and drive to the marked house in Hamlet.

## Look around the house

Follow Mia inside and walk through the rooms while she looks the place over. When she needs a hand, go back to the entrance and interact with her to help her up.

## Clear the lawn

While Mia is on the phone, tidy the front lawn. Pick up a piece of junk, carry it to the blue dumpster and press R2/RT to throw it in. Keep going until the scene moves on.

## The pool

Follow Mia round to the back yard. Interact with the mattress in the pool, hold L2 and R2 (LT and RT) and tap X/A to drag it out together.

## Save Mia

Something in the pool goes for Mia's leg. Shoot it fast.

> **Spoiler:** It is an alligator. Right after, gang members from the house next door turn up with guns and tell the two of you to stay away.

## The excavator

Once the visitors have gone, Mia points you to the excavator across the street. Get in and drive it over to the house next door.

## Wreck the place

Smash the house and the cars on the driveway. Press R1/RB to close the claw on a car, pick it up and throw it.

## Drive to the office

When you have done enough damage, get back in Mia's car and drive to her office in town to finish.
