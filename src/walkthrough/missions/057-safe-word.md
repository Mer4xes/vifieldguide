Another piece of **The Cartel Heist**. Nicole can't open the bank's vault without practice, and the closest thing to it is the safe in Gil's mansion. In the game the mission shows up as **Steal the Practice Safe**, and either [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) or [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) can start it.

> **Note:** Story-order lists disagree on whether this or [The Missing Link](/walkthrough/the-missing-link/) comes first. They're separate jobs, so play them in whichever order the game offers.

## Drive to Peacock Estates

Talk to Nicole, then take the big truck parked outside. Gil's mansion is in Peacock Estates, right at the southern edge of Vice City.

## Find a way in

The game switches you to Jason. Slip in through the front gate, crouch, and work your way round the right side of the house through the archway. Before you get far, a woman walks out of a door and stops you: from here on, Jason has to fit in.

## Play along at the pool

Gil is shooting a film in the back garden, and the crew takes Jason for the pool guy. Follow Gil's assistant out back, go to your mark and pick up the skimmer. Sweep the left stick back and forth to skim the pool until you're told to stop, then do the other small jobs the director hands you.

> **Tip:** When the director asks if Jason wants to take part further, saying no costs you nothing and keeps Lucia happy. See [the choice](#choices) above.

## Deal with Rodney

The script calls for a fight with another actor, Rodney. Hit him to stay in the scene, take a few punches back, then knock him out.

## Into Gil's office

Head round the house to the door you nearly used earlier. Go through the first set of double doors, then the closed doors on the right of the hallway, into Gil's office.

## Find the remote

Search the desk. The remote for the safe is in the drawer on the left.

## Winch the safe out

When Lucia backs the truck up to the house, open the window. Attach the winch to the safe, and the game switches you to Lucia: hold R2 / RT until the safe tears out of the wall.

## Escape with the safe

Drive off with the safe dragging behind you. Steer left and right to swing it into the cars chasing you, and keep heading west to the marker where Felipe is waiting. The police join in on the way.

> **Watch out:** Hitting cyclists with the safe, or knocking over gravestones when the route takes you through the cemetery, pushes your [criminal profile](/how-it-plays/#criminal-profile) towards Psychopath.

## Drop it with Felipe

Follow the route to a garage and drive in to hand the safe over.

## Practice on the safe

You're taken to the practice safe. It comes in three stages:

1. **Drill.** Hold L2 + R2 (LT + RT) at the right spot. The left gauge creeps up as you get through, so keep adjusting, and keep the right gauge where it is. Don't let the drill light go red.
2. **Cable.** Move the cable in and out with the left stick and look around with the right. Follow the path without touching the cable or the walls, then hook the gold ring at the end with R2 / RT.
3. **Hold the door.** Hold L2 + R2 and tap X / A to keep the door secure while Nicole drills and puts in the combination.

Open the safe for **$1,500** and **Gil's Kalinga .50**. You can run the practice as many times as you like; quit out when you're done to finish the mission.

> **Tip:** The heist at the end of the chapter uses exactly the same safe cracking, so a few extra runs here are worth it.
