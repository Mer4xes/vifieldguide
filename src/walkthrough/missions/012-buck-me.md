Lucia calls not long after **Coming Home**. It is a short mission, but it gives you the BuckMe app, the game's way of keeping money you can't lose.

## Go to the bank

Drive to the Fleeca bank in Key Lento and talk to the teller. She sends you on to the manager.

## See the manager

Follow the manager into his office, where Lucia joins Jason. A normal account is off the table after the credit check, so the manager points them to BuckMe instead.

## Set up BuckMe

The app opens on Jason's phone with a $100 welcome bonus in the checking balance. Follow the prompt and move that $100 into Family Savings to finish.

> **Watch out:** Money in Family Savings can't be taken out again. In return it is protected: you never lose it. Only put in what you won't need to spend.

> **Tip:** Cash in your pocket is lost when you die or get arrested. Deposit it at an ATM; the app's ATM Finder sets a waypoint to the nearest one.

> **Tip:** Reaching savings goals in Family Savings raises the checking limit and unlocks discounts.
