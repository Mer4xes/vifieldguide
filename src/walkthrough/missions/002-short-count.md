## Talk to Jason

Walk into the motel room to start the mission. After the cutscene you control Lucia alone for the first time.

## The bakery

Drive to the bakery in Little Cuba and go inside. Speak to the owner at the counter.

> **Missable:** Pick up the cassette on the counter before you talk to the owner. You cannot get back in afterwards.

## Tail the courier

A courier leaves through the back. Follow on foot and keep one street corner between you. If the meter above the minimap fills, stop behind cover until it drains.

> **Tip:** Stay on the opposite pavement. The courier only checks the side of the street they are walking on.
