Lucia's release date comes in by phone after **Feng Shui**. There is no fail state for most of this one, but what you do before the pickup decides how the relationship between Jason and Lucia starts.

## Get ready before she's out

You have free time before Lucia calls. There are five optional errands: buy groceries, buy flowers, buy Lucia a necklace, get a haircut ("The Chop") and pick up clothes for her. You can also fix up Jason's house, which Lucia notices when she gets there.

> **Tip:** Do all five errands before the pickup and the relationship starts at three of four hearts. You can check it in the Jason and Lucia menu.

> **Watch out:** Buy the necklace. Both guides we checked say it comes back later, in Chapter 2.

## Wait for the call

A marker shows up at the prison early, but driving there does nothing until Lucia calls again. Sleep at Jason's house to move time on if you are done with the errands.

## Pick her up

Drive to Hamlet Correctional and talk to the guard at reception. Bring the clothes in the car if you got them.

## Drinks at the Rusty Anchor

Head to the Rusty Anchor bar in Key Lento and find Cal inside. Buy two beers at the bar (it is $6) and take them out to Lucia. If you brought her clothes, she changes before she comes out.

## Dance with Lucia

Accept the dance prompt and push the left stick forward to keep dancing until the next scene starts.

> **Spoiler:** Kat, Lucia's friend from prison, is there with Mattia, and she is pregnant.

## Pool, then trouble

Mattia challenges Jason to a game of pool. A few shots in, a group of Aviators starts trouble and the fight moves outside. Hold the block button (X on PS5, A on Xbox) when they swing, then hit back.

## Run for the beach

The police show up. Run with Lucia toward the beach to get away. The night ends back at Jason's house, where Lucia now lives.
