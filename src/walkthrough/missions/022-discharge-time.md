Follows straight on from [Home Sweet Home](/walkthrough/home-sweet-home/). You start as Lucia and spend most of the action as Jason.

## Find Jason

Lucia arrives at the hospital alone. From the entrance, go left and up the stairs to the second floor. Ask at reception, or follow the pink line on the floor through the door on the left. Jason is in **room 203**.

> **Spoiler:** They are not the only visitors. The same people who went after the Key Lento house turn up, along with crooked police.

## Wheelchair shootout

Lucia puts Jason in a wheelchair and pushes him out while you aim and shoot as Jason.

## Grab a shotgun

Lucia goes off to find a way out, leaving Jason to hold the corridor. Take a shotgun from one of the attackers. Every blast pushes the wheelchair backwards, so line up the next shot after each one.

## Crawl to the gun

An explosion throws Jason out of the chair. Crawl across the floor to the gun and hold on. Lucia takes care of the rest.

## Defend the ambulance

The last stretch has Jason in the back of an ambulance, shooting at the police cars chasing you. Keep it up for a few minutes until the closing cutscene.

> **Spoiler:** The two of them get away west into Kelly County and wake up in a motel the next morning. That is the start of Chapter 2.

> **Note:** Finishing Chapter 1 earns the story trophy [Paradise Lost](/trophies/#paradise-lost).
