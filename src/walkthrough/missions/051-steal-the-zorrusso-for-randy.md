The first job Randy gives you himself. You met him when Lucia dropped off Raul's Schafter in [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/); now he wants a **Zorrusso**. In the game it's called **Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS**, and it's where the **key cloner** comes from.

## The key cloner

As the mission starts, Randy has the key cloner put on your phone. It isn't an item to collect; it's there straight away. It opens and starts **keyless** high-end cars, the ones the slim jim won't work on.

## Find the Zorrusso

Drive to the apartment building in Ocean Beach marked on the map. The Zorrusso is parked there. If you're not sure which car it is, scan the cars with the WAiNK app.

## Clone the key

Walk up to the driver's door, press interact and choose **Clone Key**. Move the stick until you find the sweet spot and it clones faster. Then get in.

> **Tip:** The key cloner works on any keyless car from now on, not just this one. The [money guide](/guides/money/#key-cloner) covers which cars are worth it.

## Outrun the tracker

Expensive cars like this carry a tracker that tells the police where you are if you drive too slowly. Keep your speed up all the way to Randy and don't stop.

> **Watch out:** Slow down too much and the tracker gives you away. Wide, fast roads are your friend.

## Deliver it to Randy

Take the Zorrusso to Randy's dealership in Balsero and park at the marker. He shows you his list of other cars he wants, and the mission ends.

> **Note:** Randy's wish list is a long-term earner: he pays well for the cars on it, and finishing his strand earns the **Supply & Demand** trophy. See [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#randy). Next in the story: [Sinfrontera National Bank](/walkthrough/sinfrontera-national-bank/).
