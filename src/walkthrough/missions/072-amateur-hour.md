A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job for [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/). She heads back to **Ajax Gym** for another session and Cisco, one of the two bodybuilders, ropes her into collecting a debt. It opens once you've done the gym's **Blood Sweat and Gear** workout. Short, with one car chase.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

## Find Cisco on the Trauma Floor

As Lucia, go into Ajax Gym and up to the **Trauma Floor**. Cisco is on the stage doing deadlifts. Talk to him.

> **Spoiler:** Someone who makes adult films owes Cisco money, and he wants backup when he goes to get it. Lucia says yes because it's a way to get on his good side.

## Change at your locker

Lucia sends a text on her own; you don't need to do anything. Go to her **locker** and change your outfit.

## Head out to Cisco's car

Cisco leaves through the **back door of the Trauma Floor** while Lucia is on the phone. Follow him out to his car in the back and get in.

## Drive to the pick-up spot

The marker is only a short drive **north**. Park in the spot the game shows you.

## Wait on the bench

Cisco ducks out of sight. Sit on the bench nearby and wait for the camper van to roll up.

## Stop the Coochie Camper

When it arrives, run back to Cisco's car and give chase. Cisco starts shooting straight away and you can join in, but the quick way to end it is to put rounds into the camper's **tires**.

## Let Cisco collect

Once the camper stops, the man inside, Judd, climbs out. Hang back and let Cisco get his money.

## Back to Ajax Gym

Drive Cisco back to the **back of Ajax Gym**, where you picked the car up, and leave it in the **same parking spot**. That ends the mission and pays **$1,000**.
