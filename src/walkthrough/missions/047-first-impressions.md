A Chapter 3 story mission with both leads in the same car: [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) at the wheel, [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) riding along. It's the first job [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) gives them after [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/), and your job is the getaway.

## The garage code

The mission starts at a garage with a keypad by the door. The code is **2404**. Enter it, open the garage and get in the car.

## Pick up Raul

Drive Lucia to the meeting point on the map and wait for Raul to get in. As soon as he's in the car, the police are on you.

## The police chase

Part of the chase is played from Jason's seat, shooting at the cars behind you while Lucia drives. Then control goes back to Lucia. Keep moving and follow the route.

## Jump the bridge

The route takes you to a bridge that is partly raised. Drive at it and the game handles the jump.

## Swap cars

After the bridge, drive to the marked parking area. Leave the car there and get into the van waiting for you.

## Drop Raul in Bayside

Drive Raul to the marker in Bayside and stop. The mission only ends once he has climbed out, so wait for him.
