The last stretch of Chapter 4. After [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/vcia-takedown/) everyone is told to lie low, and for a while nothing happens. Then a message turns up at the **Y Hotel front desk**. Collecting it starts Sunset, played as [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) together.

> **Watch out:** Sunset starts a long run of story missions with no free roam, all the way through most of Chapter 5. Do the checklist above before you walk up to the front desk.

> **Tip:** To get the message sooner, return Valentina's call when your phone offers it. Otherwise you just have to wait a while in game.

## Drive to the marina

Take any of your cars to the **marina on the south side of Vice Beach**. When you arrive, walk towards the dock and the game moves you up to the houseboat.

## Find Raul

Go aboard. Inside, take the **second door on the left**, then go into the small **bathroom**. Raul is in there.

> **Spoiler:** Raul has been tortured and is barely alive. Valentina gave him up to Méndez, and he never told them where the hard drive is. Jason and Lucia end it for him before the police close in.

## Fight down the dock

The police are waiting outside. Fight your way to the **far end of the dock**, switching between Jason and Lucia so one covers the other. A **helicopter** joins in; bring it down as soon as you can and the officers on foot are much easier to deal with.

## Get to the boat

At the end of the dock, get into the boat quickly.

## Defend the boat

Your partner takes the wheel while you shoot. Once you're out of the marina, police boats and more helicopters come after you. Ammo is tight here: when the active character's rifle runs dry, the game swaps you over and the other one drives. Get clear of the area and the mission ends, going straight into [Starting From Zero](/walkthrough/starting-from-zero/).
