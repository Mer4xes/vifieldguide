After [Girls' Trip](/walkthrough/girls-trip/) you wait for Dunk to call (the game lists this as Painkiller Supplier). Doing the other open Chapter 2 missions makes the calls come sooner. After the second one, head to his trailer.

> **Tip:** A big gunfight is coming. Buy a stack of Zombix and top up your ammo before you go.

## Meet Dunk

Drive to Dunk's trailer in Port Gellhorn and follow his lead.

## The docks

Go with Dunk to the docks and drive through the gate to the office.

## Get out

The Kolts start shooting. Get back in the car, reverse out and shake off the cars that follow you.

## Dr. Bill

Pick up Dr. Bill, the man with the pills, and get him into the car.

## The airstrip

Drive to the meeting place, an airstrip southwest of Port Gellhorn.

> **Spoiler:** It's an ambush. Bill is shot as you arrive and can't be saved. Search his body afterwards for about $1,000.

## Hold out and find Dunk

Use cover and clear out every gunman, including the ones inside the buildings. Heal with Zombix when you need it. Then find Dunk in the main building.

## Take Dunk home

Drive Dunk back to his trailer. Lucia brings the painkillers to Jason at the motel to end the mission.

> **Note:** The next day Dunk pays **$1,700** into BuckMe and marks two places worth robbing on your map. His trailer is now a [fence](/guides/activities-and-collectibles/#fences) for stolen goods.
