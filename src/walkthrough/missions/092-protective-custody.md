The second-to-last mission, and the longest stretch of fighting in Chapter 5. [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) both play, and for most of it they're apart.

## Unlock it at Abe's office

Before Abe will see you, his office wants **$150,000** sent through **BuckMe**. Once it's paid, head back to the office in **Downtown Vice City**. After the scene there the game asks you to confirm, because from here the final two missions play one after the other. Pick **Postpone** if you aren't ready.

> **Note:** You won't lose anything left in free roam. When the final mission is over, the game drops you back at the point just before this one.

## Back to the penthouse

Drive back to the penthouse. Jason heads out, and Lucia is left at the **pool table** with a shot Dardan set up. It's a trick shot: bank the cue ball off the cushions **three times** before it gets there.

## Make the drop

Switch to Jason and drive to the far **south-east corner of Panos Plaza**. Sit on the marked **bench** and wait. When the drop has been picked up, walk back to **Dardan**.

> **Spoiler:** It's a set-up. Giorgio sells them out, and Jason and Lucia are taken into custody separately. A state prosecutor tells Lucia the only way out is the hard drive in Giorgio's hands.

## Lucia breaks free

After the scenes, Jason is walked to a cell at the police station. Then you're Lucia, cuffed in the back of a transport van. Hold **L2 + R2** (LT + RT) and tap **X** (A) to break free, then get the cuffs off.

Be quick when the door opens: shoot the two men there, grab a gun one of them drops, deal with the rest and **run until you've lost them**. Lucia then gets a bike; follow the **highway south** towards Jason.

## Out of the precinct

Back to Jason. An officer lets him out of the cell. Follow her down the hallway to the **left**, into the room on the left. Close the door and push a **locker** in front of it.

Head up the stairs; Jason gets a gun on the way. Go **east**, checking the side rooms and the stairwells as you pass. He only has one magazine, so take ammo from everyone you drop. In the far room, run for the **railing** and go over it.

## Hold the armory

Turn back and follow the officer a short way down the hallway to the room on the **right**: the armory. Use the **long guns** there on the enemies in the street and in the buildings across from you. More guns and **body armor** are at the locker Jason used in the cutscene.

> **Tip:** Stock up properly here. The fights from now on come one after the other with no break.

## The warehouse

Clear the street, then cross to the **warehouse in the alley**. More enemies come at street level, so keep moving towards the open door.

> **Spoiler:** The officer who got Jason out is shot as she opens the warehouse door.

Turn on whoever follows you in straight away. Then hold the door through the **NOOSE assault**: vehicle after vehicle of them, until the game moves you on.

## Lucia crosses Hamlet

You switch to Lucia at the edge of **Hamlet**, and she has to reach Jason. Patrol cars drive the streets looking for her. Cut through the **back yards** and watch for enemies stepping out from behind houses. Being seen doesn't fail the mission, it just sends them after you, so you can also sprint the whole way and leave them behind.

## The task force

Outside the warehouse, kill every marked enemy. Then **Méndez** climbs out through the roof of his **truck**. Stay behind cover while he's firing and hit him when he stops. When he's hurt enough he drops back inside and more enemies arrive; take them out and he comes back up.

> **Tip:** The **trunks** of the cars around the area hold weapons, body armor and health items.

After enough damage there's a scene, then you finish him off and the mission ends. [The End of the Road](/walkthrough/the-end-of-the-road/), the final mission, follows straight on.
