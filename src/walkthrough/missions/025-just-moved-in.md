Opens after [What's in the Bag?](/walkthrough/whats-in-the-bag/). An easy one, and the store is right across the street.

## Go to the Alpha store

Walk out of the motel and cross over to the Alpha convenience store at the gas station opposite.

## Shop the list

Eight things are on the list. They are on different shelves, so walk the aisles:

- a soda
- protein bars
- fruit
- soup
- gauze
- bandanas
- hair clippers
- a makeup or toiletry bag

> **Tip:** Each item is paid for the moment you pick it up. You don't need to go to the till or talk to the clerk.

## Back to the room

Carry the shopping back to the motel room. The mission ends when you get there.
