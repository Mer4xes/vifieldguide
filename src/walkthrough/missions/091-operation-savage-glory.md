The biggest job of [Chapter 5](/walkthrough/#chapter-5). It starts by itself at the penthouse once [Doggie Bag](/walkthrough/doggie-bag/) and [VC Baby](/walkthrough/vc-baby/) are both done. [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) and [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) take turns, and the game tells you when to switch.

> **Note:** Rocco, the sniffer dog you brought back in [Doggie Bag](/walkthrough/doggie-bag/), does the searching on the cargo ship. The plan is the one you scouted in [Bird in the Hand](/walkthrough/bird-in-the-hand/).

## Meet Vinny upstairs

Go up to the top floor of the penthouse, where **Vinny** is waiting. After the briefing the crew split up, and you start as Lucia.

## Rig the bridge pillar

Lucia takes the **boat** out to the bridge. Pull up beside the **pier** and step off onto it.

Interact with the side of the pillar. Slide the charge across the wall until the light on its back turns **bright green**, then press **R2 / RT** followed by **X / A** to set it. Go round to the other side of the pillar and do the same again.

## Clear the cargo ship from the air

The game switches you to Jason, flying out to a **cargo ship** with a rival shipment on board. He has a **sniper rifle with unlimited ammo**, so take out everyone on deck before you land.

> **Tip:** The **lights on their guns** give the enemies away. As you get closer, Kevin turns the helicopter's searchlight on them too.

## Follow Rocco through the ship

Once you're down, stay with Rocco and fight off whoever shows up. Kevin calls out enemies he spots before you do. Each time Rocco stops, there's a container to deal with:

1. The first is on the **upper level**. Open it with the [Cut-Off Tool](/guides/equipment/#cut-off-tool), go in, and set the explosives on the **crate at the back**.
2. Back outside, an enemy **helicopter** comes along the left side of the ship. Shoot it down straight away.
3. Halfway up the ship, Rocco stops by **two more containers**, one after the other. Plant a charge on the side of each.
4. The last container has to be opened. Throw the remaining explosives inside.

## Get off the ship

Now hold out on deck with Rocco until the helicopter comes back. Expect plenty of shooters in the **windows** further along the ship. When the helicopter arrives on the **left**, get in fast. Once you're clear, press **D-pad right** to set off the charges and sink the shipment.

## Raise the bridge

Back to Lucia. Go into the **bridge control building** on the right and deal with the operator upstairs. Use the controls to **raise the bridge**, then put an explosive on the control panel and blow it so nobody can lower it again.

## Into the water

Lucia puts on a **scuba mask**. Jump off the bridge and follow Vinny under the water. At the boat, he hands you explosives: press **R2 / RT** to fix them to the **underside**, then keep following him.

At the **grate**, interact with it. Line the drill up with a bolt, press **L2 / LT**, then hold **R2 / RT** to unscrew it. Do every bolt.

## Sneak through the coast guard base

When you come out on land, go round the building on the **right** and keep to the right side, away from the buildings, as you follow the road to the far end.

The guards there stand in **pairs**. Shoot one of each pair while Vinny takes the other at the same moment, then slip quietly through the doors. Inside, there are more guards between you and the **big garage**, and two more in the garage itself. Then use the **shutter** to open the door.

## Cover from the helicopter

The game hands you Jason, who heads inland to the **National Coast Guard base**. Snipe from the side of the helicopter while Kevin lights up targets, as on the ship.

## Defend the cargo

Back on the ground as Lucia, hold off the attackers while the **truck** drags the cargo outside, then follow it and deal with the next wave.

When the prompt comes up, switch to Jason. Shoot the man on the **roof with the RPG** before he hits the helicopter. You can also bring down the helicopters dropping troops in, and pick off enemies on the walkways and rooftops.

Switch back to Lucia when told, fight your way to the cargo and attach the **hook**.

## Ride the cargo net

Lucia climbs onto the net as the helicopter lifts off. Move the **left stick** left and right to swing round the cargo for cover or a better angle. When prompted, press **D-pad right** to blow up the **boats** you mined earlier.

Jason drops Lucia a **grenade launcher**, with **unlimited ammo** for this part. Use it on the vehicles chasing you, and on the helicopters that follow you through the city at about your height.

> **Watch out:** Kevin warns you twice. The first time a **tower** is coming, the second time a **train**. Both times, swing round to the **right side** of the net, or the cargo smashes into you.

## Back to the penthouse

You end up back at the penthouse with everything you had before Chapter 5 returned. Leave through one of the **elevators** to finish the mission and unlock **Snow Storm**.

> **Spoiler:** The crew pull it off, but it costs them: Vinny and Liz don't make it back. Giorgio gets the senator to help call off the police search, and tells Jason and Lucia to lie low in the penthouse and go and see Abe about their pardon.

> **Note:** The next step starts as soon as you walk out of the penthouse: a short visit to **Abe's office** in Downtown that is only a cutscene. After it, you're free to roam again: the map lists a main mission with no marker, Unfinished Business, which is simply the game giving you time to tie up loose ends. Abe then wants **$150,000** before [Protective Custody](/walkthrough/protective-custody/#before-you-start) opens, so this is the time to save up, and to move what you want to keep into Family Savings.
