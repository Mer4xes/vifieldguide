A short mission that introduces shops and your first proper weapon. Nothing in it can be missed.

## Go to Ammu-Nation

Jason's car is parked at the house. Drive to the Ammu-Nation marked on the map, close to home.

## Buy a long gun

Talk to the clerk and pick a long gun from the counter. Choose one you can afford with the cash you have; the mission continues either way.

## Deal with the welcome party

When you walk out, three Kingfishers jump out of a truck and come for Jason. Fight them off. A phone call afterwards wraps up the mission.

> **Spoiler:** The Kingfishers are after both Jason and Donnie after what happened at the camp.
