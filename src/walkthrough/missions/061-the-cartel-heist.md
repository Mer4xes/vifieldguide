The job that ends Chapter 3. [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) briefs the crew at the planning room in Stockyard once [Hidden Charges](/walkthrough/hidden-charges/) and every other Chapter 3 mission are done. The bank is Sinfrontera National, where the cartel La Polilla Negra keeps its money, and both [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) are in it, with the game switching between them.

> **Note:** How well the prep went shows up here. Crates kept in [Company Property](/walkthrough/company-property/) became charges in Hidden Charges, and every charge is extra time in the bank. The safe cracking is the one you practised in [Safe Word](/walkthrough/safe-word/).

## Set off the charges

Get into the black car with Lucia and Nicole and drive downtown towards the towers you rigged. Park in the marked spot by the road and press the prompt to blow the charges. With all three towers down, you have just under 11 minutes before the police can get to the bank. Then drive south to the bank.

## Take the lobby

Walk in and wait for Karim's signal. Knock out the marked guard, then threaten each marked customer and member of staff until they move to the middle of the room. Do the side rooms too.

> **Tip:** Karim calls out anyone heading for the door. Deal with them first. If someone pulls a gun, shoot it out of their hand instead of killing them: that keeps your **Professional** rating.

## Down to the vault floor

The game switches you to Lucia. Take the open lift down with Raul and Nicole. Threaten Lex until he walks into the security room, then keep the pressure on the guard there until he opens the door. When he moves to the wall, knock him out.

## Clear the roof

Back to Jason. La Polilla Negra security comes at you from the lifts and from the floor above. Clear them, then take the lift up to the roof. The guards there are watching the helicopter, so you get the first shots in. Climb the ladder to the top level, clear it and secure the backup terminal.

## Crack the vault

Lucia again, at the vault door. It's the [Safe Word](/walkthrough/safe-word/#step-10) routine with two changes:

1. **Drill.** Both drill bars move this time, so keep correcting both of them.
2. **Cable.** Left stick moves the cable, right stick turns the camera. The path takes a hard right turn; follow it to the gold hook at the end.
3. **Hold the door.** Hold L2 + R2 (LT + RT) and tap X / A while Nicole drills through.

## Loot the vault

Grab the big stacks of cash first. They're worth far more than the loose notes, and the clock is still running.

## Hold the bank

The police arrive. As Jason, hold the second floor while SWAT breaks in. Swap the shotgun for something with more range. When the game moves you to Lucia, cover the left side of the building.

When you're told to, regroup with Raul in the middle of the room. A SWAT truck reverses into the lobby and throws a flashbang: stay in cover until you can see again, then finish off whoever is left.

## Smoke out the street

Head for the back of the SWAT truck. Lucia climbs in and passes Jason a smoke grenade launcher. Fire smoke at the police cars lined up in front of the bank, and Lucia makes a run for the bus.

## Get Nicole out

As Jason, shoot the police rushing the front, then help Nicole to her feet. Follow her through the door they came in by, turn right to the stairs and head down to the basement.

> **Watch out:** There's a flashbang waiting on the stairwell landing.

Keep going down. Take the left-hand door when Nicole points it out to reach the delivery bay, then follow the driveway out. The Brigade, a SWAT vehicle, is at the top of the ramp: get in and drive off once Nicole is aboard.

## Defend the bus

Lucia is on the bus. Shoot the chasing cars through the back, and listen to Raul: he tells you when one is coming up on either side. After a truck rips the roof off the bus, Raul hands you a grenade launcher with unlimited ammo.

A helicopter follows. Fire a grenade at it when it hangs right behind the bus, or switch weapons and use focus mode (L3 + R3) to shoot the pilot.

## Catch the bus

Jason, in the armoured truck, has to catch up. Ram police cars out of the way, pull up along the left side of the bus and jump across. Then drive the bus along the freeway, keeping left when Raul tells you to.

After the final cutscene you can carry on in free roam or go straight to **Ocean Beach**, the first mission of Chapter 4. The heist pays **$120,000** and earns the [Break the Bank](/trophies/#break-the-bank) trophy.

> **Note:** This is the last mission of Chapter 3. The chapter's story trophy is [Vice City, Baby!](/trophies/#vice-city-baby).
