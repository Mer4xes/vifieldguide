Brian's first rent run. You can do this before or after **Just a Scratch**.

## Go to the shop

Drive to the marker. The shop out front sells beach odds and ends; the real business is in the back.

## Ask for the rent

Talk to the clerk. He can't, or won't, pay. Lean on him until he gives in.

> **Tip:** While you are in the shop, you can empty the cash register too. It is optional.

## Open the back room

The back room has a keypad. The code is **0420**.

## Take the goods and call Brian

Grab what is inside, then phone Brian to tell him how the rent is being paid.

> **Spoiler:** The tenant pays in acid instead of cash.

## Drop it at the lockup

Brian sends you to his lockup further south. Open it with code **7700** and leave the goods inside. The lockup works as a fence from now on, somewhere you can sell drugs.
