## Cast off

Meet Brian at the boat yard and take the skiff at the end of the dock. This is the boat handling tutorial: ease the throttle and steer wide.

## The pickup

Follow the marker out past the reef. Stop beside the buoy and hold the prompt to haul the cargo aboard.

> **Watch out:** The reef is shallow on the direct line. Keep the channel markers on your left going out.

## Home run

Head back to the yard. A patrol boat crosses your route: cut the engine and drift until it passes, then dock to finish.
