The second mission of Chapter 3, played as Jason. It starts back at the Jack of Hearts club in Crosstown. Short, with one choice that only changes how the deal plays out.

> **Note:** Whether the Pure route pays $1,500 is still unclear: the walkthroughs we checked contradict themselves on it. We'll add the amount once it's confirmed.

## Up to the studio

Go into the Jack of Hearts and take the stairs all the way up. The door at the top leads into the studio. Dre'Quan is in the recording booth; wait for him to finish. He asks which hook you liked, and any answer is fine. Then go upstairs with him.

## Pick up from Tyrone

Ride in Dre'Quan's car to an apartment block nearby and follow him to a room on the second floor. Tyrone and Dre'Quan start talking; you can cut in if you don't want to wait.

## Cut or Pure

Tyrone gives you a choice of product. See the choices box above for what each one leads to.

## Football in the park

On the way back to the car, Dre'Quan takes a detour through a park. A quick tutorial teaches you to throw a football: pass it back to the kids.

## The deal

Drive a short way south to the meeting spot near the Jack of Hearts. Follow Dre'Quan to the group by the cars and hand the bag to the buyer when the prompt shows. Checking the product takes a while, and you can tell them to hurry up.

> **Tip:** Keep it calm. If you finish the handover without any violence, you get the **Professional** bonus when you collect the money.

## Collect and leave

When they are done, take the backpack back with the money inside and follow Dre'Quan to the car.

## The man with the bag

While Dre'Quan is busy shouting at someone in a building nearby, a man walks up and says the bag belongs to him. He attacks whatever you say. Knock him out with your fists, then get in the car.

## Drive to Southside

Drive Dre'Quan to a shop in Southside. A cutscene ends the mission about halfway there.
