A text after **Island Dreams** marks Jason's house. Go inside to start. It is a relaxed one with no way to fail.

## Beer and the couch

Take a beer from the fridge and sit down on the couch with Cal.

## Rearrange the room

Move the TV, coffee table, couch and chair with Cal.

> **Tip:** When it comes to the coffee table, accept the first spot. Asking to move it again just puts it back where it was.

## Tenebrae Lux

A black-and-white first-person game. You can't win it: head for the door on the right and climb the slope until Jason gets bored and it ends.

## Street Trash III

A top-down shooter on a timer. You can't win this one either. Play until the timer runs out, or die straight away to skip it.

> **Spoiler:** The next morning the prison calls about Lucia's release, which leads straight into **Coming Home**.

> **Note:** Afterwards Cal texts Jason a fishing dare in Gambit Bay. It's the one that earns **Baited My Tackle**; see [Cal's fishing challenge](/guides/side-contacts/#cal).
