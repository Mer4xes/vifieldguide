A short clean-up job at Donnie's trailer in Key Lento. The only thing that matters long term is the trunk.

## Get the bag

Go into Donnie's trailer and pick up his bag.

## Pack his things

There are six items to collect around the trailer. Use focus mode (L3 and R3 together) to highlight what you still need.

## Trouble outside

Three men are waiting for you when you step out. Fight them, or get to the car and drive off.

## Check the trunk

Before anything happens to the car, open the trunk and take **Donnie's H&L .38**.

> **Missable:** The revolver is only in this car. Once the car is destroyed, you can't get it. The other unique weapons, and where they are, are in the [unique weapons guide](/guides/unique-weapons/).

## Lose the car

Get rid of Donnie's car to finish the mission.
