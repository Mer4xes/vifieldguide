Chapter 2 starts the morning after [Discharge Time](/walkthrough/discharge-time/), in a motel room in Kelly County. It only takes a few minutes, and there is nothing to miss.

## Open the bag

You play as Lucia. Take out the first item and turn it over to find the note underneath, then read it.

> **Spoiler:** The first item is a small statue of a woman, with a note tucked under its base.

## The second item

Pick up the other thing in the bag and look it over. The mission ends here.

> **Spoiler:** It is a hard drive.

## Free roam as Lucia

From here you can move around Kelly County freely as Lucia. Jason is laid up in the motel room for now, so he isn't playable for a while.

> **Note:** The [Paradise Lost](/trophies/#paradise-lost) trophy for finishing Chapter 1 pops around here.
