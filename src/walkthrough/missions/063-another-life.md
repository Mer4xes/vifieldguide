This one starts the moment [Ocean Beach](/walkthrough/ocean-beach/) ends, and you play it as [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/). There's nothing to fight and nothing to fail: each objective is one interaction, and the prompts tell you where to go.

> **Spoiler:** It's a flashback to Lucia's old home, on the last morning before the arrest you saw in the [Prologue](/walkthrough/prologue/).

## Answer the phone

Pick up the phone lying on the table.

## The room at the end of the hall

Walk down the hallway to the room at the far end and interact with what's inside.

> **Spoiler:** It's Lucia's baby, in the crib. Pick the baby up.

## Back to the living room

Go back to the living room and use the marked items there.

## Turn up the radio

When the prompt appears, use the radio on the kitchen counter.

## Dance

Press Triangle / Y to dance round the living room. A few more scenes play out and the mission ends. The **Vice City, Baby!** trophy pops here.

## Straight after: Pool Day

> **Note:** What follows is a short scene that isn't marked on the map as a mission. You're back in the present, as [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/), in the Y Hotel suite.

Go out to the balcony to find Lucia (if you head for the door instead, she calls you back) and agree to go down to the pool. Once you're settled at the cabana, walk past the pool on the right to the bar and order Lucia a **Tequila Sunrise**.

On the way back a man called **Eddie Acosta** stops you and invites you to a party. Say yes or no: his party is marked on the map for later either way. See [Eddie's one job](/guides/side-contacts/#eddie).

Hand Lucia her drink, then sit and people-watch with her: look at the man on the flamingo float, the men on the left and the woman in the big hat by the edge of the pool when she points them out. When she gets up, follow her down to the beach. Valentina calls, and the next two missions appear on the map.

Next in the story is [Company Cars](/walkthrough/company-cars/). [Lawyer Up](/walkthrough/lawyer-up/) opens at the same time.
