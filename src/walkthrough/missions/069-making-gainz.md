A couple of in-game days after [Heavy Lifting](/walkthrough/heavy-lifting/), [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) gets in touch with [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/). Go to the planning room in Stockyard with Lucia on her own. A scene takes you straight to Ajax Gym. There's no fighting, and the only thing to think about is the choice.

## Find Nate

Go upstairs to the main workout area. Nate is over by the weight benches.

## Get his attention

Talk to him, and keep talking. It takes a few goes before he pays Lucia any notice.

## Train with Nate

Use the bench press he's standing behind. Then follow him to the squat rack and do that set, and follow him outside to the deadlift area for one more.

## Use the exercise bike

Nate heads off with Kordell. Walk round to the far side of the pool and get on the free exercise bike.

## The workout montage

A long montage follows. Keep doing each workout and interaction as it comes up. It cuts between Lucia and Nate in the gym and scenes with [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/).

> **Note:** During the montage Nate offers Lucia steroids. No is selected unless you change it. Yes changes how Lucia looks for the rest of the game, so only pick it if that's what you want.

## Leave the gym

At the end Nate shows Lucia the Trauma Floor, Kordell's private workout area. Then head out of the gym.

## Meet Jason

Jason is waiting just north of the gym, round the side of the building.

## Drive to the planning room

The game switches you to Jason. Drive back to the planning room, use the intercom to get in, and the mission ends after the scenes inside.

> **Watch out:** From now until [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) is done, Jason and Lucia can't be together in public. Their room at the Y Hotel is the exception.
