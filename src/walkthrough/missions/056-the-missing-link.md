Part of the build-up to **The Cartel Heist**. [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/)'s crew needs something only a man called Monty can give them, and Andrea knows where to find him. [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) goes in with her.

> **Note:** Story-order lists disagree on whether this or [Safe Word](/walkthrough/safe-word/) comes first. They're separate jobs, so play them in whichever order the game offers.

## Meet Andrea

Go to Andrea's marker. She takes Lucia to a cockfight, which is where Monty can be found.

## Through the crowd

Follow Andrea past the cockfights to the room at the back where Monty is waiting.

## The photo shoot

Monty wants pictures. Play along and follow the on-screen prompts when they show up.

> **Spoiler:** The shoot is cover. Andrea isn't leaving without Monty, and before long he's out cold.

## Cover Andrea

Monty's men don't let him go quietly. Stay with Andrea and keep the attackers off her while she gets him out to the car.

## Drive Monty away

Get in the car with Monty and drive to the marked site in Vice City.

## Get him talking

At the pool, take over the machine when the game tells you to. Lower Monty towards the water, wait for his answer, then raise him again. Repeat until he gives up what the crew needs.

> **Tip:** Wait for each prompt to come up before you press anything. Monty needs time to answer between goes.

## Deal with Monty

Once he's said enough, the last objective is to deal with Monty. The mission ends after that.
