The first step of **The Cartel Heist**, the big job that ends Chapter 3. [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) wants eyes inside the bank before he plans anything, so [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) go in as ordinary customers. Nothing gets robbed today.

## Go to the bank

Head to Sinfrontera National Bank in downtown Vice City, marked on the map. Jason and Lucia go in together.

## Act like a customer

Walk in calmly. You're here to look, not to start anything, so don't draw a weapon or run.

## Look over the security

Check out what Raul needs to know about, following the prompts:

- **The guards:** armed guards on the floor.
- **The cameras:** 105 of them.
- **The vault elevator:** the elevator down to the vault floor has a biometric reader.

> **Watch out:** Leave the biometric reader alone. Tampering with it sets off the alarm and blows the visit.

> **Spoiler:** This is no ordinary bank. It's where a cartel keeps and cleans its money, which is why the security is so heavy.

## Join Raul

When you've seen enough, follow the marker to Raul inside the bank. The mission wraps up after the scene with him.

> **Spoiler:** Raul came to meet Nicole Park, the security expert who designed the vault. She doesn't turn up, and getting hold of her becomes the next job.
