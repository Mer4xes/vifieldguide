Dre'Quan calls [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) after [Making Gainz](/walkthrough/making-gainz/). He has a meeting lined up that could make the [Real Dimez](/characters/real-dimez/), and he wants to look the part. There's just one problem with the watch he bought for it.

## Meet up with Dre'Quan

Go to the marked store on **Washington Beach** in Vice Beach. Head inside and **upstairs**, then wait while [Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/) has it out with the clerk.

> **Note:** The watch turns out to be a fake. Dre'Quan wants his money back, or a real one.

## Drive to the Cruda parking lot

Follow him back out to the car. The men who sold him the watch are at the **Cruda parking lot** to the south. Drive in through the entrance and keep going up to the **top floor**, then park next to the marked car.

## Back Dre'Quan up

Let Dre'Quan do the talking with the men in the car. It doesn't go his way: they get out swinging, so help him put them down with your fists.

> **Watch out:** As soon as the fistfight is over, another car pulls up and the gunmen inside start shooting straight away. Get to cover and drop them, then keep an eye on the **stairs**, where more come up.

## Escape the area

With the roof clear, get out before the police close in. There's no timer on this part.

## Pick an outfit

Dre'Quan wants you both dressed properly for the meeting. Drive to the marked clothing store near the **south end of Ocean Beach**. Two outfits are set out on mannequins: pick either one. Dre'Quan pays for yours and takes the other.

## Go to the hotel

Head **north** to the marked hotel, where the club owner is waiting.

## Follow Dre'Quan

Stick with Dre'Quan as he makes his way through the hotel and out the back. The mission ends once he's done talking.

> **Spoiler:** The man you meet is Royce Jones, who runs the VYB nightclub and decides who gets booked in Vice City. He'll give the Dimez a headline spot, but only if Jason and Dre'Quan get rid of the dirt Dante Fresh is holding over him. That job is [Fresh Evidence](/walkthrough/fresh-evidence/).
