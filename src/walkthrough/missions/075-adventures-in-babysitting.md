A night shift for [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) as bodyguard to Andrés. It opens after [Making Gainz](/walkthrough/making-gainz/): Jason gets a call to come back, and the mission starts at Andrés's house on **Starfish Island**. The first half is errands around a nightclub; the second half is one long fight.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

## Meet Andrés and drive to The Vault

Follow Paulo round the back of the house to Andrés, then drive the two of you to **The Vault**.

## Get him inside

Stay with Andrés as he walks in. A drunk tries to hold him up at the door; you can step in to keep him moving.

## Fetch his drink

On the first dance floor, go to the **bartender on the left**. Andrés wants his drink **without lime**. You can order it his way or not; it makes no difference.

Hand it to him at the corner by the stairs, take the **photo** he asks for on his phone, then follow him upstairs.

## Bring the group over

Talk to the **group of girls on the right**, then walk them back to Andrés's booth. Check the order with the bartender close by, come back and take the **empty seat** on the couch.

## Talk to Donatello's security

Go over to the guard at the other booth, have a word, then return to Andrés.

## The drink at the table

Andrés offers Jason drugs. Say yes or no, it's up to you, but you have to **take the drink** he hands you. Then follow him to the DJ booth, into the office and back to the DJ booth, push your way out and sit back down at the table.

## Find him in the bathroom

Andrés wanders off. He's in the bathroom on the **far side of the floor**, in the **last stall**. Interact with the door.

## Make the pickup

Head down the main stairs and out of the **bottom door**. The dealer is in a car at the **end of the alley on the right**. Talk to him, then go back inside. Andrés is at the bar in the **southeast corner**.

## Protect Andrés

Whatever you said earlier, Jason has been dosed, and the screen stays warped until you're out of the club. Armed men storm the bar. Grab weapons off the men you drop so you're not stuck with a handgun.

> **Watch out:** At the bottom of the stairs more of them pull up in a vehicle. Go out through the **right-hand door**.

> **Watch out:** One of them tackles Jason and goes for him with a knife. Mash **Square / X** to fight him off.

## Cover the getaway

Outside, jump in the car with the rest of the security team. You get an **LMG** to shoot back at the cars chasing you. Jason takes a lot of damage while he's leaning out of the roof, so watch your health.

> **Tip:** Try not to hit people running from the shooting. It counts against your Criminal Profile.

## Follow Paulo to the roof

Follow Paulo up the escalator and through the building. At the **train tracks**, interact with Andrés to help him over. Take the elevator up and clear the enemies waiting on the **helipad**.

## Get Andrés on board

Interact with Andrés to push him into the helicopter.

## Shoot down the helicopters

You're handed a rifle with **unlimited ammo**. Go for the **tail rotors** of the enemy helicopters; it brings them down faster than aiming for the pilot or the gunners. Press **Right on the d-pad** when the game tells you to switch sides.

For the last part you swap to a **rocket launcher**. Hold your aim on a helicopter until the lock-on square **turns red**, then fire. Once the air is clear the mission ends.

> **Spoiler:** The attackers are FAP, and they were after Andrés himself. Back at his house the news is worse: his son, Rafa, has been taken.
