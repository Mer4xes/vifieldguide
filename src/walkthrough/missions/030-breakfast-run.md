Jason calls Lucia some time after [Slam Dunk](/walkthrough/slam-dunk/), roughly half an in-game day later. Playing other open missions in the meantime can bring the call on sooner.

## Hank's Waffles

Meet Jason at Hank's Waffles.

## Rob the diner

Aim at the staff and hold the threaten button to scare them into handing over the cash. Each robbery runs on a **one-minute timer**, shown at the bottom right.

## Rob the liquor store

Walk out and the game takes you straight into the next store. Same routine.

## Rob the thrift store

One more, the same way.

## Back to the car

Get in the car. The rest of the spree plays out in a cutscene.

## Lose the cops

You leave with two wanted stars. The small dirt roads off the main road are the easiest way to shake them.

> **Note:** Finishing the mission earns the story trophy [Trust](/trophies/#trust).
