Donnie calls with a job: a drop he was expecting is not where it should be. This one ends in a gunfight you can't sneak past.

## Find the package

Go to the marker by the river. The package is gone. Look around at the water's edge and examine what is left of it.

## Down the river

Take the boat that is waiting there and follow the river to a shack.

## Clear the shack

The Kingfishers at the shack turn hostile when you arrive, so be ready. Open fire early: the boat gives you very little cover and takes damage fast.

## Grab the package

Go inside the shack and pick up the package. More Kingfishers turn up by boat; take them out before you leave.

## Drop it at Donnie's

Take the package to Donnie's trailer in Key Lento and leave it in the box behind it.

> **Tip:** The drop behind Donnie's trailer stays open after this mission as a fence. Bring narcotics you find there to sell them. It is one of the better early ways to make money.
