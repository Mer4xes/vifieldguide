[Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/) has a score to settle with DWNPLY, and he has a plan that needs [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/). It's a light mission: some talking, one bit of sleight of hand and a car chase at the end.

## Head to the Eris store

Meet Dre'Quan and drive to the Eris store, where DWNPLY is signing his new sneakers. The idea is simple: show up as a fan with a pair of shoes and get close to him.

## Get hold of a pair

The sneakers sold out long before you arrived, so you'll have to get a pair from someone who already bought one. Look for a shopper with a pair and talk to them. They want **$1,580**.

> **Tip:** You don't have to pay the full price. Haggling brings it down, and leaning on the seller is another option.

## Up to the VIP lounge

Go back to Dre'Quan with the shoes and follow him to the VIP lounge where DWNPLY is holding court.

## The fan act

Walk up to DWNPLY and hand him the shoes like any other fan. While he's busy with them, the objective changes: take his car keys.

## Out to the parking lot

Get out of the lounge with Dre'Quan and head down to the parking lot. Search the lot for DWNPLY's car and get in.

## Lose the chase

DWNPLY's people come after you as soon as you pull out. Keep driving and shake them off.

## Stockyard Speed Shop

Drive the car to Stockyard Speed Shop. The mission ends there.

> **Spoiler:** Dre'Quan isn't stealing the car for the money. He wants it for a music video, and you'll see it again in Cooties.
