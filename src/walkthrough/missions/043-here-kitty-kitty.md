Jason only, straight after [Tyrone](/walkthrough/tyrone/). Tyrone wants a man called Charles found. There is one choice on the way, and it only changes how you get the address.

## Charles's apartment

Drive to the apartment marked on the map. Charles isn't home. You have two ways to find out where he went:

- **Pay a neighbour $20** for a tip.
- **Go into the empty flat** and search it. A clue inside tells you where to look, and it costs nothing.

## The camp in La Perle

Both routes send you to a camp in La Perle. Go in and find Charles.

> **Spoiler:** Charles is in a bad way, out of his head on Kitty Litter.

## Tyrone opens for business

From now on Tyrone buys stolen goods, so he becomes one of your [fences](/guides/fences/).

> **Note:** Tyrone's optional jobs on the Kitty Litter labs open around here; see [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#tyrone). The next story mission is [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/).
