Starts with a text and a photo from Moses some time after [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/).

## Find the Crawler

Go to the search area by the old Trader's Union building in Port Gellhorn. The car is a white '82 Crawler, a jeep. Armed men hang around the area: you can fight them, or just get to the jeep and drive off.

## Bring it to Moses

Drive back to Moses's garage. The base pay is **$1,250**, with **$500** extra if the jeep arrives without damage, so take it easy on the way.

> **Note:** Soon after, Moses gives Lucia a garage in Port Gellhorn for free, and the Vice City Real Estate app opens so you can buy more garages. See [every garage and its price](/guides/garages/).
