Starts on its own straight after [Checking Out](/walkthrough/checking-out/), while you are on the highway into Vice City. The Chapter 2 trophy, [T\*\*s Ass Drug Money](/trophies/#t-s-ass-drug-money), comes with it.

## Head for the club

A little way down the road, Jason calls Dre'Quan, who tells you to meet him at the Jack of Hearts in Crosstown. Drive there.

## The front door

The car pulls up out front by itself. Walk up to the bouncer right in front of you.

## Round the back

Go round the building to the other entrance. Dre'Quan comes out to meet you there; follow him through the club to the stairs.

## Money for the dancers

Dre'Quan hands you a stack of cash to throw at the dancers. It is up to you. If you take it, walk up to a dancer, hold L2/LT to aim and press R1/RB to throw. Keep going until it is all gone.

## Upstairs

Go back to Dre'Quan at the stairs and follow him up to meet his boss.

> **Spoiler:** The boss is Boobie Ike, who owns the club. Dre'Quan runs a record label out of it. Boobie offers Jason and Lucia an apartment of his nearby.

## Drive to the apartment

When the talking is done, go back to the car and drive to the apartment a short way off.

## Park up

Pull into the covered parking spot. In this chapter, cars you own are kept here.

## Move in

Walk round to the front. The door across from the parking spots can be used later too. Go up to the second floor; the apartment is the first door you see. Go in to finish.

> **Tip:** After the mission Moses texts you: Pay 'n' Spray garages now let you buy and register cars that have Standard Trackers.
