[Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/)'s launch night for the [Real Dimez](/characters/real-dimez/), and he wants both [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) on the door. It opens once you've done the other Chapter 4 setup missions for [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/vcia-takedown/). Meet Dre'Quan by the entrance of **Club VYB** in **Washington Beach**. The early part is party management; the end is a long brawl.

> **Note:** What this mission pays depends on who you let in. The picks below are a strong, well-paying set.

## Meet Dre'Quan at the club

Find Dre'Quan near the front entrance. When he gets up, follow him on a lap of the club.

## Test the lights

Lucia goes up to the **DJ booth**. Play with the lights and effects as much as you like, then leave with **O / B**.

## Pick the VIP tables

Jason has to choose **three** guests. Each shows an **Offer**, money you get up front, and **Followers**, which decides how much the night makes later. The strongest trio:

- **Donatello Lambrini**, who has the most followers.
- **The Game Developer**, who makes the biggest offer: **$50,000** up front.
- **KD**, next in followers, brings company and has done Dre'Quan favours before.

Hold **O / B** to confirm.

## Look after Maleek Rivers

Back as Lucia, go to the marked booth. Maleek Rivers wants the lights changed so they're on him. Change them for him.

## Check on KD

If you let KD in, Dre'Quan sends you over to him. Say yes when he asks for a photo and take a picture of him at his table.

## Meet the cars

As Jason, in the lobby, a man claims he was already inside. Throw him out or let it go, your call. Then go out of the front door to Dre'Quan. When the cars pull up, open the door of the one on **Dre'Quan's right**.

## Escort Sexxy Red

Follow Dre'Quan back in as he leads the new guests to a table. Walk the group down the stairs to the **lower dance floor** and on into the VIP area.

## Free up a table

Dre'Quan needs one of your three VIP groups moved to make space. Pick the **Game Developers**: they've already paid their $50,000, they don't add much in followers and they're happy to take the "upgrade".

## Grab DWNPLY

In the green room, Lucia follows Jason out onto the **balcony** above the dance floor. Drop down to the lower level, go to the **front right** of the stage, right under DWNPLY, and press **R2 / RT** to drag him off.

When his people grab Lucia, mash **Square / X** to break free, then fight off the crew members who come at you.

> **Spoiler:** DWNPLY has taken over the night and is holding the Real Dimez in the green room.

## Beat the big guy

Upstairs, Jason faces the hardest fist fight in the game. The man wears **brass knuckles**, so focus on **dodging** at first. Later on, pick up the **knife from the bar counter** and use it on him. The fight ends once Jason pulls off one of his brass knuckles, which you keep.

## Finish DWNPLY

As Lucia in the green room, hit DWNPLY quickly to take his gun off him and knock him out.

## Enjoy the show

Jason and Lucia end up on the dance floor. Dance together or just watch the Dimez play. Dre'Quan then asks if you want to stay or head off; answer either way and a last scene with him ends the mission. **DWNFALL** pops when it's over.

> **Spoiler:** Dre'Quan tells them he's going on tour with the Real Dimez and thanks the pair for the night.
