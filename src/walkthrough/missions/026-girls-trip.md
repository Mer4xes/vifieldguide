Opens once [Just Moved In](/walkthrough/just-moved-in/) and the short Overnight Charges talk with the motel manager are both done.

## Ask around

Go to the motel office and ask Zach about painkillers. He points you to Vicki in **room 10**.

## Buy from Vicki

Knock on room 10 and pay Vicki **$50** for the pills.

## Not what you paid for

Take the pills back to Jason. They turn out to be Zombix, the over-the-counter stuff, so head back to Vicki and have it out with her. She offers to take you to someone who can get the real thing.

## To Port Gellhorn

Drive Vicki's car to Port Gellhorn and stop at the club where she works. Wait while she collects her pay.

## Find Dunk

The man you need is Dunk, and he isn't where Vicki expects. Follow the markers: he is not at his trailer or the boatyard. You find him at the car wash.

## Trouble at the car wash

Dunk is in trouble with the Kolts, a local gang, and they have him cornered. You get a few dialogue options here.

> **Tip:** Picking **Defuse** both times talks everyone down without a fight. Provoking them or attacking works too, but you then have a gunfight on your hands.

## Chase Dunk

Dunk bolts through a shutter at the back. Open it and go after him. The chase ends when he crashes at a farm.

## Take Dunk home

Drive Dunk back to his trailer. Vicki pays back the $50 into your BuckMe account, and Dunk promises to be in touch about the painkillers.
