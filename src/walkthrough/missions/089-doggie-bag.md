The first of the jobs Giorgio's crew line up after [Taking Liberties](/walkthrough/taking-liberties/). Talk to **Vinny on the balcony by the pool** in the penthouse to start it. You play it as [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/).

> **Note:** Giorgio needs to find a rival's cocaine shipment, and Vinny's answer is to borrow the coast guard's best nose: a sniffer dog called **Rocco**. The coast guard are searching a cruise ship at the port, and Rocco is on board with them.

> **Tip:** Lucia's silenced pistol is loaded with **non-lethal rounds** for this one. A headshot puts a guard down without killing him.

## Get onto the ship

Start by going into the **open garage** in front of you. Inside, take the hallway to the **left** so you avoid the worker on the right-hand side of the shelves. Use the **Snap Gun** on the blue **Storage** door to get through quietly.

There's some **cash** to pick up in the next room. Knock out the worker there to get across to the other side.

Outside, wait for the guards to wander away from the **bridge** into the ship, then walk across.

## Up to the top deck

Inside, follow the way up to the higher deck, dealing with the guards who are in your path. In the next big area, take the **elevator** to the top deck.

## Find Rocco

Step out onto the open deck and head **south-east**. The **stairs down** to the lower walkways keep you a little further from the guards. Rocco is near the **pool area** at the back, with his handlers around him.

## Put Rocco to sleep

Wait until he comes close to you, focus on him and press **X / A** to give him the **drugged biscuit**. Once he's dozed off, pick him up.

## Get to the helicopter

The landing zone is at the **far south-east end** of the ship. Go to the edge of the deck and follow it to the back, knocking out any guard who gets in the way. Climb into the helicopter quickly to finish.
