Your first car chase with shooting from the window. It starts at the pool bar in south Key Lento.

## Ride with Brian

Get in Brian's car and wait for him, then drive to the liquor store nearby to pick up Cal.

## Find Arnold

Drive south to the search area marked in pink. As you get closer, the area shrinks until it points to where Arnold is.

## Chase him

Arnold runs, and gunmen on motorcycles open fire. Hold L1/LB to aim from the car and R1/RB to shoot. Press L3 and R3 together to slow time, which makes the bikers much easier to hit.

> **Tip:** The chase takes you over ramps on the bridges. Those count as stunt jumps, and completed ones stay marked on the map.

## Catch him at the docks

Arnold ditches the car and runs on foot. Follow him to the docks, where it ends.

## Decide

You choose whether Arnold lives. Either way he does not come back, and nothing else in the game changes.
