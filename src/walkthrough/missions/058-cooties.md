The car [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) drove away from DWNPLY in [Ride or Die](/walkthrough/ride-or-die/) gets a second career. [Dre'Quan](/characters/drequan-priest/) wants it in a music video for [Real Dimez](/characters/real-dimez/), and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) is the one behind the wheel.

## Meet Dre'Quan in Crosstown

A couple of in-game days after Ride or Die, Dre'Quan gets in touch. Head to his marker in Crosstown to start.

## Drive for the camera

Lucia is driving DWNPLY's car for the video. Hold R2 / RT to keep it rolling and use the left stick to shift its position as it circles. The shoot then moves out onto the streets; keep doing the same thing until the car comes to a stop.

## Break up the fight

Get out when the car stops. Press R2 / RT to drag the man off Roxy, then deal with his friend when he comes at you.

## Wreck the car

The girls film themselves laying into the car, then hand Lucia a bat so she can join in. Take a few swings. After that, press X / A to shake the spray can and R2 / RT to paint the side of the car.

## Lose DWNPLY's crew

DWNPLY and his people turn up to take the car back. Drive off and keep going until every car behind you has dropped away and you're outside the red area on the map.

> **Note:** It works just like the getaway in Ride or Die: there's no set route, only the red area to get out of.

## Drop the car at the studio

Drive to the studio marker and leave the car there. That ends the mission, and **Bae-Luxe's Bat** goes into your weapons.
