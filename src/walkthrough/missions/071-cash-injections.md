A while after [Making Gainz](/walkthrough/making-gainz/), Nate gets in touch with [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) and wants to hang out. Head to the Muscle Beach gym on Ocean Beach as Lucia. It starts as a favour and ends in two gunfights, so go in ready for a fight.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

> **Spoiler:** Nate is selling Synthol on the side, and he promises to put in a good word for Lucia with Kordell if she helps him move it.

## Meet Nate and follow him

Find Nate at Muscle Beach. He walks off the sand towards the road, so stay with him until you reach his car.

## Drive to the deal

Get in Nate's car and drive a few blocks north. The meeting is in a back alley, marked on the map.

## Open the trunk

Park up in the alley. When Nate asks for the goods, walk round to the back of the car and open the trunk.

## Protect Nate

The handover doesn't last long. When the prompt comes up, press **Square** (PS5) or **X** (Xbox) to knock the gun out of the hand of the man next to Lucia. Then take out the buyers around Nate. In the confusion one of them gets into Nate's car and drives off.

## Chase down Nate's car

Take the car the buyers left in the alley and go after him. There's no way to stop the car by shooting the driver here, so put your effort into keeping it in sight.

## Take the car back at the motel

The chase ends in an alley next to a motel, and a lot of the buyers' people are waiting. Clear them all out before you go for the car. More of them come out of the doors on the sides and shoot down from the upper floor, so check above you.

## Get away

Drive off in Nate's car. If the police have seen you, you'll need to lose them before the mission moves on.

## Ice cream with Nate

Once you're clear, Nate wants to buy Lucia an ice cream. Drive to the parlor marked on the map in the north of Vice Beach. The mission ends there.
