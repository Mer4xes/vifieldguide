The last mission of the story. It follows straight on from [Protective Custody](/walkthrough/protective-custody/), and you play as both [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/), switching between them as it goes.

> **Spoiler:** The target is the hard drive in Giorgio's office, the one the prosecutor told Lucia about.

## Gear up

The mission opens in the **Keys**. Pick any weapons you like, and take the **body armor** and **explosives** too. Then walk out of the marked area on the minimap.

## Air support

You're flown to the penthouse tower with Lucia on the **helicopter's turret**. Enemies pour out onto the **balconies**; keep sweeping them until the helicopter has circled the building.

## Into the office

Jason jumps out once the loop is done. Giorgio's office is at the **back of the floor** he lands on, and more enemies wait inside. Behind the desk, use the **computer**: the plugging-in happens by itself.

## Lucia in the air

Switch to Lucia, now flying the helicopter. First shoot down the **enemy helicopter**, then turn the gun on the enemies closing in on Jason inside the penthouse.

## Back to the office

Switch to Jason. The way he came in is blocked, so go to the **far side of the floor**, climb up the **collapsed floor** to the level above and work your way **round the elevators** on the top floor. Back in the office, pick up the **hard drive**.

## The crane

You're Lucia again, this time on a **crane** that's coming down. Run to the **far end** and jump as it falls; she lands inside the penthouse.

## Down to the garage

As Jason, follow the **elevator shaft** down to the garage and kill the enemies shooting at Lucia. Once they're down, go to her and free her from the **rubble**.

## Fight out of the garage

Now you control both and can switch at any time. Fight your way to the far side of the garage floor; there's more **body armor** on the way. Near the far end, get to the car to climb up.

## Outrun the helicopter

Hold the accelerator and race round the **outside of the building** to stay out of the helicopter's fire. When enemies jump onto the car, aim at the helicopter itself and shoot the **two men inside** to bring it down.

## The last drive

At the construction site, walk into the **elevator**. After the scenes you're put in a car: drive to **Cal's house**, then keep going **south** as the objective tells you.

## The choice

When the car is stopped, two options come up on screen. Each leads to a different ending, and the story is over either way.

> **Spoiler:** The two options are **Swim For It** and **No Surrender**.

> **Note:** Both choices earn **You Gotta Know When to Get Off**, the gold trophy for finishing the story. To see the other ending, load the save you made before Protective Custody and play both missions again.

## After the credits

The game puts you back in free roam at the point **just before Protective Custody**. The pause menu now has the **100% checklist** and the **cheats list**. Our [100% checklist](/guides/100-percent-checklist/) and [100% route](/guides/100-percent-route/) show what's left, and [cheats](/guides/cheats/) lists what each one needs. For a plan of what to do next, see [after the story](/guides/after-the-story/).
