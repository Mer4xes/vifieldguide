To start, talk to Lucia on the beach in the south of Key Lento after [Recalibration](/walkthrough/recalibration/). From that conversation on, the story runs straight through the last three missions of Chapter 1 with no free roam until Chapter 2 begins.

## Up to the lockers

Mattia drops the two of you off below the visitor center. Go up the ramp and follow the long walkway to the lockers at the far end, then take the bag.

## Back to the car

Walk the bag back to the car.

> **Spoiler:** The pickup is a setup. Police are waiting at the car and arrest Jason and Lucia, and it soon becomes clear that a corrupt captain, Méndez, wants that bag. Things turn violent, Jason is shot, and control switches to Lucia.

## Fight back as Lucia

As Lucia, shoot your way out of the building.

> **Note:** The police you kill in this shootout don't count against your criminal profile. It is part of the story.

## To the boat

Lucia has to help Jason down to the docks. She holds him up with one arm and shoots a pistol with the other, so keep moving and fire at whoever is closest.

## Out to sea

Get the boat going and head through the pink marker into open water.

## Run to Cal's

Drive the boat to Cal's house to end the mission.

> **Spoiler:** Jason passes out on the way. Cal takes him to a hospital in Vice City for surgery, and the story picks up with Lucia on her own.
