Mostly a fun one, and the first time the story takes you up to Vice City. You play both Jason and Lucia.

## The bar in Key Lento

The day starts at the bar in Key Lento. Get a drink to set things off.

## Out to the yacht

Take the boat out to the yacht.

> **Spoiler:** On the way, Lucia's ankle monitor comes off.

## Jet skis and champagne

There is a jet ski race, then a bit of fun shooting champagne bottles.

## Find Mattia

Look around the yacht for Mattia to move the day along.

## Vice City at night

The party moves to a club in Vice City.

## Out of the hotel

The night ends at a hotel, and you have to get out of there in a hurry. Follow the markers.
