The first mission as Jason, and the one that sets up stealth, focus mode and the criminal profile. Finishing it earns the **Just Like Paradise** trophy.

## Pick up the drop

Donnie's plane drops packages into the sea. Steer the boat behind it and ease off the throttle next to each package so Cal can hook it out of the water.

## Watch the deal

Once the packages are aboard, the hand-off happens on a dock. Open the weapon wheel, pick the binoculars and aim with L2/LT to watch it play out.

> **Spoiler:** The deal goes wrong and the Kingfishers take Donnie. The rest of the mission is about getting him back.

## Sneak into the camp

Take the boat ashore near the Kingfishers' camp and continue on foot. Stealth is the easier route: stay crouched, keep to cover and take guards down from behind.

> **Tip:** Press L3 and R3 together for focus mode. It highlights enemies, objects and where you need to go.

## Free Donnie

Find Donnie's shack. Focus mode and the map both point to it, and you can walk in through the front door. Untie him, then deal with the big man who comes at you in a fist fight. You get Zombix afterwards to heal up.

## Back to the plane

Make your way to the seaplane at the dock. You can fight through or sneak, whichever suits you.

> **Tip:** Near the route back there is a trailer with cash inside and one enemy guarding it. It is optional, but worth the detour.

> **Tip:** How you treat enemies feeds Jason's criminal profile. Taking people down quietly counts toward Professional, shots to arms and legs toward Hardened, and killing toward Psychopath. The mission does not care which you choose.

## Land at Brian's Marina

Fly the damaged seaplane to the marker. To land, slow right down and keep the nose level as you touch the water near Brian's Marina. Then go and talk to Brian.

## Head home

Drive Cal back to his place, then drive to Jason's house in Key Lento, which is now yours to use.

> **Spoiler:** On the way home Lucia calls. She is getting out early.
