This starts on its own as soon as [Tourist Trap](/walkthrough/tourist-trap/) ends. You play only as Lucia.

## Inside the house

Two men are waiting at Jason's house. Fight them off.

## Get out

Forget the money. There isn't time to reach it, so head straight for the front door and keep going.

> **Spoiler:** The men have set the house on fire, and it goes up moments after Lucia gets clear. The Key Lento house is gone for good.

## Slip away

The police are searching the area. Stay crouched, keep to the shadows and work your way to the main road that runs through the middle of Key Lento.

> **Tip:** The quickest way out is to steal a car on the main road and drive out of the search zone. Just make sure no police car is watching when you take it. Walking out also works if you stay hidden.

## Reach the edge

Once you get near the edge of the search area, a cutscene plays and the next mission, [Discharge Time](/walkthrough/discharge-time/), begins.
