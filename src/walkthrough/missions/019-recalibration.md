Lucia phones Jason after [Don Gone](/walkthrough/don-gone/), and the mission starts when you get home to her. The mission itself is easy. What comes after it is the reason to slow down.

> **Watch out:** The guides we checked disagree on exactly when free roam closes: as soon as this mission starts, or right after it ends. Either way, do the checklist above **before** you head home to Lucia.

## Pick up beer

Stop at the liquor store on the map and take a six-pack from the fridge. It is paid for as you pick it up, so there is no need to go to the till.

## Drive to Cal's

Take the beer over to Cal's house.

## Find Cal

Cal is round the back of the house, lying on a lounger by the water. Talk to him.

## Out on the boat

Get in the boat and follow the marker to the fishing spot. The trip takes a couple of minutes.

## Fishing

Fishing works like this:

- Cast with **L2 + R2** (LT + RT on Xbox).
- Turn the right stick clockwise.
- When a fish bites, press **R2** (RT) to set the hook.
- Pull the left stick away from the direction the fish is swimming and spin the right stick fast to reel it in.

Keep going until the objective is done.

## Back to the dock

Drive the boat back to Cal's dock.

## Grill the fish

The grill is next to Cal. There are three pieces of fish, and each needs cooking on both sides until it is done. Cal puts them in buns for you.

## Take Lucia home

Lucia isn't hungry and says the heat has got to her. Follow her out to the front of the house and drive her home. A cutscene ends the mission.

> **Tip:** Catching fish for Cal, Kenton's treasure dives and the Kingfisher hideouts drop off the map for a while after this point. They come back later in the story, so you don't lose them.
