A Chapter 3 job from [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) that keeps cutting between [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/): one of them on the river, the other on the metro. It has two start points on the map, a bar in Stockyard for Lucia and a boatyard in La Perle for Jason, and either one begins it.

## Jason: follow the boats

The Guardia's boats head off down the river and Jason follows on a bike. Keep to the dirt paths along the bank: they stay close to the water and are the only way to keep up. Stay with the boats until they pull in.

## Lucia: catch the train

Lucia is following one of the gang onto the metro, and the train leaves before she can get on. That's scripted. Get to the next station before it does. The elevated track runs overhead, so run along under it, climb the stairs and get into the last carriage.

## Lucia: put him on the phone

Walk up the carriage to the Guardia man and pull a gun on him. Use the prompt to lean on him until he calls his people and tells them someone new is taking the boat.

## Jason: take the boat

Jason walks up to the couriers on the dock, and they don't buy it. Get back on the bike and follow Karim's directions over the earpiece: turn right at the bridge and keep going until the road ends with a jump onto the boat. Take out the crew on board.

> **Tip:** Karim's route is the quick way onto the boat. Watch the map and turn exactly when he says.

## Lucia: stop the train

Back on the metro, the gang comes for Lucia in the carriage. Fight them off hand to hand, then make for the emergency stop button at the end of the carriage. Hit it before the train gets to the next station. It stops above the river and Lucia drops down onto Jason's boat.

## Defend the boat

Jason drives and Lucia shoots from the back with an assault rifle. Guardia boats and jet skis come first, then the Coast Guard. Take out whatever is chasing you and keep going until you're clear.

> **Spoiler:** They get away with the boat and what it's carrying, and bring it all back to Raul.

## Meet Raul

Follow the marker to Raul to hand over the haul. He pays **$3,000** and the mission ends. Next in the story is [Credit Check](/walkthrough/credit-check/).
