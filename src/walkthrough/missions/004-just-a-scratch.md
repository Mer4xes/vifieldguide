One of the shortest missions in the game. It opens at the same time as **Lab Fees**, and you can do them in either order.

## Drive to Coastal Auto Care

Take Jason's '70 Ganado to Coastal Auto Care, marked on the map.

## Fix the engine

Drive into the workshop and choose the full engine repair. This first one costs nothing.

> **Note:** Guides disagree on whether a respray is part of the job. If the objective stays open after the engine repair, respray the car and it will complete.

## Drive out

Leave the garage to finish. Anything else on the menu, like paint or parts, is optional and costs money.
