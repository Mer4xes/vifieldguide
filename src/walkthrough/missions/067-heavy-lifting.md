Once [Lawyer Up](/walkthrough/lawyer-up/) is done, [Raul](/characters/raul-bautista/) calls: he wants the hard drive you've been keeping. You play as both [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) and [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/), and the game switches between them for you. There's no fighting in this one.

## Collect the hard drive

Go to your storage locker in Crosstown. Ride the lift up to the stash and take the drive.

## Meet Raul on the houseboat

Drive out to Raul's houseboat in Leonard County. Take the stairs on the right-hand side up to the top deck, where Karim is waiting with him.

> **Spoiler:** Karim works out who the drive belonged to: Giorgio Gallo, heir to the Bilgeco shipping business. Raul then tells you who he wants to rob: a crew of bodybuilders run by Kordell Grant, an old associate of his.

## Take the boat to Ajax Gym

After the scene you're at the wheel of a boat. Head south to Ajax Gym and pull in at the dock next to it.

## Get the tour

Lucia goes in first and Jason follows. Talk to the receptionist (the one in the blue tank top), then follow J-Wang round the gym.

## Search Raina's bag

Back at reception the game hands you Lucia. Use the marked locker to change into gym clothes, then open the locker on its left and go through Raina's backpack.

## Work out undercover

Head up to the main gym floor. Jason starts on a treadmill: tap X / A over and over to keep up. Then, as Lucia, use the leg extension machine with L2 / LT and R2 / RT.

## Slip into the tanning rooms

When Kordell's people leave, go with Jason to the tanning salon. He keeps the staff member busy while Lucia walks past into the tanning rooms.

## Trap Nate

Nate is in the second room on the right. Pick up the lifting strap lying on the floor, then use the tanning bed to lock him in.

## Search Nate's bag

His bag is to the right. Take out his phone and read his messages in the WhatUp app.

> **Spoiler:** The messages point to a job Kordell's crew is planning at Vice City International Airport.

When you're done, put the phone away and use the tanning bed again to let him out.

## Meet Jason outside

Leave the gym. Changing back into your own clothes first is optional. Jason is waiting outside by the water.

## Tail the bodybuilders

It's night now and you're behind the wheel. Follow the bodybuilders' car at a distance. It's a long drive.

> **Tip:** Keep an eye on the marker over their car. White is fine. If it starts turning yellow you're too close, so ease off the accelerator and let another car slip in between you.

## Follow them into the hotel

They stop at the Grand Majestic Aparthotel. Go through the front entrance after them and watch them get into a lift.

## Up to the roof

Walk over to the lifts. Jason calls the second one. Ride it to the top floor and follow the corridor out onto the roof to end the mission.
