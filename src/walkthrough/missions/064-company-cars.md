Once the pool scene after [Another Life](/walkthrough/another-life/) is over, go back to the Y Hotel and talk to Santino, the concierge, in the lobby.

> **Tip:** The marker may show a lock straight after the pool scene. Ignore it: Santino will still talk to you.

## Talk to Santino

He offers to send you to the dealership now or later. Say now, and the **hotel car service** drives you there. Remember it: the car service out front works as a free taxi any time you're at the hotel.

## Speak to the salesman

At Status Auto Imports, look for the salesman arguing with another man beside the red car and talk to him.

> **Tip:** The showroom is full of expensive cars. If you're filling your WAiNK car collection, scan them while you're here.

## Follow Rob

Rob takes you across to the cars lined up on the far side of the showroom.

## Pick Jason's car

Jason chooses first. Get into the driver's seat of the one you want and confirm with X / A. The **Pfister Comet S2** has the best base stats if speed is what you're after.

## Pick Lucia's car

Now do the same for Lucia. A truck or an SUV gives you a more mixed pair. Both cars are yours to modify, upgrade or sell later.

## Chase Lucia

Lucia drives off first. Stay on her tail.

## Race back to the Y Hotel

She soon turns it into a race back to the hotel. Dents don't matter, since the cars are already yours, but expect a comment from Lucia if you arrive in a mess. Reaching the Y Hotel ends the mission.

Next is [Lawyer Up](/walkthrough/lawyer-up/), which Valentina set up during the pool scene.
