A [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) job that you play from both sides. [Lucia](/characters/lucia-caminos/) is there to help Kordell's crew pull off a roadside job, while [Jason](/characters/jason-duval/) uses the time to sneak into their building and photograph what they're really planning. It opens after [Child's Play](/walkthrough/#chapter-4) and starts with Lucia at the crew's base in Salton.

> **Note:** In this part of Chapter 4 several missions are open at the same time, for Jason and for Lucia. The order you get them in can be different from ours.

> **Tip:** Power Play doesn't appear by itself. After [Dirty Work](/walkthrough/dirty-work/) the mission list shows **Nate Will Be in Touch**, which only means waiting for his call. It comes once Jason's open missions are finished, [Child's Play](/walkthrough/childs-play/) included, and you're playing as Lucia.

## Find Kordell

Go in as Lucia and ask at the front desk. You're pointed down the hall, where Kordell and the crew are waiting. Once Lucia heads out with them, the game hands you Jason.

## Get into the building

Don't try the front. Go round to the right side of the building and climb the ladder up to the roof. Walk across, drop down to the lower level on the far side and slip in through the door that's standing open.

## Reach the planning room

Head down to the ground floor. The planning room is behind the door on the right; use the **Snap Gun** on the lock so nobody hears you.

## Find the heist plans

Take photos of what's lying around: the shirts, the bags by the garage door and the whiteboard. The whiteboard is the one that matters, but its front is only a training plan. Pick it up (L2 / LT), turn it over and photograph the back.

> **Spoiler:** The back of the board is the real thing. Raul isn't helping Kordell's crew out of kindness: he wants them to do the hard work so he can take the score off them.

## Block the road

Back with Lucia. Grab the cones from the crew's truck and set them out in a line across the road to stop the traffic.

## Arm up and cover the crew

Follow Kordell along the roadside. When he knocks something over, go to it and take the **assault rifle**. Once the armored truck is on its side, the police start pouring in from every direction. Stay in cover and keep them off the crew while they empty it.

## Fight up through the construction site

Follow Kordell under the highway and into the building site, then up through it floor by floor. The sides are open, so expect shooters from more than one direction. Near the top the crew drops into cover on its own; clear the roof and the balcony, climb one more floor and jump across to the highway from the corner.

## Push down the highway

Stay with the group as it moves along the highway between the wrecked cars. Enemies pop up from behind the cars in front and along the right-hand side of the road.

## Take a car and lose the cops

When you're told to grab a car, ignore the ones facing you, because that way is blocked. Run to the far end and take the small car pointing down the road. The crew shoots at whoever follows. Take the **first right** on the highway to dodge the roadblock, keep following the road and the mission ends once the police lose you.
