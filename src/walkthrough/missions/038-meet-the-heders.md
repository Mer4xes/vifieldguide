Played as both Jason and Lucia, after [Dealer's Choice](/walkthrough/dealers-choice/). It doesn't show up straight away: wait until Brian gets in touch, then start it from the apartment. The first half is a dinner with no action; the second half is a theft at the port.

## Dinner with the Heders

Get ready at the apartment and drive to the restaurant in Vice City. Head up to the rooftop bar, where Brian and Lori are waiting at their table, and sit through dinner with them.

> **Spoiler:** Brian uses the evening to put the two of you in touch with an old contact of his, Raul, who has work going.

## Switch to Lucia

When dinner wraps up, the game hands control to Lucia. Follow the markers to her ride and drive to the port.

## Find the SCOOP container

Inside the Port of Vice City, you're after one container: the one marked **SCOOP**. Check the markings on the stacks until you find it.

## Cut it open

Use the cutting tool on the container and follow the on-screen prompts until it's open.

## Get the car out

The car you are after is inside. Get it out of the port and onto the open road.

## Deliver the car

If the police are on you, lose them first. Then drive to the marked drop-off to finish.

> **Note:** Next is [Dubious Provenance](/walkthrough/dubious-provenance/). Raul's first proper job for Lucia comes later in the chapter, in [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/).
