<h2 id="drones">Top Fun drone races</h2>
<p>Each Top Fun spot has three time trials. <b>Beat the par time on the first two</b> and the third opens; on the third, any finish counts for the checklist. That makes 28 par times you have to beat. Look for them under <b>Map › Activities › Top Fun Drones</b>. A taxi gets you to every spot except Dalton Island. The list runs roughly from south to north.</p>

<h2 id="ranges">Shooting ranges</h2>
<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Buy a Waiver Pass</b> for $5 from the clerk in the range's store. The challenges then show up on the map.</li>
<li><b>Rent the gun</b> a challenge asks for ($10) if you don't own one. Rented guns are good enough for gold.</li>
<li><b>Every challenge you finish or quit raises Focus</b> a little, for good. Top Focus back up between runs by eating or drinking at home, or washing your face.</li>
<li><b>One tick per gold.</b> Griffin has three levels per challenge, Rodgers Ranch four, which adds up to the 42 that The Shootist wants.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="races">Unique races</h2>
<p class="stunt-how">Forty races at six venues. Each one costs an entry fee plus a vehicle rental, and a win more than pays it back. At each spot, one race opens the next, so play them in order and tick them off as you go. Ten wins across Gellhorn Raceway, the Mud Club and Boone Speedway also unlock the <a href="/guides/cheats/">Repair Vehicle cheat</a>.</p>
