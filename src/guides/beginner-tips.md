<h2 id="saving">Saving</h2>

Pause the game (**Options** on PS5, **Menu** on Xbox), open **Game** and choose **Save Game**, then pick a slot. You can do it anywhere in free roam, but not while a mission is running: the option is greyed out until it's over.

The game also **autosaves when you finish a mission**. There is a single autosave, and each new one replaces the last. It doesn't save on a timer, so a long session of free roam with no missions in it isn't saved unless you do it yourself.

There is **no mission replay**. Whatever you miss inside a mission, and any choice you make, sticks unless you load an older save. So:

- **Keep several manual saves and rotate them.** Overwriting the same slot every time leaves you nothing to go back to.
- **Save before a mission with a choice in it.** Our [story choices](/guides/story-choices/) page lists them.
- **Save before a point of no return.** The [missables guide](/guides/missables/) has every one, and the [100% route](/guides/100-percent-route/) shows them in order.

<h2 id="map">The map and the index</h2>

Press the **touchpad** to open the map, then **right on the D-pad** to open the **index** down the side. It sorts everything you've discovered into groups:

| Group | What you'll find there |
|---|---|
| **Personal** | Your home, your personal vehicles, the garages you own and ATMs to bank cash. |
| **Story** | Every mission you can start. The ones under **Optional** don't move the story on and can wait. |
| **Robberies** | Banks, stick-ups, gang hideouts and vehicle drop-offs you've found. See the [robberies guide](/guides/robberies/). |
| **Activities** | Races, shooting ranges, golf, gyms, clubs, and fishing and hunting spots. |
| **Exploration** | Points of interest, side quests, stunt and base jumps and pawn shop collectors. |
| **Vehicles** | Mod shops, Pay 'n' Sprays, gas stations, transport stops, ferry terminals and garages. |
| **Weapons** | Ammu-Nation stores, split into Everyday, Premium, Hunting and Pawn. |
| **Style** | Clothes shops, tattoo studios and hair and make-up for both characters. |
| **Food** | Bars, cafes, fast food, convenience and liquor stores. |

Highlight an entry and press **X** to make it your destination. You can only have **one active destination** at a time, but you can drop extra markers anywhere on the map with **Square**. New finds have an **orange diamond** next to them until you've looked.

> **Tip:** In free roam you don't even need the map. Hold **down on the D-pad** and press **R3** on a marker you can see in the world to head straight for it.

Our [shop locations](/guides/shops/) page lists every store by county if you want to plan a trip before the index shows it.

<h2 id="travel">Getting around without driving</h2>

Driving is usually fastest, but when you don't feel like it, there are three ways to be taken somewhere. **None of them is free.**

| Ride | Where or when | How it works | Pay with |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bus and train** | Any stop or station | Go to a stop or station and wait for the next one (hold **down on the D-pad** to see stops near you). Once on board, pick your stop and hold **Triangle** (**Y**) to skip the trip. | The fare |
| **Paradise Taxi** | Early in Chapter 1 | Call it from **Contacts** on your phone (up on the D-pad) and pick where to go. | Pocket cash |
| **RydeMe** | Chapter 2 | Open the app on your phone. Ask for a ride home to your safehouse, or to anywhere else. | BuckMe |

A taxi or RydeMe car drives the whole way in real time. To skip it, focus on the driver with **L2** (**LT**) and press **Triangle** (**Y**): that's **$100** on top of the fare. From Chapter 3, **BuckMe Premium** takes 20% off every RydeMe trip.

> **Watch out:** Paradise Taxi only takes the cash in your pocket. If you've banked it all, take some out at an ATM first.

<h2 id="auto-drive">Auto drive</h2>

Auto drive lets Jason or Lucia drive themselves to your waypoint while the camera shows off the view. It isn't quicker than driving yourself; it's for sightseeing, screenshots and the radio.

1. Set a waypoint on the map and get behind the wheel.
2. Hold **down on the D-pad**. While still holding it, press **Options** (**Menu** on Xbox) to switch to the cinematic camera.
3. Hold the button the **Enable Auto Drive** prompt shows in the bottom corner.

Once it's on, the **triggers** choose one of three speeds, the **right stick** moves the camera and **R3** (**RS**) jumps to the next angle. Move your waypoint and it re-routes. It keeps to the rules of the road and waits in traffic like everyone else.

<h2 id="police">How the police find you</h2>

Police only turn up when **someone reports a crime**: a witness who saw it (or saw you with a gun out), a shop alarm, or a stolen car with an Advanced Tracker. The wanted meter goes up to **six stars**.

What makes GTA 6 different is that the police **remember what they saw**. Red icons under your stars show each clue they have, and every clue makes you easier to spot again. Clear them and you're just another face in the crowd:

| Clue | How to clear it |
|---|---|
| **Face** | Wear a mask before the crime and take it off out of sight, swap an accessory, or get a new haircut at a [salon](/guides/shops/#salons). |
| **Outfit** | Change clothes. Your personal vehicle's trunk can do it anywhere. |
| **Vehicle** | Ditch it for another car, or respray it at a Pay 'n' Spray. The [cars guide](/guides/cars/#impound) explains what happens to a car you leave behind. |
| **Weapon** | Holster it with **L1** (**LB**). |
| **Partner** | Split Jason and Lucia up and go different ways. |
| **Camera footage** | Destroy the footage on site before you leave, or keep out of the cameras' view to begin with. |

Once you've got away, the area around where you were last seen stays marked on the map as **Heat**. If you're spotted there again, or commit another crime there, the stars come straight back. Stay away and it cools off.

> **Tip:** Don't shoot your way out. Every officer you kill makes the response bigger and counts against your [criminal profile](/how-it-plays/#criminal-profile). Break line of sight, hide and work through the clues instead.

<h2 id="wasted">Wasted or busted</h2>

When you're killed or arrested, you lose:

- **The cash in your pocket.** Money in BuckMe is safe, so bank it at an ATM. The [money guide](/guides/money/) has the details.
- **Guns you took from enemies.** Weapons you paid for stay yours: take them out again from the **weapon locker** at any Ammu-Nation or from your personal vehicle's trunk.

<h2 id="safes">Safes and safe codes</h2>

The map tells you whether a business has a safe, how tight its security is and roughly what it's worth. Story missions hand you their codes. In a robbery you have two ways to get the **four-digit** code:

- **Find it.** It's often written down somewhere in the back office. Focus mode (**L3** and **R3** together) helps you spot it.
- **Ask for it.** Aim at an employee and choose the prompt that demands the safe code.

> **Tip:** Put a mask on first, and deal with the cameras before you leave. The [robberies guide](/guides/robberies/) lists every jewelry store, pawn shop, bank and payday loan store, and the [fences guide](/guides/fences/) says who buys what you take.
