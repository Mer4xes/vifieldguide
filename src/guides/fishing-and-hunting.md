<h2 id="fishing">Fishing</h2>

There is no fishing licence and nothing to unlock. As soon as you're in free roam you can fish from a dock, the shore or a boat. The rod sits in the **equipment slot** of the weapon wheel (hold **L1/LB**), next to the binoculars.

<h3 id="lures">Lures</h3>

You start with two lures: a **Small Saltwater Lure** for the sea, docks and coast, and a **Small Freshwater Lure** for lakes and rivers. Bigger lures hook bigger fish that fight harder, and you buy them:

- **Chuck's Tackle & Shot** in Key Lento, Mariana County. It's the Ammu-Nation you visit in [Gear Up](/walkthrough/gear-up/), so you know the way from the first chapter.
- **Other gun shops that stock fishing gear.** See every Ammu-Nation by county in [shop locations](/guides/shops/#ammu-nation).

The **Catch of the Day** challenge wants three fish on every type of lure in a single day, so buy the full set before you try it.

<h3 id="controls">Controls</h3>

| Step | PlayStation | Xbox |
|---|---|---|
| Pick the rod | Hold L1, equipment slot | Hold LB, equipment slot |
| Get ready and cast | Hold L2, then R2 | Hold LT, then RT |
| Spot fish with Focus | L3 + R3 | LS + RS |
| Work the lure | Turn the right stick slowly | Turn the right stick slowly |
| Hook a bite | R2 | RT |
| Fight the fish | Left stick, away from where it swims | Left stick, away from where it swims |
| Reel it in | Spin the right stick while it rests | Spin the right stick while it rests |

> **Tip:** Don't reel while the fish is pulling. Steer against it with the left stick until it tires, then reel.

<h3 id="catch">What happens to the fish</h3>

In free roam every fish goes **back in the water**. You can't keep, cook or sell it. What you do get:

- **Focus.** Every fish you land raises your Focus, so fishing is one of the easy ways to train it.
- **The log.** Each species goes into **Progress › Animals › Fishing**, which also keeps your biggest catch and how many you've landed. There are **35** species.
- **The Fisheye cheat** after **100 fish**. See [cheats](/guides/cheats/).
- **Challenges** and **trophies**: see the list [below](#tick-list).

Cal's fishing challenge, for **Baited My Tackle**, opens after [Feng Shui](/walkthrough/feng-shui/) in Chapter 1: a stoplight parrotfish over 2 lbs in Gambit Bay. The full how-to is on [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#cal).

<h2 id="hunting">Hunting</h2>

Hunting is where animals make you money, and it's **Jason's** job. Lucia can't start it.

1. **Veteran Hunted.** After [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/) in Chapter 2, Bret texts Jason about a deer hunt in **Lummox County**, near Mount Kalaga. Go alone. It pays $500 and a unique hunting rifle, and opens Bret's other hunts.
2. **Hunt and carry.** Carry what you kill back to your vehicle and put it in the trunk. Use a [personal vehicle](/guides/cars/#personal): if a stolen car is wrecked, whatever is in its trunk goes with it.
3. **Sell to Bret.** He buys what you bring in. A clean kill pays more: use the weapon the animal's info suggests and drop it with one well-placed shot. Every extra body shot lowers what the carcass is worth.

Bret also hands out **Target Decks**, hunts for particular animals. The 100% checklist wants all **15**, and **Fair Game** is for the unique animal he asks for. More on him on [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#bret).

> **Watch out:** **Hunting Spree** wants a boar, a rattlesnake and a panther within 15 minutes, without taking a hit from people or animals. Go in with full health and armour.

<h2 id="animal-log">The animal log</h2>

The pause menu keeps a log of every animal you've **studied**, under **Progress › Animals**. It has **255** entries in eight groups:

| Group | Entries |
|---|---|
| Hunting | 61 |
| Birds | 60 |
| Fishing | 35 |
| Cats and dogs | 34 |
| Other | 23 |
| Mammals | 18 |
| Aquatic | 18 |
| Reptiles and amphibians | 6 |

**To study an animal**, aim at it (hold **L2/LT**) up close, or look through the binoculars from further off. When the **Study** prompt shows, hold **R1/RB** until the meter fills. The species name pops up and it's in the log. Friendly animals you've studied can then be **petted** the same way.

- The 100% checklist wants **100 of the 255**. Studying is all it asks: you never have to kill, skin or catch anything for it.
- Every dog and cat breed is its own entry, so a walk through a park or the [zoo](/activities/#zoos) fills the log fast.
- Animals also count toward **Encyclopedia** (100 animals, vehicles and weapons). How vehicle scans work is in [activities and collectibles](/guides/activities-and-collectibles/#study-and-scan).
- What lives where, from the reef to the Grassrivers, is on our [wildlife page](/wildlife/).
