## When does GTA 6 unlock?

The game unlocks at midnight in your own time zone on Thursday, November 19, 2026. That applies to both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, so the release rolls around the world region by region instead of going live everywhere at once.

> **Note:** The US is the one place where the time is not settled. Rockstar says midnight local time, and AP and Forbes take that to mean midnight in each US time zone. The US PlayStation Store listing, though, reportedly shows one unlock for the whole country at midnight Eastern, which would let Central, Mountain and Pacific players in on Wednesday evening (9 p.m. Pacific). Rockstar has not published a US table. If you are west of the East Coast, check your console from 9 p.m. Pacific on November 18.

## Xbox Cloud Gaming

Cloud streaming does not follow the midnight rule. Xbox says it starts at **1 a.m. Eastern on Thursday, November 19**, which is 10 p.m. Pacific on Wednesday, November 18. The cloud version runs at Xbox Series S settings, so you can also stream it on Xbox One.

## Preload

Digital pre-orders have been able to download since Tuesday, November 10. Rockstar moved that date forward by two days from the November 12 it first announced.

- **PS5:** preload opened at midnight local time on November 10.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** preload opened for everyone at once, at 12 a.m. GMT on November 10.

Preloading only puts the files on your console. You still cannot start the game before the unlock time.

## How much space do you need?

Rockstar says the preload needs up to 212 GB, depending on your console. Free that much before you begin so the download does not stall halfway.

> **Tip:** Turn on automatic updates. Patches have already been going out before launch, and you want them installed before midnight.

## Updates before launch

Preloaded copies have already received two updates, according to press reports based on console update trackers. Rockstar has not published patch notes for either of them, and it has not said whether a separate day one patch is coming.

- **November 13:** version 1.002, roughly 1 GB on PS5.
- **From November 16:** version 1.002.001, roughly 600 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S. On PS5 it went out in waves and had reached everyone by the morning of November 17, US time.

On PS5 you can force a check: highlight the game, press Options and choose **Check for Update**. On Xbox, open **My games & apps** and look under **Updates**.

> **Note:** Version numbers and sizes above are reported, not confirmed by Rockstar. We will replace them with official patch notes if Rockstar publishes any.
