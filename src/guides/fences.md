<h2 id="cars">Selling stolen cars</h2>

**Moses** is the first. After [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/) in Chapter 2 he buys cars that don't have a tracker, from his place in Port Gellhorn. From [Daily Driver](/walkthrough/daily-driver/) on he also sends six specific requests, one at a time, and leaves you his Sultan when they're done; see [Moses's car jobs](/guides/side-contacts/#moses). **Randy** joins in Chapter 3: Lucia meets him when she delivers Raul's Schafter in [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/), and from then on his dealership in Balsero takes stolen cars too. Randy also has his own jobs, starting with a Zorrusso; see [Randy on side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#randy). From Chapter 3, cars with a Standard Tracker can also be sold at any Pay 'n' Spray. Which cars pay best, and when, is in the [money guide](/guides/money/#cars).

<h2 id="narcotics">Selling narcotics</h2>

You can sell drugs from Chapter 1. [Lab Fees](/walkthrough/lab-fees/) turns **Brian's lockup** into a fence, and after [Clean Up After Donnie](/walkthrough/clean-up-after-donnie/) the **drop behind Donnie's trailer** in Key Lento takes narcotics as well. In Chapter 2, **Dunk's trailer** in Port Gellhorn opens from [Slam Dunk](/walkthrough/slam-dunk/), and Dunk also marks two robbery targets on your map the day after. In Vice City, **Tyrone** starts buying once you've found Charles for him in [Here, Kitty Kitty](/walkthrough/here-kitty-kitty/).

<h2 id="jewelry">Selling jewelry and Marked Bills</h2>

**Yasmani** gets in touch after [Boobie Time](/walkthrough/boobie-time/) in Chapter 3. He buys jewelry and Marked Bills, and his first jobs have you rob three jewelry stores for him; see [Yasmani's jewelry jobs](/guides/side-contacts/#yasmani).

<h2 id="totals">Fencing for 100% and trophies</h2>

The [100% checklist](/guides/100-percent-checklist/) tracks how much you have fenced:

- **Vehicles:** $200,000
- **Narcotics:** $100,000
- **Jewelry:** $100,000
- **Marked Bills:** $250,000 laundered

The Marked Bills figure is the same one the **Dime on the Dollar** trophy asks for, so the two come together. See the [trophy list](/trophies/) for the rest, and [activities and collectibles](/guides/activities-and-collectibles/) for robberies and the other ways to get goods to sell.
