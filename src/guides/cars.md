<h2 id="scan">Scan it first</h2>

Before you touch a car, scan it with the **WAiNK** app (hold up on the D-pad). The scan tells you what it will take to steal, what the car is worth and what it would cost to register. The icons to look for:

| Icon | What it means | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| **Locked** | The doors are locked. | Smash a window, or the [Slim Jim](/guides/equipment/#slim-jim) to open it quietly. |
| **Alarm** | Breaking in loudly sets off the alarm and draws attention. | The Slim Jim, or a different car. |
| **Keyless** | It can't be hotwired at all. | The [Key Cloner](/guides/equipment/#key-cloner). |
| **Standard Tracker** | No mod shop will touch it until it's registered to you. | Register it, or sell it at a Pay 'n' Spray. |
| **Advanced Tracker** | Tells the police where you are unless you're moving fast. Common on high-end cars. | Keep your speed up all the way to the buyer. |

<h2 id="steal">Getting in</h2>

- **Try the handle first.** Plenty of parked cars aren't locked.
- **Smash the window** if it is. It's the fastest way in, but it's loud and can set off the alarm.
- **The Slim Jim** comes from the Chapter 2 mission [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/). It opens a locked door without breaking anything, so nobody notices.
- **The Key Cloner** is for the keyless cars you find all over Vice City. It arrives with Randy's job [Steal the Zorrusso for Randy](/walkthrough/steal-the-zorrusso-for-randy/), about halfway through Chapter 3. Stand at a door, interact and pick **Clone Key**, then find the sweet spot with the stick to speed it up.
- **Carjacking** still works: pull the driver out of any car on the road.

> **Tip:** In a police chase, a plain unlocked car is often the better grab. Breaking into a keyless car or setting off an alarm costs you time you don't have, and an Advanced Tracker keeps giving you away.

<h2 id="trackers">Trackers and who buys what</h2>

| Tracker | Sell it to | Register it |
|---|---|---|
| **None** | [Moses](/guides/fences/#cars) in Port Gellhorn, from Chapter 2 | At Moses, from Chapter 2 |
| **Standard** | Any Pay 'n' Spray, from Chapter 3 | At a Pay 'n' Spray, after [Welcome to Vice City](/walkthrough/welcome-to-vice-city/) |

Cars with an **Advanced Tracker** can be registered or sold at a Pay 'n' Spray too, but only once you're far enough into the story. We don't have the exact point yet.

Randy at his dealership in Balsero buys stolen cars too, from [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/) in Chapter 3. What pays best, chapter by chapter, is in the [money guide](/guides/money/#cars).

<h2 id="personal">Stolen car or personal vehicle</h2>

|  | Stolen (unowned) | Personal vehicle |
|---|---|---|
| **On the map** | Only the last one you left | Always, under **Personal** in the map index |
| **Wrecked or lost** | Gone for good, with everything in the trunk | Scooter Bros brings it back, for a fee |
| **Trunk** | Carries things, and is cleared if you're wasted or busted | Stores your gear, swaps your loadout and changes your outfit |
| **Mods** | Not while it has a tracker | Yes |

**Registering a car** makes it yours. Lucia's first one is in [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/): respray a car at his garage and register it in her name. From [Welcome to Vice City](/walkthrough/welcome-to-vice-city/) on you can drive a car into any **Pay 'n' Spray** and register it there; the fee depends on the make and model. At first only cars without a tracker can be registered; Standard Tracker cars follow after Welcome to Vice City, and Advanced Tracker cars later still.

**Jason and Lucia each have one personal vehicle at a time.** To keep more cars in their names you need room to store them. Moses gives Lucia a free garage in Port Gellhorn after [Daily Driver](/walkthrough/daily-driver/), and the Vice City Real Estate app sells eight more; the story and the [1010 Murals](/collectibles/1010-murals/) add others. Every one is on the [garage list](/guides/garages/).

> **Tip:** The trunk of your personal vehicle is a mobile locker. Change outfits there to lower your appearance heat, swap the guns you carry, and drop stash bags or hunting spoils in it so you don't lose them.

<h2 id="scooter-bros">Scooter Bros</h2>

The **Scooter Bros** app arrives with Moses in Chapter 2. It handles three things, and each one costs money:

1. **Delivery.** Ask for any registered car and they bring it to you, taking the one you don't want back to storage.
2. **Recovery.** A personal vehicle that's been wrecked comes back through the app.
3. **The impound.** They'll get your personal vehicle out of the police lot, for a much bigger fee. Anything you'd stashed in the trunk may not come back with it.

<h2 id="impound">How cars end up in the impound</h2>

- **Ditching a car to lose the police.** It clears the car's heat, but the police usually tow it.
- **Getting wasted or busted** while you're in your personal vehicle.

The map index shows where an impounded car is, under **Personal**. Scooter Bros is the easy way to get it back.

<h2 id="keep-stolen">Keeping a stolen car</h2>

You don't have to register a car to hold on to it, but you have to look after it. The map only remembers the last unowned car you left. Park it in a space outside a safehouse, where there is one, or put it in one of your garages. A stolen car that gets destroyed can't be recovered.

<h2 id="fuel">Fuel</h2>

Every vehicle runs on gas. A fuel gauge shows above the minimap. When it runs low it turns yellow and the map marks the gas stations nearby. A car that runs dry simply stops, so top up before a long chase or a getaway. There are [57 gas stations across Leonida](/guides/shops/#gas-stations), 26 of them in and around Vice City.

No car at all? Leonida has [25 train stations](/guides/shops/#train-stations), at least one in every county. Wait on the platform and ride the train instead.

<h2 id="trophies">Trophies and challenges</h2>

- **[On the Spot](/trophies/on-the-spot/):** register 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app. Each needs a free garage space.
- **[Little Boxes](/trophies/little-boxes/):** buy all 8 garage properties ($160,000, [list and prices](/guides/garages/)), which also gives you the space for On the Spot.
- **Carjacking Spree** and **Carjacking Pro** are car challenges: register or respray stolen cars from 10 makers in 30 minutes, and make $100,000 in a day selling stolen cars. See [challenges](/guides/challenges/#criminal).
