faq:
  - q: "How do you get Baited My Tackle in GTA 6?"
    a: "After Feng Shui in Chapter 1, Cal texts Jason a fishing challenge in Gambit Bay. Take a boat to the spot, drop anchor and fish with the Small Saltwater Lure until you land a stoplight parrotfish over 2 lbs, then send it to Cal. The trophy unlocks when you win."
  - q: "How do you increase max stamina in GTA 6?"
    a: "Finish The Final Flush, Dr. Merle's side job. Scan a No Flushers poster in a public bathroom with WAiNK, reply TRUTH to the text, then meet him at Frank's Waffles in Tequesta. When the job ends your maximum stamina goes up for good. It's open in any chapter and counts toward Strangers & Freaks."
  - q: "How do you unlock hurricanes in GTA 6?"
    a: "Finish Retest Freddie's Defenses. Freddie's compound is on the north side of Lake Leonida in Leonard County: get in once through the tunnel under the container on the east side and talk on the intercom, then come back when he messages and climb the water tower at the northeast corner. Hurricane weather is switched on once the job is done, and Freddie pays $10,000."
  - q: "How do you get into Freddie's bunker in GTA 6?"
    a: "The first time, use the loader on the east side to push the container off a hidden staircase and take the tunnel. On the second visit the tunnel is sealed, so climb the northeast water tower to its first level and jump onto the building inside. Watch for proximity mines (a red light and a beep) and shoot the drones from the roof."
  - q: "How many Strangers & Freaks are there in GTA 6?"
    a: "The trophy asks for 8 mission strands for strangers, and eight are known to count: Kirk, Kyle, Gellhorn's missing tourists, Yago, Pilot Pete, Dr. Merle, Freddie and Papa Audacity (the Unknown Stuntman). Finish the last job of each and the trophy pops."
  - q: "How do you start The Unknown Stuntman in GTA 6?"
    a: "In Chapter 3, go to the park at the south end of Ocean Beach (a poster in the Bayside apartment lobby points there) and talk to Papa Audacity in his white suit. Fix the gas canister on his ramp, light the fuse on his bike, then give your details to the AudaCity engineer. Jason or Lucia can do it."
  - q: "How do you beat x50 in Trial by Tire in GTA 6?"
    a: "On the car park roof you have just over a minute. Go for the big jumps in the middle of the roof first, which add x10 each early on, and throw in flips for extra. Then finish with the leap of faith off the corner ramp into the water."
  - q: "How do you get the AudaCity Sandking in GTA 6?"
    a: "Finish Quit Your Job, Sell Your House, Papa Audacity's third job: after the helicopter, the skydive and the stadium stunt you drive him to hospital in his truck, and it's yours afterwards. Register it to keep it, or sell it for over $10,000."
  - q: "When should you sell DacityCoin in GTA 6?"
    a: "During The Audacity, Papa Audacity's launch party and the last job of his strand. The coin is worth the most just before the bike jump into the rooftop pool, so sell it in the phone app then, for about $25,000."
  - q: "Which side missions don't count for Strangers & Freaks in GTA 6?"
    a: "Cal, Shirley, Moses, Billy, Andy, Yasmani, Tyrone, Eddie Acosta and Shayna's jobs don't count toward the trophy, even though several of them have a trophy of their own."
  - q: "How do you start the missing persons quest in GTA 6?"
    a: "In Chapter 2, look at one of the missing tourist posters in Port Gellhorn: in the Starlet Motel reception, or on the wall outside the 24/7 just south of Delights if you can't get into the motel any more. Then go to the abandoned building just north of Moses's shop and look through the three lockers."
  - q: "Where are the three missing tourists in GTA 6?"
    a: "All three are in and around Port Gellhorn: in the trunk of a pink car in the junkyard next to the Pay 'n' Spray, under the floor of an abandoned trailer by the water tower, and in a car sunk in a small pond on the edge of town. Report each body to the tip line."
  - q: "Where do you meet Kenton in GTA 6?"
    a: "At his Island Dreams shop in Key Lento, in the Chapter 1 mission Island Dreams. He runs treasure dives, and his final mission earns This Was No Boat Accident."
  - q: "How do you find Kenton's treasures in GTA 6?"
    a: "Take Kenton's boat, the Huntress, from Island Dreams in Key Lento, put on scuba gear and dive at one of his wrecks or dive sites. Equip the metal detector with L1 / LB, pan with the right stick and swim slowly toward the beeps until you can pick the piece up. Kenton pays a little per piece and much more for a full set."
  - q: "How many Kenton treasure sets are there in GTA 6?"
    a: "Seven, with 41 pieces between them, and the 100% checklist needs five. Five sets are on shipwrecks around Key Lento and count as underwater discoveries too; Belpoint Strait and the Bobcat Dive Site are open dive sites that don't. They open in stages: three after Island Dreams, three more once Kenton calls you to check in with him, and the last after his Chapter 3 job."
  - q: "Who is Randy in GTA 6?"
    a: "Randy Kozlov is a car fence with a dealership in Balsero. Lucia meets him in Chapter 3 when she delivers a car for Raul in Car Service. His first own job is a Zorrusso, which gets you the key cloner for locked high-end cars. His final mission earns Supply & Demand."
  - q: "How do you finish Randy's missions in GTA 6?"
    a: "Deliver cars from his wish list. After about five he gives you ¡Dale Mamba! (a Mamba GT in Leaf Links, $12,110), which also lets you register Advanced Tracker cars. When the whole list is in he sends you after a Truffade Thrax on Dalton Island (Mansion Thrax, $31,510). Then Delivery Day ($25,000) ends the strand with Supply & Demand, and Randy leaves you Raul's Tempesta in the Domestic Timbers garage."
  - q: "When does Kenton's last mission start in GTA 6?"
    a: "In Chapter 3. Jason needs at least three of Kenton's treasure sets done and the check-in at his shop that follows them; Kenton then gets back in touch, and the job starts at his shop in Key Lento. It earns This Was No Boat Accident and opens his seventh dive site."
  - q: "Which cars are on Randy's wish list in GTA 6?"
    a: "High-end sports and luxury cars: the Zorrusso, Coquette D10, Vitello, Infernus, Windsor Drop, Itali RSX, Jugular, Banshee, Growler, Paragon, Dubsta, Gauntlet Hellfire, Jubilee, Baller ST-D, Carbonizzare, Tempesta, Toros, Neon, Piccia Furia and Cypher. The list is on the map under Robberies, and Randy points you to the next car after each delivery."
  - q: "Where is the Infernus for Randy in GTA 6?"
    a: "In the police car park in Crosstown, Vice City, near the top of the building. Randy texts you a code for the stairwell door (5270). You need the key cloner, and the car has a tracker, so keep your speed up on the way to Randy."
  - q: "How do you unlock Yago's races in GTA 6?"
    a: "Finish Dealer's Choice in Chapter 3 as Jason. A few minutes later Manuel adds you to a group chat and the first street race shows up in Salton. Race Against Yago, the third job, is limited-time, so do it as soon as it appears."
  - q: "What are the safe codes for Yasmani's jewelry jobs in GTA 6?"
    a: "1680 at Casa de Empeno, 6815 at Richardson Jewelry and 0104 at Donovan Banks & Co."
  - q: "How do you start Kyle's missions in GTA 6?"
    a: "Read one of Kyle's flyers on the wall outside the Starlet Motel reception in Chapter 2. His first job, Check Out McKittrick Farm, then shows on the map."
  - q: "How do you start Eddie Acosta's party in GTA 6?"
    a: "Meet Eddie at the Y Hotel in Ocean Beach during the Chapter 4 story. A few in-game days later he texts an invite to a party in Heathcliff Heights. Go alone, as Jason or Lucia. It is a one-off side job and doesn't count toward Strangers & Freaks."
  - q: "What is the safe code in Passing the Torch in GTA 6?"
    a: "3718. The safe is in the back room of the hangar at the Sundown Private Airfield, behind the red door, next to Pete's KZ-59 assault rifle."
  - q: "How do you start Pilot Pete's missions in GTA 6?"
    a: "Pete phones Jason early in Chapter 4. His first job, The Last Mile: Passing the Torch, starts at the Sundown Private Airfield in Leonard County. The strand is Jason only and counts toward Strangers & Freaks."
  - q: "How do you start Andy's missions in GTA 6?"
    a: "Play as Lucia in Chapter 3. After Mami Knows Best and Car Service, Andy calls and then texts an address in Belville. The five jobs end with Puff Puff Pass, which unlocks Road to Rehabilitation."
  - q: "How do you start Kirk's Skunk Ape missions in GTA 6?"
    a: "Look for Kirk's skunk ape flyers in Chapter 1, in the Mariana County swamps around Hamlet. His three jobs only run at night: Search for the Skunk Ape, Search for Tree Effigies and, from Chapter 2, Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove, which is for Lucia only. The strand counts toward Strangers & Freaks."
  - q: "How do you get the tranquilizer rifle in GTA 6?"
    a: "It's the Mister Mangrove Laird 350, from Kirk's last job, Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove. You're given it as soon as you climb into Kirk's helicopter, so you keep it whichever ending you pick."
  - q: "How do you destroy a Kitty Litter lab in GTA 6?"
    a: "Head into the purple search area, find the building leaking pink gas and wreck the equipment inside with explosives or fire: a molotov, or a propane or gas canister you shoot. Tyrone pays $2,000 or $2,500 a lab through BuckMe. The labs aren't missable, and enough of them unlock Spin On This."
  - q: "What cars does Moses want in GTA 6?"
    a: "After Daily Driver, Moses asks for six vehicles, one at a time: a Declasse Yosemite 1500 ($1,850), a Karin Futo ($1,500), an Annis Hellion ($1,400), a BF Bandito ($3,350), a Declasse Vamos ($4,200) and a Western Nightblade ($6,310). Each pays more if it arrives in good condition, and the next request only comes once you've delivered the last one."
  - q: "How do you get Moses's Sultan in GTA 6?"
    a: "Deliver all six cars from Steal Vehicles for Moses. Moses then texts that he has a gift for you: open the smaller garage next to his shop in Port Gellhorn and take the Sultan inside. You can keep it by registering it, or sell it."
  - q: "What is the garage code for the Vamos in Steal Vehicles for Moses?"
    a: "2727. The Vamos is in a shed east of the house near Grayson; the code is on signs inside the main trailer, and the button to open the garage is on the right."
  - q: "How do you unlock The Lone Rider in GTA 6?"
    a: "Finish Billy's last job, Hometown Heroes, in Chapter 3. Before it come Thrillbillies, Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, Mall Rats (solo) and The Impaler."

<h2 id="cal">Cal's fishing challenge</h2>

Cal is Jason's fishing buddy from Key Lento. Once you've finished [Feng Shui](/walkthrough/feng-shui/) in Chapter 1, he texts Jason and dares him to land a bigger fish than his at a spot out in **Gambit Bay**, Mariana County. It is a single job, it's for **Jason**, and beating Cal earns **Baited My Tackle** and a boost to Focus.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h">Catch a Bigger Fish <span class="chip plain">Chapter 1 on</span> <span class="flag">Jason</span></div><span class="strand-pay">Focus boost + trophy</span><p>You need a boat. If Jason's own isn't at the house, take one: the docks by the Rusty Anchor usually have a couple of fishing boats tied up. Head to the marked spot, open the vehicle menu and drop the <b>anchor</b> so you stay put. Take the rod out of your weapon wheel (bottom-right slot), keep the basic <b>Small Saltwater Lure</b> on and cast. Cal wants a <b>stoplight parrotfish over 2 lbs</b>: anything smaller doesn't count, so keep casting until one bites. When you land one big enough, send it to Cal (X / A) to win.</p></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="merle">Dr. Merle and The Final Flush</h2>

Dr. Merle speaks for the **No Flushers**, a movement convinced that what you flush is being collected. His one job, **The Final Flush**, is open in any chapter for Jason or Lucia, counts toward **Strangers & Freaks** and ends with a **permanent rise in maximum stamina**, so you run and swim further for the rest of the game. That makes it worth doing early.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h">Find a No Flushers poster <span class="chip plain">Any chapter</span></div><span class="strand-pay">Starts the job</span><p>The handmade posters, with a QR code and a plea not to flush, hang in and around public bathrooms across the map, even ones you can't go into. Two to try: the bathroom block on the north tip of <b>Corsair Key</b> in Mariana County, and the campground on the east side of <b>Watson Bay</b>. Scan the poster with <b>WAiNK</b>. <h3 id="randy-list">Randy's wish list</h3>

The list sits on the map under **Robberies → Randy's Wish List**. Every car on it is a high-end model, most of them keyless, so bring the key cloner.

- **Ask Randy for a lead.** After each delivery he points you to one more car on the list. The cars also turn up parked around the city, and he takes a listed car from wherever you found it.
- **Expect a tracker.** Wish-list cars usually carry an Advanced Tracker, so keep your speed up all the way to his dealership in Balsero.
- **Bring it in clean.** Damage cuts the payout and a car in good condition earns a bonus. There's a gas station just north of Randy's showroom where you can fix a car up before you hand it over.
- **Not there?** Save and reload, and the cars in a showroom or street get swapped around.

<h2 id="bret">Bret and hunting</h2>

Bret's hunting business hands out Target Decks, hunts for specific animals. The 100% checklist needs all 15, and **Fair Game** is for a unique animal he asks for. The first three decks, with every animal and where to look, are on [fishing and hunting](/guides/fishing-and-hunting/#target-decks).

Jason meets him in Chapter 2: after [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/) Bret texts Jason about a deer hunt in Lummox County, *Veteran Hunted*. Go alone. It pays $500 and a unique hunting rifle, the Killtelligence SM-69, and opens more hunting jobs.

How hunting, clean kills and selling to Bret work is in [fishing, hunting and the animal log](/guides/fishing-and-hunting/#hunting).
