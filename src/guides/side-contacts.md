<h2 id="strangers-and-freaks">Strangers & Freaks</h2>

The silver **Strangers & Freaks** trophy asks you to finish 8 mission strands for strangers across Leonida. Not every contact counts: fences like Moses and Yasmani are part of the money side of the game rather than stranger strands. Check the trophy tracker before and after the last mission of a strand to see if it counted.

<h2 id="kenton">Kenton and the shipwrecks</h2>

Kenton's dives start in [Island Dreams](/walkthrough/island-dreams/). You search wrecks with a metal detector and sell what you find to him. The 100% checklist counts 5 of his sets and 33 underwater discoveries. His last mission earns **This Was No Boat Accident**.

<h2 id="randy">Randy and the key cloner</h2>

Randy wants specific cars. His first job in Chapter 3 has you steal a Zorrusso, and it gets you the **key cloner**, which opens locked high-end cars. His last mission earns **Supply & Demand**.

<h2 id="tyrone">Tyrone and the Kitty Litter labs</h2>

Tyrone buys drugs, and his strand is about shutting down an operation called Kitty Litter. It starts in Chapter 3. His last mission earns **Spin On This!**

<h2 id="bret">Bret and hunting</h2>

Bret's hunting business hands out Target Decks, hunts for specific animals. The 100% checklist needs all 15, and **Fair Game** is for a unique animal he asks for.

Jason meets him in Chapter 2: after [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/) Bret texts Jason about a deer hunt in Lummox County, *Veteran Hunted*. Go alone. It pays $500 and a unique hunting rifle, the Killtelligence SM-69, and opens more hunting jobs.
