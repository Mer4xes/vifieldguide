<h2 id="strangers-and-freaks">Strangers & Freaks</h2>

The silver **Strangers & Freaks** trophy asks you to finish 8 mission strands for strangers across Leonida. Not every contact counts: fences like Moses and Yasmani are part of the money side of the game rather than stranger strands. Check the trophy tracker before and after the last mission of a strand to see if it counted.

<h2 id="billy">Billy and the Thrillbilly crew</h2>

Billy runs a dirt-bike crew out of the Thrillbilly Mud Club. Either lead can do his jobs.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h"><a href="/walkthrough/thrillbillies/">Thrillbillies</a></div><span class="strand-pay">Up to $2,500</span><p>Opens during <a href="/walkthrough/coffee-break/">Coffee Break</a>. Buy outfits, rent a bike and race one of Billy's crew. Just don't finish last.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h"><a href="/walkthrough/twist-wrist-smash-grab/">Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab</a></div><span class="strand-pay">$2,000</span><p>Billy texts a few in-game hours later. Chase down an armoured van in Port Gellhorn and keep the Sanchez.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Mall Rats, The Impaler, Hometown Heroes</div><p>His next three jobs, in that order. Guides follow.</p></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="kyle">Kyle and McKittrick Farm</h2>

Kyle is a farmer in Kelly County who flies a crop duster. Read one of his flyers on the wall outside the Starlet Motel reception to put his first job on the map. Jason or Lucia can do all three, and the strand counts toward **Strangers & Freaks** whichever way it ends.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h">Check Out McKittrick Farm <span class="chip plain">Chapter 2</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$400</span><p>Kyle has hurt his hand and needs his fields dusted. Take off from the airstrip east of the motel, pick <b>Spray Chemicals</b> from the weapon wheel (hold L1/LB) and hold R1/RB over the marked field while Kyle drives below. Land and park where you started, then talk to him. He joins your phone contacts.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Collect Chemicals for Kyle <span class="chip plain">Chapter 2</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$1,000</span><p>Kyle texts about a day later. At a gang compound in Leonard County the chemicals in a white van cost $5,000. You can pay, or refuse and take the van by force: fight your way out, drive it to Kyle's barn and expect two stars and a chase on the way.</p><p>Afterwards you can eat Kyle's strawberries at the farm for a short boost to all stats.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Buddy? <span class="chip plain">Chapter 3</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$0 or $5,000</span><p>Kyle's last text comes a long time later, around the middle of Chapter 3. Go to McKittrick Farm and look for him. How it ends is your call, and only one ending pays.</p>

<h2 id="yago">Yago and the street races</h2>

Yago's strand is for Jason only and runs through Chapter 3 in Vice City. Each job arrives by message from Yago a few in-game days after the last one. Story missions in between speed it up, and a lock on his map icon means you have to wait. The strand counts toward **Strangers & Freaks**.

> **Watch out:** **Race Against Yago** is marked on the map with a limited-time hourglass. Do it as soon as it shows up, before carrying on with the story.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h">Join a Street Race</div><span class="strand-pay">Up to $3,000</span><p>A few minutes after <a href="/walkthrough/dealers-choice/">Dealer's Choice</a>, Manuel adds you to a group chat. Rent one of three cars in Salton ($600 to $900; the dearest one is the easiest to win with) and race. This is where you meet Yago.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Bumper to Bumper</div><span class="strand-pay">$500</span><p>Meet Yago at his shop in Little Cuba. He pays for the upgrades: pick the Custom Street Racing Build under Pro Builds, then Nitrous Boost under Performance. Follow him to the race, finish it, lose the police and meet him in the north of the city.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Race Against Yago <span class="flag">Limited time</span></div><span class="strand-pay">Up to $3,800</span><p>Back in Salton, talk to Santiago, choose the second race, <b>Freeway Run</b>, rent a car and race.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Brother to Brother</div><span class="strand-pay">Yago's car</span><p>Knock on the back door of El Rincon Notorio in Little Cuba. A deal in west Vice City goes wrong: fight back to the car staying close to Yago, then chase him when he drives off. You keep his car, to store or sell.</p>
<details class="spoiler"><summary><span class="sp-tag">Spoiler</span><span class="sp-what">How the strand ends</span><span class="sp-hint">Tap to reveal</span></summary><div class="sp-body"><p>Yago turns out to be an undercover cop who chose not to arrest Jason. His car flips at the end of the chase, and you get to try to save him or leave him. He dies either way.</p></div></details></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="yasmani">Yasmani's jewelry jobs</h2>

After [Boobie Time](/walkthrough/boobie-time/) in Chapter 3, Yasmani gets in touch from his shop on Ocean Beach. He wants one piece from each of three stores, in any order, and Jason or Lucia can do it. Each piece is in a safe; destroy the security footage before you leave. Bring everything back to his shop to finish.

| Store | What he wants | Safe code | Way in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casa de Empeno | Buffalo skull chain, back office | **1680** | Front only; the smallest store, so do it first |
| Richardson Jewelry | Diamond ring, northeast corner of the central room | **6815** | Back door with the Snap Gun |
| Donovan Banks & Co. | Amethyst necklace, middle of the long back room | **0104** | Back door with the Snap Gun |

> **Tip:** Hand the first piece in straight away. Yasmani gives you the **Snap Gun**, which opens doors without setting off the alarm, so the police take longer to arrive at the other two.

The three pieces are worth $20,000 between them. Smash the display cases at the front for more; at the last two stores you can also slip out the back quietly instead. Jewelry stores count toward the Smash 'N' Grabs line in the [100% checklist](/guides/100-percent-checklist/).

<h2 id="kenton">Kenton and the shipwrecks</h2>

Kenton's dives start in [Island Dreams](/walkthrough/island-dreams/). You search wrecks with a metal detector and sell what you find to him. The 100% checklist counts 5 of his sets and 33 underwater discoveries. His last mission earns **This Was No Boat Accident**.

<h2 id="randy">Randy and the key cloner</h2>

Randy wants specific cars. Lucia meets him in [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/), when she drops off Raul's Schafter at his dealership in Balsero, and from then on he buys stolen cars ([fences](/guides/fences/)). His first own job in Chapter 3, **[Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS](/walkthrough/steal-the-zorrusso-for-randy/)**, has you steal a Zorrusso, and the **key cloner** unlocks on your phone as it starts. The cloner opens and starts keyless high-end cars, and it opens Randy's wish list of 18 high-end cars that pay well in perfect condition. How to use it, and what to watch for with trackers, is in the [money guide](/guides/money/#key-cloner). His last mission earns **Supply & Demand**.

<h2 id="tyrone">Tyrone and the Kitty Litter labs</h2>

Tyrone buys drugs, and his strand is about shutting down an operation called Kitty Litter. It starts in Chapter 3 with the story missions [Tyrone](/walkthrough/tyrone/) and [Here, Kitty Kitty](/walkthrough/here-kitty-kitty/), after which he works as a [fence](/guides/fences/). His last mission earns **Spin On This!**

<h2 id="eddie">Eddie Acosta's party</h2>

Eddie is a one-off. You first run into him at the Y Hotel pool in Chapter 4, in the [pool scene straight after Another Life](/walkthrough/another-life/#step-6), and a few in-game days later he texts an invite to a party in Heathcliff Heights. Jason or Lucia can go, but on your own, not as a pair. He has no further jobs, so he doesn't count toward **Strangers & Freaks**.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h">Attend Eddie Acosta's Party <span class="chip plain">Chapter 4</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$0, or −$50,000</span><p>Head to the marker in the middle of Vice City. Walking into the lobby calls Eddie; wait for him by the elevator, then head up to the roof. What happens up there decides whether this job costs you money.</p>
<details class="spoiler"><summary><span class="sp-tag">Spoiler</span><span class="sp-what">What Eddie wants, and how to get out</span><span class="sp-hint">Tap to reveal</span></summary><div class="sp-body"><p>Eddie locks you out on the roof and asks for $50,000 for a crypto "investment". Pay and he lets you back in, and all you get is a worthless NFT picture. Don't pay. His door is bulletproof, so shooting at it won't help: take the bike on the roof, ride flat out over the solar panels to jump to the next building and take the elevator down there. If you left him alone, he is waiting by the elevator in the lobby and you can scare him off; if you shot at him, he has already run.</p></div></details></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="bret">Bret and hunting</h2>

Bret's hunting business hands out Target Decks, hunts for specific animals. The 100% checklist needs all 15, and **Fair Game** is for a unique animal he asks for.

Jason meets him in Chapter 2: after [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/) Bret texts Jason about a deer hunt in Lummox County, *Veteran Hunted*. Go alone. It pays $500 and a unique hunting rifle, the Killtelligence SM-69, and opens more hunting jobs.
