<h2 id="strangers-and-freaks">Strangers & Freaks</h2>

The silver **Strangers & Freaks** trophy asks you to finish 8 mission strands for strangers across Leonida. Not every contact counts: fences like Moses and Yasmani are part of the money side of the game rather than stranger strands. Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete's strands count; the other four aren't confirmed yet. Check the trophy tracker before and after the last mission of a strand to see if it counted.

<h2 id="kirk">Kirk and the Skunk Ape</h2>

Kirk is a cryptid hunter in the Mariana County swamps, chasing a creature he calls the Skunk Ape, or Mr. Mangrove. His flyers, around Hamlet in Chapter 1, are what get you started. All three jobs happen **at night**, so sleep in a bed to skip ahead if you need to, and bring a flashlight. The strand counts toward **Strangers & Freaks**, and the last job is for **Lucia only**, so it can't happen before Chapter 2.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h">Search for the Skunk Ape <span class="chip plain">Chapter 1</span> <span class="flag">Night</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$1,000</span><p>Meet Kirk at his truck in the swamp and follow his drone through the woods. When he stops at a nesting site, talk it through, then search the circle he marks: the feeding site in the middle, then the claw marks on a tree a little east of it. The creature bolts and you can't catch it. Look over the effigy hanging at the edge of the woods, then run for Kirk's truck when a huge boar turns up. It always catches you near the truck; Kirk drives it off and pays you.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Search for Tree Effigies <span class="flag">Night</span></div><span class="strand-pay">No cash</span><p>Kirk texts once some time has passed and marks a large search area in the swamps. Find the three effigies hanging in the trees, in any order. The job ends by itself after the third.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Help Kirk Find Mr. Mangrove <span class="chip plain">Chapter 2</span> <span class="flag">Lucia only</span> <span class="flag">Night</span></div><span class="strand-pay">Rifle, maybe $10,000</span><p>Kirk texts a few days after the effigies. Meet him at Cottonmouth Lake and get into his helicopter: you're handed the <b>Mister Mangrove Laird 350</b>, a tranquilizer rifle, on the spot. Follow the spotlight until the creature runs for an abandoned shack, then go in on foot to the shack on the right. What happens after you find him is best left unspoiled, but you'll spend a short stretch as some very small animals before Lucia gets a choice. Only one of the endings pays, and the strand counts whichever you pick.</p>
<details class="spoiler"><summary><span class="sp-tag">Spoiler</span><span class="sp-what">Who Mr. Mangrove is, and which ending pays</span><span class="sp-hint">Tap to reveal</span></summary><div class="sp-body"><p>Lucia breathes in spores in the shack and passes out; you play a frog, then a fly, and overhear that Mr. Mangrove is Harry Sherrick, a man who went missing ten years ago and was changed by the spores the way Kirk is being changed. If Lucia shoots Mr. Mangrove and carries him to the helicopter, Kirk sends $10,000 through BuckMe. The other choices pay nothing.</p></div></details></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="billy">Billy and the Thrillbilly crew</h2>

Billy runs a dirt-bike crew out of the Thrillbilly Mud Club. There are five jobs, and either lead can take them, with two exceptions: **Mall Rats** has to be done alone, and **Hometown Heroes** pulls in both Jason and Lucia. Billy's strand doesn't count toward **Strangers & Freaks**; its last job earns **The Lone Rider** instead.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h"><a href="/walkthrough/thrillbillies/">Thrillbillies</a> <span class="chip plain">Chapter 2</span></div><span class="strand-pay">Up to $2,500</span><p>Opens during <a href="/walkthrough/coffee-break/">Coffee Break</a>. Buy outfits, rent a bike and race one of Billy's crew. Just don't finish last.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h"><a href="/walkthrough/twist-wrist-smash-grab/">Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab</a> <span class="chip plain">Chapter 2</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$2,000</span><p>Billy texts a few in-game hours later. Chase down an armoured van in Port Gellhorn and keep the Sanchez.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Mall Rats <span class="chip plain">Chapter 3</span> <span class="flag">Solo</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$12,000</span><p>Billy gets back in touch well into Chapter 3, so expect a long gap. The marker is behind the Malibu in Ocean Beach. You ride with the crew through a car park and straight through a shopping mall to a jewelry store. Smash the cases, then open the safe in the back, either with an explosive or by waiting while Billy gets the code out of the staff. Stay on Billy's tail through the police chase and ride up into the back of the waiting van to finish.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">The Impaler <span class="chip plain">Chapter 3</span></div><span class="strand-pay">No cash</span><p>A few in-game days later Billy calls and Wyman texts; answer Wyman to put the job on the map. Climb into the police impound in Ambrosia and take the one black-and-white police car at the back, dealing with the two officers by it. The button to open the gate is on the left. You leave with three stars: follow the railway into the train tunnel, where the police won't follow. Then drive carefully to the drop-off and leave the car in the tunnel there.</p></li>
<li><div class="strand-h">Hometown Heroes <span class="chip plain">Chapter 3</span> <span class="flag">Jason &amp; Lucia</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$10,000</span><p>Billy texts a few in-game days after The Impaler. It starts at the Mud Club. Jason takes the stolen police car onto the raceway and rams the racers, then Lucia rides with Billy to the race office, where there's a small open safe to empty. A fight follows as Lucia; later, as Jason, you drive to a sugar factory, fight your way upstairs to Lucia and get out on anything with wheels. Finishing it unlocks <b>The Lone Rider</b>.</p>
<details class="spoiler"><summary><span class="sp-tag">Spoiler</span><span class="sp-what">How Billy's strand ends</span><span class="sp-hint">Tap to reveal</span></summary><div class="sp-body"><p>A rival gang, the Final Chapter, is waiting. Ricky is taken hostage and killed, Ian dies in the shootout and Sara doesn't make it out of the factory. Billy is left at a gas station and you walk off with $10,000 of the money.</p></div></details></li>
</ol>

<h2 id="kyle">Kyle and McKittrick Farm</h2>

Kyle is a farmer in Kelly County who flies a crop duster. Read one of his flyers on the wall outside the Starlet Motel reception to put his first job on the map. Jason or Lucia can do all three, and the strand counts toward **Strangers & Freaks** whichever way it ends.

<ol class="strand">
<li><div class="strand-h">Check Out McKittrick Farm <span class="chip plain">Chapter 2</span></div><span class="strand-pay">$400</span><p>Kyle has hurt his hand and needs his fields dusted. Take off from the airstrip east of the motel, pick <b>Spray Chemicals</b> from the weapon wheel (hold L1/LB) and hold R1/RB over the marked field while Kyle drives below. Land and park where you started, then talk to him. He joins your phone contacts.</p></li>

<h2 id="andy">Andy and Cellerica</h2>

Andy is part of **Cellerica**, a wellness group with a compound in Belville and a line of vape sticks called Manifesticks. The strand is for **Lucia only**: in Chapter 3, once Lucia has done [Mami Knows Best](/walkthrough/mami-knows-best/) and [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/), Andy phones her and then texts an address in Belville, which puts the first job on the map. There are five jobs, and between most of them you have to wait for a call or a text while you play other Lucia missions. Andy's strand doesn't count toward **Strangers & Freaks**; the last job earns **Road to Rehabilitation**.

<li><div class="strand-h">Pay the Cellerica Fee</div><span class="strand-pay">Costs $5,000</span><p>A message from Andy, no location. Open the BuckMe app and send Andy the $5,000. You get it back at the end of the strand.</p></li>

<li><div class="strand-h">Read the Label</div><span class="strand-pay">$1,800</span><p>Cecilia gets in touch after a while. In the north of Vice City, knock out the two people by the Cellerica van, drive it the short way to the warehouse in Belville and park in the yellow marker.</p></li>

<h2 id="yasmani">Yasmani's jewelry jobs</h2>

After [Boobie Time](/walkthrough/boobie-time/) in Chapter 3, Yasmani gets in touch from his shop on Ocean Beach. He wants one piece from each of three stores, in any order, and Jason or Lucia can do it. Each piece is in a safe; destroy the security footage before you leave. Bring everything back to his shop to finish.

| Store | What he wants | Safe code | Way in |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casa de Empeno | Buffalo skull chain, back office | **1680** | Front only; the smallest store, so do it first |
| Richardson Jewelry | Diamond ring, northeast corner of the central room | **6815** | Back door with the Snap Gun |
| Donovan Banks & Co. | Amethyst necklace, middle of the long back room | **0104** | Back door with the Snap Gun |

> **Tip:** Hand the first piece in straight away. Yasmani gives you the **Snap Gun**, which opens doors without setting off the alarm, so the police take longer to arrive at the other two.

The three pieces are worth $20,000 between them. Smash the display cases at the front for more; at the last two stores you can also slip out the back quietly instead. Jewelry stores count toward the Smash 'N' Grabs line in the [100% checklist](/guides/100-percent-checklist/).

<h2 id="tyrone">Tyrone and the Kitty Litter labs</h2>

Tyrone buys drugs, and his strand is about shutting down an operation called Kitty Litter. It starts in Chapter 3 with the story missions [Tyrone](/walkthrough/tyrone/) and [Here, Kitty Kitty](/walkthrough/here-kitty-kitty/), after which he works as a [fence](/guides/fences/) and starts sending you after labs. Destroy enough of them and he gives you his last job, **Spin On This**, which earns **Spin On This!** The strand doesn't count toward **Strangers & Freaks**.

<h3 id="kitty-litter-labs">How to take out a lab</h3>

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Get a location.</b> Tyrone or Dook send you labs as purple search areas, and you can also stumble on them yourself. A cook you hold up will sometimes give away another lab.</li>
<li><b>Find the building.</b> Inside the area, look for the one leaking pink gas. Many are locked garages or trailers: pick the lock with the Snap Gun or break the door.</li>
<li><b>Wreck the equipment.</b> Explosives or fire both work: throw a molotov, or shoot a propane or gas canister near the gear. Careful with the cooks, who can set the room alight themselves.</li>
<li><b>Get paid.</b> Tyrone sends $2,000 or $2,500 through BuckMe. Labs are not missable and stay open in free roam.</li>
</ol>
