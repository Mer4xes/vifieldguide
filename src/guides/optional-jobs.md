Between the story missions, Leonida keeps throwing small jobs at you: errands for the neighbours, a call from Lucia, a bag of drugs at the bottom of the sea. None of them are needed to finish the story, but several pay well, a few hand you a car or a bike, and six of them don't wait. This page lists every optional mission that opens between story missions, in the order they come up in each chapter. The side strands of the contacts and strangers (Kenton, Kirk, Moses, Andy and the rest) have their own guide: [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/).

<h2 id="chapter-1">Chapter 1 jobs, step by step</h2>

<h3 id="lost-drugs">Find the Lost Drugs</h3>

**Jason · Mariana County · after [Down to Earth](/walkthrough/down-to-earth/)**

A call comes in once Down to Earth is done: a load of drugs has gone missing in the swamps, and it's on Jason to bring it back. Four search zones appear on the map, strung out from south to north. Every package gives off a beep and a flashing yellow light, so slow down and listen once you're inside a circle. Try not to die, and don't lose the bag on the way back.

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Recreation Area (the southern zone), 2 packages.</b> One is caught up in a tree by the beach, where three men are waiting. Deal with them, then shoot the package down. The other floats at the far end of the docks, in the south of the zone.</li>
<li><b>Burned Houses (second from the south), 3 packages.</b> The first is on the south shore, in front of the second burned-out house (come in from the east). The second is inside the lone burned house on the north shore. The third is at the south-west edge of the circle, in a blue paddling pool next to a water slide.</li>
<li><b>Pelican Valley (third from the south), 2 packages.</b> Alligators are lying on them. Shoot or scare them off; both packages are on the ground where they were, close together.</li>
<li><b>Thorpe Adventures (the northern zone).</b> The map promises two more, but someone got here first: there's an empty, torn-open package outside a shed. Kill the two men inside and take the <b>half brick of cocaine</b> they're holding.</li>
<li><b>Drop it off at Donnie's trailer.</b> That ends the job. There's no fixed reward: what you bring back counts as goods for a <a href="/guides/fences/">fence</a>.</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="clock-out">Clock Out</h3>

**Jason · Key Lento · after [Day Release](/walkthrough/day-release/) · timed**

Some time after Day Release, during the day, Lucia calls to say her shift ended early and asks Jason to come and get her. **Accept the call**: turning her down skips the job. The call doesn't come in every game; if it never does, just carry on with the story.

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Watch the clock.</b> You have until <b>4 PM</b> in-game, or the job fails. The time left shows on the minimap, the phone and the map.</li>
<li><b>Bring a car.</b> Lucia is at the <b>Key Lento Community Center</b>, where she works. Turn up on foot and she won't walk home, so arrive in a vehicle; any car you take off the street is fine.</li>
<li><b>Drive her home.</b> Take her back to the house. She gets out on her own, and things between the two of them improve.</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="donnie-drugs">Donnie's Lost Drugs</h3>

**Jason · off Key Lento · after [Swamped](/walkthrough/swamped/) · $1,200**

Brian has news: Donnie sank his boat off **Corsair Key** with drugs still on board, and he wants them back. The marker is out at sea, off the north coast of Key Lento. Swimming there takes ages, so take a boat; one tied up on the coast nearby will do.

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Head for the pink search area</b> by boat.</li>
<li><b>Dive in its south-east part.</b> The sunken boat is lying on the seabed.</li>
<li><b>Open the compartment</b> at the back of the wreck and take the bag.</li>
<li><b>Take it to Brian's lockup.</b> He pays <b>$1,200</b>, and the job is done.</li>
</ol>

> **Tip:** Diving here is good practice for [Kenton's treasure sets](/guides/side-contacts/#kenton-treasure), which are all in Mariana County waters too.

<h2 id="chapter-2">Chapter 2 jobs, step by step</h2>

> **Note:** Chapter 2 also has *Take Care of Jason*, which isn't optional and has no marker. It sits under Story on the map: come back to the Starlet Motel each day to check on Jason, and each visit helps the relationship. It wraps itself up later in the chapter, after [Breakfast Run](/walkthrough/breakfast-run/), and can't be missed.

<h3 id="pick-up-gun">Pick Up A Gun</h3>

**Lucia · Port Gellhorn · after [Girls' Trip](/walkthrough/girls-trip/)**

One of the shortest jobs in the game. After Girls' Trip, a marker appears on **Gellhorn Outfitters**, the Ammu-Nation in Port Gellhorn. Go in and either buy a weapon or open the green gun locker and take out one you already own. That's it. Prices and stock are on the [shops guide](/guides/shops/#ammu-nation-gellhorn-outfitters).

<h3 id="impound">Impounded Vehicle</h3>

**Jason or Lucia · Port Gellhorn · after [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/)**

After Breakfast Run the police take Lucia's car, and once Coffee Break is done it's one of the four jobs on the map. It works as the game's lesson in getting an impounded car back.

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Find the green car icon</b> next to the Port Gellhorn police station.</li>
<li><b>Drive straight through the fence.</b> Two officers on the lot see you, and you get two stars.</li>
<li><b>Jump in Lucia's car and lose the police.</b> Once they've given up, the car is hers again.</li>
</ol>

> **Watch out:** A personal car you use in a crime and leave inside the police search area gets found and towed. Stolen cars don't. Later, the Scooter Bros app gets an impounded car back for a fee: see the [cars guide](/guides/cars/).

<h2 id="chapter-3">Chapter 3 jobs, step by step</h2>

<h3 id="get-wheels">Get Your Wheels</h3>

**Jason or Lucia · Vice City · after [Dealer's Choice](/walkthrough/dealers-choice/)**

The police recognised both cars after the escape at the end of Chapter 2, and both are in the impound. This job has no marker: it shows up under Story on the map, and you can finish it anywhere, in either of two ways.

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Get a car back</b> through the <b>Scooter Bros</b> app. It brings it out of the impound and delivers it to you, for a fee.</li>
<li><b>Or register another car</b> at a <a href="/guides/shops/#pay-n-spray">Pay 'n' Spray</a>. You need a free space for it; if you're at the limit, buy a <a href="/guides/garages/">garage</a> first.</li>
</ol>

One car is enough to finish it, though you can get both back if you like. How registering and trackers work: [cars guide](/guides/cars/).

<h2 id="chapter-4">Chapter 4 jobs, step by step</h2>

<h3 id="its-a-date">It's A Date!</h3>

**Jason and Lucia together · Stockyard, Vice City · after [Heavy Lifting](/walkthrough/heavy-lifting/)**

A night out, no guns involved. Once Heavy Lifting is done it's on the map at the **Courtyard Bar** in Stockyard. Go with both of them, buy a drink inside, dance if you feel like it, then walk back out to the street. Leaving the bar finishes it, and the two of them grow a little closer. Skipping it changes nothing else.

<h3 id="bad-driver">Bad Driver!</h3>

**Jason or Lucia · Millport, Lummox County · after [Party Hard](/walkthrough/party-hard/) · missable**

Andrea gets in touch after Party Hard: she wants Felipe found. This one is gone for good once [Power Play](/walkthrough/power-play/) starts, so do it early.

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Go to the marked bar in Millport</b> and walk towards the back. Felipe comes out of the bathroom and runs out the back door into the woods.</li>
<li><b>Chase him down.</b> Shots to the legs or back slow him, and a few punches make him give up.</li>
<li><b>Question him.</b> Once he's stopped, aim at him (L2 / LT) to talk.</li>
<li><b>Decide what happens to him.</b> Kill him and Andrea pays <b>$20,000</b>. Let him go and there's no money now, but he gets back to you later with another optional job, <b>Collect Felipe's Gift</b>, which ends with his car.</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="drequan-gift">Dre'Quan's Gift</h3>

**Jason or Lucia · La Perle, Vice City · a few days after [Photo/Shoot](/walkthrough/photo-shoot/)**

A few in-game days after Photo/Shoot, Dre'Quan gets in touch and marks a spot on the map: he's taken a bike off a promoter and left it for you. Go to the alley behind the **[Pay 'n' Spray in La Perle](/guides/shops/#pay-n-spray-la-perle)** and you'll find **KD's SFB 1000**, a race bike. Keep it or sell it. If you don't have a fast bike yet, keep it: it is very quick once it's been upgraded at a [mod shop](/guides/shops/#mod-shops), and a [garage](/guides/garages/) keeps it safe.
