## The order we'd suggest

This is only a suggestion; nothing after the story is locked to an order.

1. **Look at the [100% checklist](/guides/100-percent-checklist/)** and tick off what the game already shows as done.
2. **Work through the free-roam part of the [100% route](/guides/100-percent-route/#free-roam).** It groups what is left by area so you aren't crossing the map for one item.
3. **Fill in the county lists** in [collectibles by area](/collectibles/#by-area) as you pass through each place.
4. **Do the [stunts](/guides/stunts/) and [challenges](/guides/challenges/)** when you want a break from collecting. Many cheats unlock from these too.
5. **Finish what the [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/) still have**, and the [gang operations](/guides/gang-operations/) you haven't hit.
6. **Check the [trophy list](/trophies/)** last. Reaching 100% earns A Long Road, the last gold on the way to the platinum.

## Money for the clean-up

What you have in **Family Savings** comes through the ending; money held outside it is lost once the ending starts, so move it across before you start [Protective Custody](/walkthrough/protective-custody/#before-you-start). You'll still want cash for cars, garages and gear. The [money guide](/guides/money/) has the repeatable earners, and the $250,000 cheat is there if you don't mind giving up trophies on that save.
