<h2 id="overview">What opens when, and what 100% wants</h2>

| Robbery | Opens | In the game | 100% needs | Trophy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Pawn shops](#smash) | Chapter 2, after [Coffee Break](/walkthrough/coffee-break/) | 15 | 34 of the 35 Smash 'n' Grabs | |
| [Jewelry stores](#smash) | Chapter 3, after [Yasmani's jewelry jobs](/guides/side-contacts/#yasmani) | 20 | (same 34) | [Sneakthief](/trophies/sneakthief/): one without being seen |
| [Small banks](#banks) | Chapter 3, after [Credit Check](/walkthrough/credit-check/) | 14 | All 22 bank jobs | |
| [Large banks](#vaults) | End of Chapter 4, after That's Where The Money Is | 8 | (same 22), plus all 8 vaults | [Maximum Withdrawal](/trophies/maximum-withdrawal/): 5 vaults |
| [Payday loans](#payday) | Chapter 3, after [Credit Check](/walkthrough/credit-check/) | 7 | All 7 | |

All three lists feed [A Long Road](/trophies/a-long-road/), the 100% trophy. Credit Check also hands you the [Dye Pack Jammer](/guides/equipment/#dye-pack-jammer), which keeps the marked bills from banks and payday loans clean so a [fence](/guides/fences/) will take them. Fencing $250,000 of them earns **Dime on the Dollar**.

> **Tip:** On the map, the **Robberies** filter (money bag icon) has a tab for each kind. Bright green means done, dark green means there's still something to take. The map only shows a location once you've uncovered that part of it.

<h2 id="smash">Smash 'n' Grabs: jewelry stores and pawn shops</h2>
<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Smash the cases</b> and grab what's inside. The alarm goes off, so be quick.</li>
<li><b>Empty the safe in the back room.</b> The cases alone may not mark the store as done.</li>
<li><b>Get away and sell the haul</b> to a <a href="/guides/fences/">fence</a>. Yasmani takes jewelry.</li>
</ol>
<p class="stunt-how">35 stores, 34 for the checklist, so one can be skipped. Payouts are the range for the whole store. For <a href="/trophies/sneakthief/">Sneakthief</a>, pick one with a back door you can open with the <a href="/guides/equipment/#snap-gun">Snap Gun</a>, like Yasmani's second and third stores.</p>

<h3 class="chal-h" id="vaults">Large banks and their vaults <small>End of Chapter 4 · 8 vaults</small></h3>
<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Play That's Where The Money Is</b>, the optional mission that turns up at the very end of Chapter 4. It walks you through the first one.</li>
<li><b>Tail an armored security van</b> until it stops at a bank. A guard carries a case of cash into the vault, and the vault stays open for a short while.</li>
<li><b>Go in while it's open</b> and take the vault, the cash drawer and the safe.</li>
<li><b>Expect four stars at once.</b> A fast motorbike gets you through traffic; swap to another vehicle once you're clear so the police lose your description.</li>
</ol>
<p class="stunt-how">Tired of hunting for vans? Yasmani sells security van tips for $10,000 (<a href="/guides/money/#vaults">when he starts</a>). Five vaults earn <a href="/trophies/maximum-withdrawal/">Maximum Withdrawal</a>; the checklist wants all eight.</p>
