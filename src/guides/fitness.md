Jason and Lucia train separately, so each needs their own sessions. Check where they stand in the **Fitness** app on the phone, or in the pause menu under **Jason & Lucia**: pick one and scroll down the profile. Training also shows on the body, since workouts build muscle and keep weight down.

<h2 id="workouts">How workouts work</h2>

Walk up to a machine or a set of weights and follow the prompt. A few rules apply to every session:

- **Finish the workout.** Quit halfway and the gains are lost.
- **Watch your stamina.** The gauge sits top left. If it runs out, the workout ends there.
- **There's a limit per session.** You can't lift all day. When a stat has gained all it can for now, the game tells you; rest before you go again.
- **Have protein afterwards.** It speeds up recovery, so you can train again sooner. There's a protein shake in the fridge at home, and gyms sell protein bars.

<h3 id="controls">Strength controls</h3>

| Exercise | What to do |
|---|---|
| Bench press, deadlift, pull-ups, squats | Hold **both triggers** to lift, ease off slowly to lower, then squeeze both triggers fully for the next rep |
| Last reps | Tap **X** (**A** on Xbox) fast when the prompt shows |
| Kettlebell swings | Squeeze both triggers to swing up, let go, and start the next swing when the bell is as far back between the legs as it goes |

> **Tip:** **Balanced Workout** wants every type of gym equipment in one day. Do it at a gym, because the weights at home don't include treadmills or bikes.

<h2 id="home">Training at home</h2>

Jason's house has **weights** outside, behind the house, from the first chapter, free to use whenever you like. That covers Strength. There's no cardio equipment at home, so for Cardio go for a run, a bike ride or a swim, or use a gym.

<h2 id="cardio">Cardio out in the world</h2>

- **Run.** Keep going for a while and a notification tells you Cardio went up.
- **Cycle** or **swim**. Both count, and both have challenges of their own: **Pro Cyclist** (15 miles in a day) and **Pro Swimmer** (3 miles in a day).
- Once Cardio is maxed for the session, more running won't add anything until you've rested.

<h2 id="focus">Raising Focus</h2>

Focus has no gym equipment. Raise it with:

1. **Shooting range challenges.** Every challenge you finish or quit raises Focus. The quickest is **Market Range Beginner** at **Rodgers Ranch** on the north-east edge of Port Gellhorn, Kelly County: buy the **$5 Waiver Pass** from the clerk, play the short challenge, quit, and start it again. Repeat as often as you like. All the ranges are on [races and ranges](/guides/races-and-ranges/#ranges).
2. **Golf** and **pool**. Playing either raises it too. Pool opponents are listed on [races and ranges](/guides/races-and-ranges/#pool).
3. **Fishing.** Every fish you land raises Focus. See [fishing](/guides/fishing-and-hunting/#fishing).

<h2 id="gyms">All nine gyms</h2>

Every gym is in Vice City. Once you've uncovered that part of the map they show as **blue dumbbells**, and the map filter lists them under **Activities › Fitness › Gyms**. You pay the membership **once** and can train there as often as you like. Joining any gym completes the **Gym Member** challenge.

> **Note:** Lucia trains at **Ajax Gym** in [Making Gainz](/walkthrough/making-gainz/), a Chapter 4 story mission.

<h2 id="partner">Training Jason and Lucia together</h2>

At a gym you can bring your partner along. While you work out as one, the other finishes their own workout at the same time, so one session trains both. That makes the paired challenges and the **Personal Best** trophy, which wants both at maximum Strength at once, much quicker.

> **Watch out:** After [Making Gainz](/walkthrough/making-gainz/) in Chapter 4, Jason and Lucia can't be seen together in public until [VCIA Takedown](/walkthrough/vcia-takedown/). Do your paired gym sessions before that mission, or after the heist.

<h2 id="boosts">Boosts that stay</h2>

Two side stories raise maximum stats for good, on top of training:

- **The alien rescue** raises maximum health, stamina and focus for both Jason and Lucia. Step by step on [mysteries](/guides/mysteries/#aliens).
- **The Final Flush**, Dr. Merle's strange job, raises maximum stamina. See [side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#merle).
