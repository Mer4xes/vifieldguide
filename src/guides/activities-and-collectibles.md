> **Note:** First pass. Every count here comes from a trusted guide or the official trophy list. Details that are still unclear are left out until they are confirmed.

<h2 id="meditation">Meditation</h2>

Jason and Lucia can meditate at set spots around Leonida. There are **18**. The **Positive Mental Attitude** trophy needs every one of them; the 100% checklist settles for 15.

You can meditate more than once, and Jason and Lucia can both meditate at the same spot. The **Fast Forward** cheat unlocks after 50 meditations. Your count is under Progress, Collections, Exploration, Meditations.

> **Watch out:** Unconfirmed, but worth knowing. One guide reports that a meditation Lucia offers at Jason's house during Chapter 1 may count toward the trophy and may not come back later. Accept it if she asks, just in case.

<h2 id="collectible-cards">Collectible cards</h2>

There are **480 cards** in **18 sets**, hidden around the map. The sets are themed, including sports teams like the **Vice City Flamingos** (24 cards). **Don't Touch My Deck** needs 3 complete sets; the 100% checklist needs 15. Completing the Vice City Flamingos set also unlocks the **Bubblegum** cheat.

<h2 id="waink">WAiNK scans</h2>

WAiNK is the phone app that scans things. Point it at landmarks, statues and art to log them; **You WAiNKer!** needs 100 locations, and so does the 100% checklist. The same app scans cars, which is how you discover vehicles for **Whip It Good** (100) and the checklist (200 base models). See [Unwanted Attention](/walkthrough/unwanted-attention/) for your first scan.

<h2 id="drone-races">Top Fun drone races</h2>

Top Fun runs drone races at **14 locations**. **Top Fun** (the trophy) needs a win at every location. The 100% checklist counts **42 drone time trials**, so each location has more than one race.

<h2 id="shooting-ranges">Shooting ranges</h2>

There are two ranges, **Griffin Shooting Range** and **Rodgers Ranch**, and their challenges hand out **42 gold medals** in total. **The Shootist** needs every gold, and the checklist wants the same 42.

<h2 id="stunts">Stunts</h2>

There are **397 stunts**: ramp jumps, flying under bridges, base jumps and more. **Cunning Stunts** needs 250 and the 100% checklist 300, so the trophy pops on the way. 50 stunt jumps also unlock the **unlimited nitrous** cheat.

<h2 id="cocaine-flamingos">Cocaine Flamingos</h2>

Pink flamingo ornaments placed around the map. Shoot one and it drops a parcel of cocaine you can sell to a fence. There are **125**; the 100% checklist wants 100 of them, and 50 unlock the **Inflatable Flamingo** spawn cheat.

<h2 id="underwater">Diving and underwater discoveries</h2>

Treasure diving opens with **Kenton** in [Island Dreams](/walkthrough/island-dreams/). Dive with the metal detector: it signals green and beeps faster as you close in. The 100% checklist counts 33 underwater discoveries and 5 of Kenton's 7 sets, and Kenton's final mission earns **This Was No Boat Accident**.

<h2 id="reading">Magazines, books and comics</h2>

Magazines, books and comics are found around Leonida. There are 34 different ones and the 100% checklist needs 30. Duplicates don't count.

<h2 id="races">Races</h2>

**Earn Cash, Not Kudos** needs wins in 20 different races; the 100% checklist wants 30 unique race wins plus 10 race challenge golds. Venues include the **Gellhorn Raceway**, the **Thrillbilly Mud Club** and **Boone Speedway**. Ten wins across those three unlock the **Repair Vehicle** cheat.

<h2 id="video-games">Video games</h2>

Four games are playable inside GTA 6:

- **Tenebrae Lux**, a puzzle game
- **Street Trash III**, a shooter
- **Time Jackers: Rewind**, a twin-stick shooter
- **Bang For Your Buck**, a light-gun hunting game

The first three are on the console at home ([Feng Shui](/walkthrough/feng-shui/) introduces two of them). Bang For Your Buck is only on arcade cabinets. **Stick Fiddler** needs one arcade game completed, and the 100% checklist wants all 4 played.

<h2 id="pool-golf-basketball">Pool, mini golf and basketball</h2>

The 100% checklist wants 5 of the 7 pool opponents beaten (look in bars), 2 of the 3 mini golf courses played (**Dino Golf Challenge**, **Frontier Adventure Golf** and **King Gator Adventure Golf**) and 90 of the 100 basketball hoops. **Master of None** asks you to try each of 9 activity types once, and these are among the easiest to tick off.

<h2 id="challenges">Challenges</h2>

Challenges are split into eight categories: Combat, Criminal, Exploration, Fitness, Shopping, Sports, Survival and Vehicle. **One Last Thing** needs 50 and the 100% checklist 70. Several vehicle cheats unlock when you finish challenges in a category.

<h2 id="fences">Fences</h2>

In Chapter 2, Moses buys cars without a tracker from [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/) on, and **Dunk's trailer** in Port Gellhorn takes stolen goods from [Slam Dunk](/walkthrough/slam-dunk/) on. Dunk also marks two robbery targets on your map the day after.

Stolen goods go to fences. **Moses** and **Randy** take cars; **Tyrone**, **Yasmani** and **Dunk** take narcotics, jewelry and marked bills. Brian's lockup works as a fence from [Lab Fees](/walkthrough/lab-fees/) in Chapter 1, and the drop behind Donnie's trailer in Key Lento takes narcotics from [Clean Up After Donnie](/walkthrough/clean-up-after-donnie/). **Dime on the Dollar** needs $250,000 in Marked Bills laundered, the same figure as on the 100% checklist, which also wants $100,000 each in narcotics and jewelry and $200,000 in vehicles fenced.

<h2 id="garages">Garages and Scooter Bros</h2>

Cars come into play in Chapter 2. [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/) gives Lucia the Slim Jim and the **Scooter Bros** app and lets her register one car as her own. After [Daily Driver](/walkthrough/daily-driver/) Moses hands over a free garage in Port Gellhorn, and the **Vice City Real Estate** app opens for buying more.

**Little Boxes** needs all 8 garage properties bought. **On the Spot** needs 10 vehicles registered in the Scooter Bros app, the service that delivers your car to you.

<h2 id="fishing-hunting-gyms">Fishing, hunting and gyms</h2>

- **Fishing:** 100 fish unlock the **Fisheye** cheat. **Baited My Tackle** is for beating Cal in a fishing challenge.
- **Hunting:** Bret runs a hunting business and hands out Target Decks. The 100% checklist needs all 15, and **Fair Game** is for hunting a unique animal for him.
- **Gyms:** 100 workouts unlock the **Body Armor** cheat. See [Gyms](/activities/#gyms) for what Rockstar showed before launch.

<h2 id="cheats">Cheats and trophies</h2>

Cheats in GTA 6 are earned in the world, not typed in from the start. Two guides say that using a cheat blocks trophies, though they disagree on the details. Make a manual save before you switch one on.
