<h2 id="keep-it">Keep the money you make</h2>

The first rule matters more than any method: **cash in your pocket is not safe.** You lose it when you die or get arrested.

- **BuckMe** arrives in [Buck Me](/walkthrough/buck-me/) in Chapter 1. Deposit cash at any ATM; the app's ATM Finder points you to the nearest one.
- **Family Savings** in the same app can never be lost, but it can't be taken out again either. Only put in what you won't need.
- **End of Chapter 1:** the cash Jason carries is lost in the run-up to the end of the chapter. Bank it before [Recalibration](/walkthrough/recalibration/#before-you-start).
- **End of Chapter 2:** [Checking Out](/walkthrough/checking-out/#before-you-start) won't start until you have **$15,000**, and it should be in BuckMe rather than your pocket.

<h2 id="cars">Stolen cars</h2>

**Moses** in Port Gellhorn is your first steady income. From the Chapter 2 mission [Moses](/walkthrough/moses/) he buys stolen cars, $1,000 and up for a decent one, with a cooldown of about two minutes between sales.

- He only takes cars **without a tracker**. Scan a car with WAiNK first to see what it has.
- He turns down cars stolen too close to his garage. Pick one up off the main road through Port Gellhorn instead and drive it over.

In **Chapter 3** two more outlets open. Cars with a **Standard Tracker** can be sold at any **Pay 'n' Spray**, about $4,000 or more for a good one. And **Randy** at his dealership in Balsero takes stolen cars from [Car Service](/walkthrough/car-service/) on; the [fences guide](/guides/fences/#cars) has who buys what.

<h2 id="key-cloner">The key cloner and Randy's wish list</h2>

The **key cloner** opens and starts **keyless** high-end cars, the ones the slim jim can't handle. It unlocks automatically when you start Randy's job **Clonin' Keys, Makin' GS** ([Steal the Zorrusso for Randy](/walkthrough/steal-the-zorrusso-for-randy/)), about halfway through Chapter 3, and goes straight onto your phone.

1. Scan the car with **WAiNK** (hold up on the D-pad). It tells you whether the car is keyless.
2. Stand by one of the car's doors, press interact and pick **Clone Key**.
3. Move the stick until you find the sweet spot. It speeds the clone up.

> **Watch out:** Cars that need the cloner often carry an **Advanced Tracker**, which tells the police where you are as soon as you slow down. Keep your speed up, or look for a car with a Standard Tracker or none. A car with a Standard Tracker can't be modded until you register it.

With the cloner, Randy's **wish list** opens: **18 high-end sports cars** that turn up around Vice City. Delivered in perfect condition they pay $30,000 or more each, so drive them in carefully. His last job earns **Supply & Demand** ([Randy on side contacts](/guides/side-contacts/#randy)).

<h2 id="jewelry">Jewelry stores</h2>

From Yasmani's first jobs in Chapter 3 ([Yasmani's jewelry jobs](/guides/side-contacts/#yasmani)), jewelry stores are some of the best money in the chapter: roughly $10,000 to $25,000 a store. You can hit several back to back, and the **back-room safe** is worth the extra time. Sell the jewelry to Yasmani. Robbing one without being seen earns **Sneakthief** ([trophies](/trophies/)).

<h2 id="bank-jobs">Small bank jobs</h2>

There are **22 bank jobs**: 14 small banks and 8 large ones with a vault. The **14 small banks** open after the Chapter 3 story mission **[Credit Check](/walkthrough/credit-check/)**, where you meet Raul's crew. The same mission gets you the **Dye Pack Jammer**, which you need before the marked bills from a bank are worth anything to a fence. Bank jobs and the **7 payday loan** jobs both open at this point.

- Banks show as **green icons** once that part of the map is uncovered. To see only them, filter the map by **Robberies → Bank Jobs**: bright green is done, dark green is still to do.
- Each small bank has a **cash drawer** and a **safe**. Loot both, or the bank doesn't count as complete.
- Expect roughly **$5,000 to $45,000** a bank.
- Where they are: 9 in Vice City (Vice-Dale County), 3 in Mariana County, 1 in Leonard County and 1 in Kelly County.

Marked bills go to [Yasmani](/guides/fences/#jewelry). Laundering $250,000 of them earns **Dime on the Dollar**.

<h2 id="vaults">Large bank vaults</h2>

The 8 large banks are where the big money is, roughly **$65,000 to $205,000** each. Their vaults open with the optional mission **That's Where The Money Is…** at the very end of Chapter 4, which also teaches the method.

1. Find a **security van** and tail it until it stops at a bank.
2. A guard carries a briefcase inside, and the vault stands open for a short time. That's your way in.
3. Each large bank also has a cash drawer and a safe, so check all three.

> **Watch out:** Robbing a vault gives you an **instant four-star wanted level**. Leave on a fast motorcycle that can weave through traffic, then swap to a different car so the police lose track of you.

> **Tip:** Once you've robbed **2 payday loans**, Yasmani sells security-van tips for **$10,000**, which saves you hunting for a van.

Robbing 5 vaults and getting away earns **Maximum Withdrawal**; the [100% checklist](/guides/100-percent-checklist/) wants all 8. After the story ends, the vaults are still the fastest money in the game: hit one, escape, bank the cash, repeat.

<h2 id="spending">Spending it</h2>

Spending $100,000 across shops earns **Treat Yo Self**. Garages and homes come through the Vice City Real Estate app from [Daily Driver](/walkthrough/daily-driver/) in Chapter 2.
