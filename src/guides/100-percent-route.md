This is the whole game as one list. Missions are in story order, and around them sit the side jobs, the limited-time missions, the [challenges](/guides/challenges/) and the [collectibles](/collectibles/#by-area) of the county you are in at that point. Open a county to see every mapped card, WAiNK scan, hoop, flamingo, magazine and meditation spot there, with where to find it. After the credits comes the free roam clean-up. Ticks are saved in this browser and shared with the walkthrough, the challenge list, the trophy list, the collectible checklists and the [100% checklist](/guides/100-percent-checklist/). Only want the things that can slip away? They are on one page in [Missables](/guides/missables/).

> **Note:** First pass. Mission order, unlocks, limited-time jobs and the collectible spots come from trusted guides. **Where each challenge sits is our suggestion**, worked out from its requirement and what the game has opened by then. None of it is checked in our own game yet.

> **Watch out:** Don't use cheats on this save. They switch off trophies and saving while active.

<h2 id="habits">Habits from the first minute</h2>

- **Scan every new vehicle** with WAiNK, boats and aircraft too. The checklist wants 200 base models. [How](/guides/activities-and-collectibles/#study-and-scan).
- **Study every new animal** through the binoculars. You need 100, and you never have to hunt them.
- **Do optional missions when they appear.** They count toward the 100 optional tasks.
- **Start limited-time jobs at once.** They carry an hourglass on the map.
- **Save by hand at the start and end of each chapter**, in slots you don't overwrite. There are 15.
- **For anything that says "in one day":** save first and start early in the in-game day.

<h2 id="skip">What else you can skip</h2>

- The two large golf courses. Two of the three mini golf locations is enough.
- Finishing the video games. Starting each one counts; Stick Fiddler wants one arcade game completed.
- Beating par on the third drone race at each location. Finishing it once counts.
- Hunting and skinning, apart from Bret's target decks.
- Most side strands, as far as the checklist goes. Several have their own trophy, though.
