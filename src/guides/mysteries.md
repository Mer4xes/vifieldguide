<h2 id="aliens">The alien secret</h2>

The strangest thing in Leonida isn't on any map. It starts with a car left by the roadside in Kelly County, carries on in a dream and ends deep underground south of Hamlet. There are no markers to follow: you need to know where to go, and it is easy to miss altogether. All of it can be done with either Jason or Lucia, as long as **one character** sees it through from start to finish.

<h3 id="abduction">Part 1: the abduction</h3>

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Wait for Chapter 2.</b> Once the Starlet Motel in Kelly County is your base, the dirt roads and trails <b>north-west of the motel</b>, out towards Port Gellhorn, are where it happens.</li>
<li><b>Go alone, after 2 AM.</b> Leave your partner behind: it won't start with both of you there. Sleep or wait until after 2 AM, then drive the back roads.</li>
<li><b>Find the abandoned car.</b> It's pulled over with its doors open and its lights flashing. It doesn't turn up every night: if it isn't there, drive off, and come back another night.</li>
<li><b>Follow the trail.</b> Clothes are dropped along a path into the woods. Use your flashlight and Focus and keep following them to a bright blue light between the trees.</li>
<li><b>Help the woman.</b> Talk to her and agree to bring her car up for her.</li>
<li><b>Walk back to the car.</b> You never make it: you're taken on the way. The hidden bronze trophy <a href="/trophies/the-truth-is-out-there/">The Truth Is Out There</a> pops, and you wake up by the road three in-game days later, with a worried call from your partner.</li>
</ol>

> **Watch out:** The car you came in can end up impounded while you're gone. It goes to the police station nearest your home. If you're stranded far away, switch to your partner and call to meet up.

<h3 id="dream">Part 2: the dream</h3>

Keep playing as the character who was taken and sleep at a safehouse. The dream doesn't always come on the first night, so carry on and sleep again until it does.

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>The white room.</b> You start in a bright, empty room. Turn around and go through the dark doorway behind you.</li>
<li><b>The egg room.</b> The floor is covered in eggs that burst if you get close. <b>Crouch at once</b>, move as slowly as you can and keep to the left wall. Burst too many and a tentacle grabs you, and you have to start again.</li>
<li><b>The solar system.</b> Look at the model of the planets in the middle of the room. The Earth shows a real place in Leonida: a fenced compound next to a tall radio tower. That's where you're going later.</li>
<li><b>The keypad.</b> Above it are four groups of light bars. Count the bars in each group from left to right: that is <b>your</b> code, and it isn't the same in every game. Write it down.</li>
<li><b>The light panel.</b> Switch the console on. There are four buttons and four lights, and pressing a button changes more than one light. Work at it until all four lights show the same colour; then three doors appear.</li>
<li><b>The three doors.</b> Go through the <b>left</b> door and take the bottle of milk off the table. Take it through the <b>right</b> door and put it in the machine. Don't open the middle door: whatever is behind it kills you. Putting the milk in ends the dream and you wake up.</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="rescue">Part 3: the rescue</h3>

<ol class="cheat-steps">
<li><b>Drive south of Hamlet.</b> The compound from the dream sits out past Hamlet towards Shoeless Keys, with barbed wire on the fences and the radio tower standing over it. Bring a solid car.</li>
<li><b>Ram the front gate.</b> Then break through the building's doors and take the stairs down.</li>
<li><b>Use your code.</b> The keypad door at the bottom takes the code from your own dream. A lift takes you down to the lab; there's a tape on the table to listen to.</li>
<li><b>Same puzzle again.</b> Solve the light panel the way you did in the dream, then milk from the left room, into the machine in the right room. Keep out of the middle room.</li>
<li><b>Get to full health first.</b> The machine opens and frees the alien. As soon as you pick it up, poison gas fills the lab and your health drains.</li>
<li><b>Run for the lift.</b> Don't stop to look around. Once you're outside, maximum health, stamina and focus all go up for good, for both Jason and Lucia.</li>
</ol>
