## Spoilers

The guide is written so you can use it without having the story spoiled.

- A mission page covers that mission only. It does not mention what happens later.
- Story reveals inside a walkthrough sit in a closed block. Tap it to read it.
- Hidden trophies stay blurred until you reveal them.
- On the walkthrough page you can hide the names of missions you have not reached yet.
- **Spoilers hidden / Spoilers shown** in the menu opens or closes every spoiler block on the site at once. Your choice is remembered.

## Accuracy

- **Confirmed** facts come from Rockstar or the official trophy list.
- **Reported** facts come from a trusted guide source, or from two independent sources that agree, checked before they go up. Steps are written in our own words.
- **First pass** means a page has not been checked in our own game yet. Pages marked **Verified** have been, on the version shown.
- Leaks, datamined content and rumoured lists are not used.

## Your progress

Ticked steps, missions, trophies and collectibles are stored in your browser. There is no account and nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data, or switch to another device, your ticks do not follow you.

## Who makes this

The VI Field Guide is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.
